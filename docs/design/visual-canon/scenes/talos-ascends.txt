A great bedchamber in the imperial palace, lit by tall windows and candles.
In a large canopied bed lies ONE very old man, Tiber Septim, at the age of 108: long white hair and a white beard, thin and frail but still commanding, propped on pillows in a white nightshirt with a dark red coverlet.
Kneeling beside the bed, ONE middle-aged man in rich imperial robes, his heir Pelagius, holds the old emperor's hand; the old man is placing a heavy gold signet ring in his palm.
A few court officials and priests stand quietly at the foot of the bed with bowed heads.
Through the tall window behind the bed, a single shaft of pale gold light falls across the old man.
