Daytime, seen from a distance, from a pine-covered hillside above the village of Helgen: a small walled mountain village of timber houses with steep roofs and one tall stone tower, in a pine-covered valley with snowy peaks behind.
Above the village, coming down out of a swirl of grey cloud, ONE dragon, Alduin, wings spread, many times larger than the houses; two hind legs and two wings, no other legs.
Fire on two or three roofs, with smoke rising.
In the village square, tiny figures scatter: Imperial Legion soldiers and Stormcloak prisoners, all too small to make out faces; none is singled out, and there is no figure in the foreground or near the viewer.
No other dragons, no blood, no bodies.
