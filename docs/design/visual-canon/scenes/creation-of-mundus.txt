A mythic scene before the world existed: a boundless void. The left part of the picture is a vast calm field of pale, motionless light; the right part is restless swirling darkness. Where the two meet, in the middle, many spirits have arisen: about FIFTEEN tall, faint figures made of soft light, in long flowing robes, faces indistinct.
In the centre, floating in the void, a great golden astrolabe of nested engraved rings, glowing: the design of the world being drawn up. Inside its innermost ring hangs a small unfinished world, a sphere with only the faint outlines of sea and land.
Beside the astrolabe, ONE figure, Lorkhan, a dark hooded silhouette seen from the side, one arm stretched toward the design as if presenting it to the others; his face is not visible.
Among the spirits on the left, ONE god, Auriel, in long pale robes with a radiant sunburst crown, looking at the design.
At the far right, three or four spirits turn away from the gathering and drift off into the darkness.
No blood, no weapons, no text, no letters, no symbols other than the engraving on the astrolabe rings.
