A harbour on the coast of Hammerfell on a grey, misty day. A stone quay and harbour wall fill the foreground, with closed warehouses and empty streets behind.
On the quay, about SIX Redguard harbour guards in helmets and light desert cloaks, with cloths tied over their mouths and noses, hold spears and long poles and wave the ship away; one holds a torch.
Out on the water, close to the quay, drifts ONE old merchant ship with patched red-brown sails: the Crimson Ship. Its rail is crowded with about TWENTY refugees, silver-skinned human Kothringi with bald or close-cropped heads, in ragged clothes, holding out their hands toward the shore.
No corpses, no blood, no text.
