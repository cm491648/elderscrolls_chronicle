A wide desert coast in daylight: a long beach of pale sand below rocky, sun-bleached headlands, with dunes rising inland under a hot, hazy sky.
In the shallows and drawn up on the sand, about EIGHT tall Yokudan sailing ships with long hulls and patched square sails; more ships wait out at sea.
Dozens of Ra Gada warriors are coming ashore: tall dark-skinned Redguards in riveted dark steel and tooled leather over long robes and sashes, wrapped headcloths or crested helmets, curved swords drawn, round shields on their arms. Some wade through the surf, some are already forming up on the beach.
In the foreground, ONE warrior stands on a low rock at the edge of the beach, curved sword raised, looking inland toward the dunes.
No enemies, no fighting yet, no blood, no text.
