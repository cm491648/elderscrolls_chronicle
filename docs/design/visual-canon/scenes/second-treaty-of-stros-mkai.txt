The southern coast of Hammerfell in 180 of the Fourth Era, a hot clear morning after five years of war: a harbour town of tan sandstone and stucco, its walls broken, roofs fallen in, palm trees burnt to stumps; the sea beyond is calm and blue.
Seen from behind and slightly above, on the left and in the foreground: EXACTLY FIVE Redguard warriors stand on top of a broken town wall, watching; desert clothing, headscarves, scimitars sheathed or lowered, no one raising a weapon. One of them, an older man, rests his hand on the cracked stones. They stand straight and still: they have held their land.
Below them, in the middle distance on the right, the harbour: a column of Thalmor soldiers in gilded elven armour trudges down a stone quay and up the gangplanks of EXACTLY THREE elven sailing ships. They are a beaten army going home: their armour is dulled with dust, dented and missing pieces, helmets carried under the arm or hanging at the belt, heads bowed, shoulders slumped; some limp, some lean on a comrade, two carry a man on a stretcher (no blood shown). Spears are carried sloped and loose, not in step; their banners are furled and dragged.
At the head of the quay stands ONE Thalmor officer in black robes, the robe's hem grey with dust, his back to the town and his head lowered, not looking back.
No fighting, no bodies on the ground, no blood, no fire, no text on anything.
