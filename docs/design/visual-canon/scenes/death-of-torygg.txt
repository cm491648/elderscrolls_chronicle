The aftermath of the duel in the courtyard of Castle Dour in Solitude, 4E 201.

At his feet High King Torygg lies dead on his back on the cobbles, eyes closed, both arms at his sides, dressed exactly as in torygg-sovngarde-user.png: long dark-brown hair, short dark beard, a gold circlet with a red stone, a short rust-red shoulder mantle with a round gold brooch, a dark grey-green knotwork tunic, a broad belt and a long dark open coat with silver studs; his sword lies on the ground beside him. No blood and no wounds.
Kneeling at his side, Elisif the Fair, his young widow, as in SR-npc-Elisif_the_Fair.jpg (long auburn hair, a gold circlet with a red stone, a long dark-red gown under a long golden-brown coat with knotwork trim and a mail piece on one shoulder), bends over his body weeping, one hand on his chest and the other covering her face.
