A last stand on a muddy road through a narrow valley in a far land, in pouring rain at dawn.
A shield wall of about TWENTY Imperial legionaries in grey steel plate and red cloaks stands in a tight square, shields locked.
At the front, ONE emperor, Uriel V, in gilded armour and a red cloak, sword raised, rallies them.
From the misty ridges on both sides, arrows rain down; on the ridgelines stand rows of tall, dark figures with long bows, barely seen through the rain.
Far off at the end of the valley, the masts of ships can be seen on a grey sea.
No blood, no text.
