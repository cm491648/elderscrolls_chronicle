Daylight on a barren grey upland, with Red Mountain smoking faintly in the distance behind. A calm, ceremonial moment of alliance.
In the centre, TWO leaders meet and clasp forearms as sworn friends: on the left the Chimer general Nerevar, on the right the Dwemer king Dumac; both of normal elven height, standing face to face, seen from the side.
Behind Nerevar on the left stand exactly FIVE Chimer soldiers (five, not six), side by side in a single rank with no one behind them, all five in Chitin armour, with pennants. Behind Dumac on the right stand exactly FIVE Dwemer soldiers (five, not four), side by side in a single rank with no one behind them, in Dwarven armour. Every one of the ten soldiers is fully visible, none hidden behind another or behind a shield. The two groups stand apart, watchful, leaving open ground between them while the two leaders meet in the middle.
No fighting, no weapons raised, no other people: twelve figures in all.
