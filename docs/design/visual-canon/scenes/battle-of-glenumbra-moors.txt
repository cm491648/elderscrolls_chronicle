A wide view of open, rolling moorland under a low grey sky, seen from a slight height; heather, bog pools and rocks, no trees, no buildings.
On the right, a vast Alessian army of many small human figures advancing across the moor; it fills most of the right half. They wear practical dark gear in browns and greys: dark leather jerkins with riveted metal plates, mail shirts, layered leather tassets, simple plate on the legs, hoods or plain open helmets, straight swords, round shields and bows. Their officers wear mail and plain plate; a few priests among them in long hooded robes with a chevron pattern down the chest. Their banners are plain cloth in muted colours with no dragon, no animal and no emblem on them.
On the left, a much smaller army of the Direnni: tall golden-skinned Altmer in close-fitting blackened steel armour with thin pale-silver edging, sharp pauldrons swept up into points, full helms with a narrow vertical eye slit, dark round shields; with them about TEN Breton knights on horseback in the same dark, silver-edged armour.
Above the elven lines, the sky is split open by a great burst of pale white-gold light, as if a second dawn: magic is falling from it onto the ground in front of the elves, and the rocks under the Alessian front ranks are cracking and giving way.
Among the elven lines, EXACTLY TWO riders on horses made of flame.
All figures small (no figure larger than a tenth of the picture height). No gold armour, no white robes on the elves. No blood, no bodies, no werewolves, no text.
