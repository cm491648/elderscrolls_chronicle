Daytime under a hazy grey sky, on a low rise of ash-grey ground; in the far background, the great volcano Red Mountain with a thin plume of smoke.
On the rise, facing the viewer from the middle distance, EXACTLY THREE new gods standing side by side and floating a little above the ground:
- in the centre, Sotha Sil, with grey skin, one hand raised as he speaks;
- on the left, Almalexia, glowing with golden skin;
- on the right, Vivec, half his body of one skin colour and half of the other.
Below them, in the foreground but seen from behind, a crowd of about THIRTY newly changed Dunmer, men and women with ash-grey skin, looking up at the three, some holding their hands up to look at their own changed skin.
No weapons drawn, no fighting, no buildings close by, no text.
