The duel of the First and the Last Dragonborn on the Summit of Apocrypha, 4E 201.
A wide stone arena under a sickly yellow-green sky crackling with pale lightning; around it rise arches and pillars built from stacked books and a ring of pointed stone arches, and a pool of black ink lies at the centre. No tentacles anywhere.
On one side stands Miraak, a tall figure in a golden-bronze dragon priest mask with horn-like spikes and long layered dark robes, wrapped in the Dragon Aspect Shout: a translucent armour of blue-white ice-crystal spikes and plates covers his body, a crown of orange-gold flame burns above his head, and golden light wraps his hands.
Facing him, seen from behind, the Last Dragonborn in an iron helmet with two large curved horns, iron plate and fur, the face never visible, a round iron-rimmed shield on the left arm and a drawn iron sword gripped in the right hand, braced to Shout.
High above the arena flies the dragon Sahrotaar, drawn as in the reference image: not a classic winged lizard but a long, sinuous serpentine dragon with a slender snake-like neck and body, slate blue-grey scales, a ridge of many short pale spines running down the neck and back, and a wide, flat, eel-like head with small eyes, no horn crown and a huge mouth of many sharp teeth; leathery wings.
