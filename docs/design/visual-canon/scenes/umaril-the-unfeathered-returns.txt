The last fight in the depths of Garlas Malatar, 433 of the Third Era: the new Divine Crusader faces Umaril the Unfeathered.
A tall underground hall of pale Ayleid stone, white marble-like blocks with slender pointed arches and curved rib-like walls, lit only by a few soft blue-green Welkynd stones set in carved stone mounts; no sky, no trees.
EXACTLY TWO FIGURES. Umaril is the main subject: he stands large in the right half of the picture, seen from the front, drawn exactly as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes him and as the reference images umaril-tes4-4.jpg and umaril-tes4-3.jpg show him: bright gold armour all over, his head a sculpted gold face-helm with a scowling metal face and pointed chin under a small pointed crest, tall thin curved horn-like spikes rising from the helm and both shoulders, a scale-patterned breastplate, clawed gold gauntlets, taloned bird-like feet, a long straight silver sword held out at his side, the other clawed hand raised; no living face, no hair, no flame tendrils, no cloak, no cape, no robe.
On the left and smaller, seen from behind, the Crusader, wearing the Crusader's Relics and looking exactly as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes Pelinal Whitestrake (this is the new Crusader, not Pelinal himself), braced behind the red kite shield with the long sword ready; the helm hides the face.
The two face each other in the moment before the blow; a faint white light gathers on the Crusader's blade.
No other knights, no spirits, no blood, no text.
