Dunmer refugees arrive at Raven Rock on Solstheim, around 4E 16.
The ash-covered south coast of Solstheim under a hazy yellow-grey sky; across the grey sea on the horizon, Red Mountain smokes.
Behind the shore stands Raven Rock: rounded, ash-brown Dunmer houses and domed shell-like buildings, with a massive buttressed stone wall, the Bulwark, running along the shore.
At the wooden docks in the foreground, a crowded boat of ash-grey Dunmer refugees with bundles and children is unloading; two Redoran guards in brown bonemold armour help them ashore, and a grey-haired Dunmer councilwoman in a long robe greets them.
Dead pines stand in drifts of ash. No text.
