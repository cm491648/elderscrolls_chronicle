A dusty road through a half-ruined town in Cyrodiil in daylight: broken stone houses, a toppled cart, a village gate with a cracked wall.
The first hired warriors of the guild at work: about EIGHT armed fighters of different races, humans, a Redguard, an elf, a Khajiit and an Argonian, in practical steel plate, chainmail and leather with no single uniform, carrying swords, axes, maces and shields, have just driven off a band of bandits. They stand in a loose, ready line across the road in front of the gate.
At their head stands ONE Akaviri officer in dark grey lacquered armour of laced plates with bronze edging and green cords, with a long curved sword, giving an order.
In the background, about FIVE ragged bandits flee down the road and over a wall.
Behind the fighters, a few grateful villagers peer out from the gate.
No blood, no bodies, no banners with emblems, no text.
