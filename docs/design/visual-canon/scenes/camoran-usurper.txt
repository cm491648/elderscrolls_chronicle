A land battle on open, rain-soaked moorland outside the walled town of Dwynnen in High Rock, under a black storm with lightning.
On the left, the High Rock alliance advances: Breton knights and foot soldiers in plain steel plate and chainmail, with simple surcoats in a few different plain colours (one for each allied kingdom), long swords, spears and heater shields.
On the right, the Usurper's army breaks under the storm: tall Dremora with dusky skin and curled horns in dark armour, and shambling undead soldiers; lightning strikes among them and the wind drives the rain into their faces.
Behind the Breton line, safe and intact on a low rise, stands the town of Dwynnen: a large manor-like castle of tan stone with steep red tiled roofs and triangular dormers, inside a crenellated grey wall.
Far in the background, out on the grey bay, a fleet of Breton sailing ships closes on a few dark ships of the Usurper that storm waves are wrecking; the ships stay out at sea and nobody attacks the town.
Shields, surcoats, sails and banners are plain or carry simple geometric patterns: no lions, no animals, no heraldry from existing games. No text.
