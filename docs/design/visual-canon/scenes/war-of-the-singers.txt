The foot of a great rocky mountain (Mount Hattu) in dry highland country, late afternoon light, dust in the air.
On the left, a small army of sword-singers, about THIRTY figures, in a curved, disciplined line; each holds a curved sword, and several of the blades shine with a pale spirit-light.
At their head, ONE older man, Frandar Hunding, calm, sword held low.
On the right, filling most of the picture and stretching into the distance, the emperor's huge army, hundreds of small figures under many banners, its front ranks breaking against the singers' line.
No figure larger than a fifth of the picture height. No blood, no bodies, no text.
