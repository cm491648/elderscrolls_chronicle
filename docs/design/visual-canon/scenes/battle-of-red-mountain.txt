Daylight under a hazy grey sky. A wide view across the barren ash plain below Red Mountain. The great volcano dominates the upper half, a thin plume of smoke drifting sideways from its summit; on its slopes stand the Dwemer stronghold towers, and at the foot of the mountain a great doorway cut into the rock shows where the army has marched out of its mountain citadel.
In the middle ground, below a foreground of open, empty ash plain, two armies of small figures (no figure larger than a tenth of the picture height) face each other in a measured, chronicle-like arrangement, each a few ranks deep, just beginning to clash in the centre:
- On the left, the Chimer host: most of its soldiers in Chitin armour, its few captains as Chimer warriors, with pennants.
- On the right, the Dwemer army in Dwarven armour.
No war machines, no siege engines, no giant constructs. The figures stand in ranks with weapons lowered, not lunging. No named heroes in the foreground, no blood, no bodies, no explosions, no lava flows or lava streaks on the mountain, no glowing objects.
