A battle on a wide grey beach under a stormy sky, seen from slightly above. A simple, readable composition with FOUR groups only.
In the centre, the invaders: a tight mass of snow demons of Kamal from Akavir, hulking pale blue-white horned figures in crude dark iron armour, pressed together and turning in every direction, clearly trapped.
On the left, a long line of Dunmer soldiers in ornate bronze and chitin armour with tall shields holds firm against them.
At the back, coming down the dunes behind the invaders, a band of Nord warriors in iron and fur charges with axes and swords.
On the right, a column of Argonian warriors with round shields and spears runs in to close the circle.
Behind the battle, grey sea. No figures in the sky, no giant waves, no gods, no blood, no text.
