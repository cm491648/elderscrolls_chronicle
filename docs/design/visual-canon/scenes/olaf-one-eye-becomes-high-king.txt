The snowy summit of Mount Anthor in northern Skyrim, by day: a wide, rocky mountaintop under a pale grey sky, with lower snowy peaks and clouds far below.
In the centre and left of the picture, the dragon Numinex lies beaten down on the snow-covered rocks: one dragon only, old and scarred, its body pressed flat to the ground, one wing folded crookedly against the rocks, its long neck low and its head turned toward Olaf, jaws half open, breathing no fire. It is defeated, not dead.
In the right foreground stands Olaf One-Eye, facing left toward the dragon's head in three-quarter view, so that the left side of his face, is turned to the viewer: his left eye is plainly blind and shut, the lid closed flat with no white of the eye showing, a thick pale scar running down through it, while his right eye, on the far side of his face, stays open. His sword is held low in his right hand, and he is shouting at the dragon: a translucent ripple of distorted air spreads from his mouth toward the dragon's head.
Around the dragon stand exactly FOUR of his warriors (four, not three or five), each fully visible and none hidden behind the dragon: one near its neck holding a thick coil of rope, one near its folded wing holding a heavy iron chain, and two on the far side of its body, one with rope and one with chain, all braced and ready to bind it.
No crown and no coronation; no other people; no blood.
