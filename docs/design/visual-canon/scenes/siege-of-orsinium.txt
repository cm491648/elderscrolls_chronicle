The Siege of Orsinium, in its first years (about 950 of the First Era): a high mountain valley in western Wrothgar under a cold, overcast sky, snow on the far peaks, dark pine forest on the lower slopes. Daylight.
Seen from a slight height behind the besiegers' lines, looking up the valley.
In the upper middle of the picture, the first Orsinium sits on a rocky mountainside, as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes Old Orsinium: a grim fortress-city of huge rough grey stone blocks, thick walls and squat square towers stepping up the slope. It has NO domes, no scaffolding and no cranes; it is a finished old city, not one being rebuilt. Orc defenders crowd the battlements with bows and spears.
On the wall above the main gate stands ONE Orc chieftain, raising a great curved horn to his lips with both hands and blowing it.
Across the valley floor in the foreground and middle ground spreads the allied siege camp: rows of tents, wooden palisades, ladders and a few wooden siege towers. Two kinds of besiegers, each in its own group: on the left, Breton soldiers and mounted knights of Daggerfall in dark steel plate; on the right, Redguard warriors of the Order of Diagna in layered leather and steel over long robes, curved swords drawn.
From the pine forest at the right edge of the valley, EXACTLY SIX brown bears come charging down the slope into the flank of the besiegers, answering the horn.
ANATOMY AND GRIP: every figure has exactly two arms and two hands. A soldier with a shield carries it on one arm and holds the weapon in the OTHER hand, never the shield and the weapon in the same hand; an archer holds the bow in one hand and draws with the other; a spearman holds the spear in both hands or a spear and a shield in separate hands.
All figures small (no figure larger than a tenth of the picture height). No dragons, no magic beams, no bodies, no blood, no text.
