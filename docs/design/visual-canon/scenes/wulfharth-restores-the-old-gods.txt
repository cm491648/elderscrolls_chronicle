A Nord settlement in the snow of Skyrim, by day under a grey sky, snow on the ground and a few timber Nord houses with steep roofs at the edges of the picture.
In the middle distance stands a chapel of the Alessian Order, foreign to Skyrim and unlike the Nord houses: a tall rectangular hall of pale dressed stone with a steep timber roof, an arched doorway and narrow arched windows, dark red cloth hangings beside the door, no emblems. The chapel is on fire: flames rise from its roof and windows, and a column of grey smoke goes up into the sky. The building is empty: no one inside it, no one burning, no bodies, no executions.
In the foreground, a little left of centre, High King Wulfharth stands in the snow in three-quarter view, facing the burning chapel, his right arm raised in command, his crown on his head.
Behind him and to his right stand exactly THREE Nord warriors (three, not two or four), each fully visible, each holding one burning torch.
No priests, no fighting, no other people.
