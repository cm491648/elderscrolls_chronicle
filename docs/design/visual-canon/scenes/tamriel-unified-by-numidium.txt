A great elven city on a coast of the Summerset Isles: graceful towers of white stone and gold, curved bridges and flowering gardens by a blue sea.
Striding out of the sea toward the city is ONE colossal brass construct, Numidium, many times taller than the highest tower, its pale tarnished brass body catching the light, steam and sparks around its joints.
On the walls and along the shore, small figures of High Elf soldiers in Skyrim-style elven armour (burnished golden-bronze, leaf-scrolled shoulder plates, tall crested helmets, fan-shaped feather-plate shields) fall back in disarray; a few elven ships are fleeing along the coast.
No figures larger than the towers except the giant, no text.
