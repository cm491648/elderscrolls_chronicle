The Thalmor take power in the palace of Alinor, capital of the Summerset Isles.
A grand white-and-gold elven palace hall with tall slender columns, high arched windows and sea light.
At the top of the steps stand THREE Thalmor Justiciars, golden-skinned High Elves in long fitted black robes with thin gold trim and high stiff collars, one holding a document.
Below them, a few Altmer royal guards in gilded elven armour lay their spears and shields on the marble floor, and two Altmer courtiers in pale robes stand aside with lowered heads.
An empty throne stands behind the Justiciars. No banners with symbols, no text.
