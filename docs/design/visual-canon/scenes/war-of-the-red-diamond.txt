A snowy hillside above a walled city of grey stone built on a colossal natural rock arch over a sea inlet: Solitude, in winter.
On the hillside stands ONE woman, Potema, the Wolf Queen: an older, fierce-faced noblewoman with grey-streaked dark hair, in a black gown and dark armour plates, a great grey wolf pelt over her shoulders, raising both hands.
Before her, from the snow, about TWELVE skeletal undead warriors in old rusted armour are rising and forming ranks; a faint sickly green light hangs around them.
In the distance, on the plain beyond the city, the banners and tents of a besieging army are visible.
