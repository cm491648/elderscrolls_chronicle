A mythic scene at the dawn of time. A dark sea under a stormy sky; no land except where the scene is happening.
In the middle ground, a burning arrow plunges steeply into the sea; bound with cords to the shaft, just behind the normal pointed head, is a large red heart glowing from within (the heart is not the arrowhead).
At the point of impact, the sea bursts upward in steam and spray, and a young volcanic cone of black rock is breaking up out of the water, lit red from inside, with a thick column of smoke and ash rising.
No figures, no gods, no people, no ships, no blood, no text.
