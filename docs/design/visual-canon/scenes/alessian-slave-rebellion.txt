Daylight, a still, tense day in 243 of the First Era. Seen from the green hills on the lakeshore, looking down across the lake, as in a wide panorama: the Imperial City on its island in the centre, the White-Gold Tower rising far above it, a long arched causeway-bridge running from the right foreground out to the island, open water on both sides.
The city's gates are shut and no defenders are visible.
In the foreground on the near shore, the besieging rebel army waits in orderly ranks behind wooden palisades and a few tents, with pennants; they hesitate and do not advance. Most of the army are Nedes, the rebel slaves of Cyrodiil; on one flank, set apart as a smaller band under its own banners, stand their Nord allies.
From the army, ONE lone knight, Pelinal Whitestrake, walks forward alone along the causeway toward the shut gate, sword in hand, seen from behind at three-quarters, with a clear gap between him and the army behind him.
No fighting yet, no bodies, no blood, no magic.
