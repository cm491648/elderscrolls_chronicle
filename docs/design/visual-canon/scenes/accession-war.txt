Argonian invaders storm Mournhold, the capital of Morrowind, around 4E 6.
The city as in Morrowind: Tribunal: buildings of grey-white stone with pale walls, green-tinted tiled roofs, pointed arched windows and low walls along canals; beyond them rises the great temple, an ochre, tent-like building of curving sweeping roofs with tall slender spires. Smoke rises from a few roofs and the sky is hazy with ash.
In the foreground a band of tall, powerfully built Argonian warriors (about SIX) charges across a paved plaza, their scaled heads crested with horns and quills. Each warrior carries ONE weapon in ONE hand (a spear or a club) and a round wooden shield strapped to the OTHER forearm; no hand ever holds both the weapon and the shield.
Facing them at a stone bridge, a line of Redoran Guards (about FOUR): ash-grey Dunmer in full mustard-yellow bonemold armour with rounded, ribbed plates, a crested bonemold helmet covering the face, a pale cloth tabard, a spear in one hand and a round bonemold shield on the other arm.
No flags with symbols, no text.
