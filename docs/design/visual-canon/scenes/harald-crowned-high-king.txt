Inside a long Nord great hall, seen from the middle of the hall looking toward its far end, with a sunken fire pit burning low in the middle of the floor.
At the far end, on a plain high-backed wooden throne, sits ONE newly crowned High King, Harald, wearing his crown.
Nearest the throne stand exactly THREE Nord chieftains.
Along both sides of the hall, the warriors of his new army stand in two lines facing each other across the fire pit: exactly FIVE on the left and exactly FIVE on the right (ten in all; five, not four or six, on each side), each warrior fully visible and none hidden behind another, all equipped alike in Iron armour as the first uniform army of the Nords, each raising one spear in salute.
No one else holds a crown; no coronation by a priest. Dignified and ceremonial; no fighting. No other people.
