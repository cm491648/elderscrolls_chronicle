Daylight under a pale winter sky. A rocky, snowy northern coast at the tip of Skyrim, with low dark cliffs and a pebble beach, seen from a little above the beach looking along the shore.
EXACTLY FOUR longships of settlers: TWO have just been pulled up onto the pebbles in the foreground, and TWO more are still coming in through the shallows behind them, oars out.
About ten settlers (men, women and children) wade ashore and unload bundles, barrels, a goat and tools; one man stands on the beach helping a child down from a ship's side.
These are settler families, not raiders: no weapons raised, no war paint, no fighting. The land beyond the beach is empty and quiet: no buildings, no towers, no villages, no other people.
The mood is arrival and hope after a hard crossing.
