The Imperial City under a stormy, swirling sky, seen from outside its walls across the lake.
Above the city, a huge dark vortex-rift has opened in the clouds; from it, enormous chains of black iron hang down, and at their ends great jagged hook-shaped anchors bite into the ground around the tall, slender White-Gold Tower, as if trying to drag it upward.
Down the chains climb small dark figures of Daedra, descending toward the city.
In the foreground, on a hill by the lake shore, a small band of about SIX defenders in mismatched armour watch, weapons ready. Each defender carries at most ONE shield, held on the arm; nobody carries a second shield or a shield on the back.
