Echo Cave, 433 of the Third Era: the battle between Mannimarco and the new Arch-Mage of the Mages Guild.
A deep natural cavern of rough grey rock, a few candles on a crude stone altar at the back; dust and loose stones thrown up by the fight.
EXACTLY TWO FIGURES, IN THE MIDDLE OF A FIGHT. On the right, Mannimarco, as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes him, lunges forward and hurls a crackling bolt of cold violet magic from one outstretched hand, his staff swung back in the other, his face twisted with fury.
On the left, the new Arch-Mage, a figure in a long, plain hooded robe of deep blue, hood up and face in shadow, braces with one foot forward and thrusts a large faceted black soul gem toward Mannimarco; the violet bolt shatters against a dark shield around the gem, while the Arch-Mage's other hand throws a bright blue-white spell back at the necromancer.
Both figures move: robes swing, sparks and shards of magic scatter across the floor between them.
No skeletons, no corpses, no other necromancers, no blood, no text.
