The assault on Saarthal: grey daylight, snow on the ground. The ruins of Saarthal rise on a snowy coastal hillside, fortified by the Snow Elves with barricades across the broken arches and gateways.
In the foreground, larger than anyone else and clearly the focus, ONE warrior, Ysgramor, charges up the slope toward the barricade at the head of the attack, swinging Wuuthrad in both hands.
Behind him, a wave of about a dozen of the Five Hundred Companions follows him up the slope. They are Nords of Tamriel equipped like the ancient Nord warrior of the DESIGN REFERENCE (Hakon One-Eye's armour), not historical Vikings; they are not the three named heroes themselves.
On the barricade and the broken walls above, about eight Snow Elf defenders stand their ground with swords, spears and bows.
The moment just before the clash: no blood, no bodies, no wounds.
