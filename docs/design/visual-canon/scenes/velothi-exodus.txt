A snowy mountain pass, daytime, cold blue light. The pass is blocked by a towering wall of clear blue ice filling the narrow gap between two rock walls; the ice wall is beginning to melt in the middle, water running down its face, and through the thinning middle a warm brown-grey land of ash plains and giant mushrooms can just be glimpsed far below.
In front of the ice wall, on the left, ONE young woman in a simple pale robe stands facing the travellers, one hand raised toward the ice.
On the right, facing her, ONE elder: Veloth, the prophet of the Chimer, bareheaded, kneeling on one knee in the snow, laying a large warhammer down on the ground before him. Like every Chimer here, Veloth has pale skin with a warm golden tint, never grey, green or ashen.
Behind Veloth, a long column of Chimer pilgrims, about TWENTY small figures, wrapped in cloaks and carrying packs, stretches back down the pass, tired and watching; none of them is in the foreground.
A hawk circles high above the pass. No weapons raised, no fighting, no buildings, no text.
