The fight with Vyrthur in his frozen chapel in the Chantry of Auri-El, 4E 201.
A dim, ancient Snow Elf chapel of pale grey-green stone with tall pointed arched windows, deep inside a glacier: huge jagged spikes of blue-white ice have burst through the floor and walls, frost covers every surface, and at the far end stands a pale glowing white throne. Only cold, grey-blue light filters in from above; no sunbeams.
On the throne steps stands Vyrthur, a gaunt old Snow Elf vampire with pale grey skin, long white hair and glowing eyes, in faded white-and-gold priestly robes, raising his hands to call down shards of ice.
Facing him, seen from behind, the Last Dragonborn in an iron helmet with two large curved horns, iron plate and fur, with a round iron-rimmed shield and a sword, the face never visible; beside them, turned three-quarters so her face shows, Serana, a pale young vampire woman with dark-brown hair braided into a crown around her head and falling loose to the shoulders, glowing orange-gold eyes, a black hood-like scarf around her neck and shoulders, dark red-brown and black Volkihar leather armour with laced bracers, and a long dark-red cloak hanging down her back, casting a crackling red spell from her open hand. Nothing is slung on Serana's back except her cloak: no sword, no weapon, no case.
