The Crystal Tower seized by Nocturnal, 582 of the Second Era, at the moment her hold on it is broken.
The main island of Summerset at dusk, seen from a high cliff path across a valley.
In the middle of the picture rises the Crystal Tower, as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes it, far taller than anything around it: a pale fluted stone tower, not a crystal.
Its lower half still stands in Summerset, among the pale towers of the High Elf city at its foot, as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes Summerset architecture.
Its upper half is being dragged into Nocturnal's realm: there the stone turns grey and is split by cracks, a slow vortex of purple-black cloud and shadow coils around it, a great flock of black birds wheels in the vortex, and broken pieces of pale stone walkway and rubble float in the air around the tower.
From the crown of the tower a clean shaft of white-gold light bursts upward and outward and starts to tear the vortex apart; where the light falls, the stone is pale again.
