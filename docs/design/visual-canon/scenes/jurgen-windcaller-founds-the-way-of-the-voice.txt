Inside High Hrothgar, high on the Throat of the World, by day: a vast, quiet hall of massive grey granite blocks, two thick square stone pillars, dark timber beams overhead, a worn stone floor; one iron brazier on a stone base burns low a few paces away; through one tall, narrow opening in the far wall, snow and a cold white sky.
In the centre, Jurgen Windcaller kneels alone on the bare stone floor in meditation, seen in three-quarter view from the front: his knees on the floor, sitting back on his heels, his back straight, his empty hands resting open on his thighs, his head slightly bowed, his eyes closed and his mouth closed; his face is calm and still.
He is the only person in the picture. Nothing else happens: no Shout, no ripple of air, no light or magic around him.
