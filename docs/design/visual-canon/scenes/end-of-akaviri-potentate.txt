A funeral procession on a grey winter morning, crossing a wide stone bridge toward the great walled Imperial City, whose tall white tower rises in the background.
At the head of the procession, ONE Akaviri mourner in dark robes plays a long bamboo flute.
Behind, EIGHT bearers in dark grey lacquered Akaviri armour with bronze edging carry a long covered bier draped in dark cloth; the body cannot be seen.
About TWENTY Akaviri mourners in dark robes and veils follow, heads bowed; some weep.
Along the bridge, a scattering of city folk watch in silence.
No blood, no text.
