A stormy sea at the end of a battle, seen from a slight height. In the centre, a coral island of twisted pale-pink and grey coral spires is cracking apart and sinking; on its highest point stands a tall coral tower whose top pulses with a cold blue light that bleeds up into the clouds.
Around the island the sea turns in a huge whirlpool; a few ships at its edge are tilting and being drawn in.
In the foreground and on both sides, a mixed fleet of about TWELVE sailing ships of different makes and colours: slender curved elven ships, heavy square-sailed human warships and smaller boats, all flying different plain flags. They are pulling away from the whirlpool.
On a broken ledge of coral near the tower, TWO bloated, slug-like Sload, small in the picture, cling to the rock as it falls.
