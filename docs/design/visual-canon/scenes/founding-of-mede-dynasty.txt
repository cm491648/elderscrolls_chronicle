Titus Mede marches on the Imperial City, around 4E 22.
Seen from the mainland shore behind and beside the marching column: a long pale cream stone bridge of many arches runs across the lake, with pairs of round crenellated gate towers along it, towards the island; on the island, beyond grassy slopes, rise the white ring walls of the Imperial City and at its centre the tall, slender White-Gold Tower. A dark storm swirls over the city, its eye directly above the tower.
On the near end of the bridge, heading AWAY from the viewer towards the city, rides a lean Colovian warlord with curly auburn hair, in a dark steel cuirass and a travel-worn red cloak, at the head of a column of about TWENTY rough soldiers in mismatched steel and leather, spears raised.
No banners with symbols, no text.
