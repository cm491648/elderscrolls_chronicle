Night, seen from the sea. In the background, across the dark water, a snowy rocky coast with low cliffs, and on it the stone city of Saarthal burning: flames and a column of smoke rise into the night sky, reflected faintly on the water. The fire is far away and fills only a small part of the picture; no people are visible in the burning city.
In the foreground-middle, ONE longship on a dark, choppy sea, sailing away from the coast. Aboard are EXACTLY THREE men and nobody else; the rest of the ship is empty:
- the older chieftain Ysgramor standing at the stern, looking back toward the burning city, one hand raised to his face as he weeps; he carries no axe (the axe Wuuthrad has not yet been forged);
- his son Ylgar at the oars;
- his son Yngol, the smith, seated near the bow.
A few stars. The mood is grief and exile, not battle: no weapons drawn, no fighting, no elves.
