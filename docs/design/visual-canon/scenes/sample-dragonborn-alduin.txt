The windswept snowy summit of the Throat of the World in daylight, under a pale stormy sky with torn clouds; wind drives thin streaks of snow across the picture. Seen from a slightly low angle across the summit, with the sea of clouds and distant lower peaks falling away beyond the edge on the right. In the middle distance on the left, among jagged rocks, the Word Wall: a single low, curved arc of weathered stone, about the height of a man and broken off at both ends, with no towers, battlements or other buildings on the summit.
Exactly TWO figures, a tense standoff just before the fight:
- In the right foreground, ONE lone warrior, the Last Dragonborn, seen from behind at three-quarters, feet braced in the snow, shield raised in the left hand and a sword held low in the right; the face is not visible.
- On the left, ONE black dragon, Alduin, landing on the snow in front of the Word Wall, standing on its two hind legs, its front propped on the clawed elbows of its half-folded wings planted in the snow (it has no other legs), long neck lowered toward the warrior, jaws open as if shouting. The dragon is many times the warrior's size.
No other people, no other dragons, no fire or frost breath, no blood.
