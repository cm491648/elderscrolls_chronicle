Dusk in the First Era, seen from a low grassy hill on the lakeshore. Across the lake lies the Imperial City on its island, calm and unharmed, with the White-Gold Tower at its centre.
The sky is clear and darkening. Across it fall EXACTLY EIGHT stars, each a small bright point with a long, thin, straight trail, spaced one after another from left to right as if falling in sequence.
On the hill in the foreground stand exactly SIX people looking up at the sky: FOUR townspeople of Cyrodiil, ONE priest of the Alessian Order holding a staff, and ONE Alessian Order soldier; one of the townspeople points at the falling stars.
A strange stillness: no fire, no destruction, no other people, no gods or dragons in the sky.
