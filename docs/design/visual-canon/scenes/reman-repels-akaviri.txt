Daylight in winter, at Pale Pass in the Jerall Mountains: a narrow snowy pass between steep slopes thick with snow-laden pines and grey rock walls that rise close on both sides; a cobbled road winds up the floor of the pass. Seen from the side, a little above the road, so both armies are visible along it.
On the left, lower down the road, at the head of a column of his Cyrodilic soldiers coming up the pass with plain pennants, stands ONE man, Reman, one hand raised as he speaks.
On the right, higher up the road, the whole Akaviri host kneels in the snow in rows that fade back up the pass; they are armed and armoured in the Akaviri style of the DESIGN REFERENCE (the Blades' armour). The front row nearest Reman is exactly SIX kneeling Akaviri warriors, heads bowed so their faces are hidden under their helmets, their katanas laid on the snow before them.
Between the two armies lies a short open stretch of the road.
Calm and solemn: no fighting, no blood, no other figures.
