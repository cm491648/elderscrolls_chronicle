Daylight, calm weather. A wide pale sand beach on the north shore of the island of Auridon, seen from a slightly raised viewpoint at the edge of the sand, the green island rising gently to the right and the open sea to the left.
Offshore, EXACTLY NINE elven sailing ships ride at anchor, arranged as THREE separate groups of THREE ships (3 + 3 + 3 = 9) at different distances on the sea; not two rows, not ten.
ONE small rowing boat has been pulled up onto the sand. In front of it, the first to step ashore, stands ONE tall elven lord, Torinaan, facing inland toward the green hills on the right, one arm raised toward the land he claims, his back half turned to the ships.
Behind him at the water's edge wait exactly FOUR more elves of his people, standing together, watching him.
No buildings, no animals, no weapons drawn, no other people.
