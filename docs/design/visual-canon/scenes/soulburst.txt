The interior of a great temple in the Imperial City, with high stone vaults and tall windows. EXACTLY THREE people, no one else.
In the centre, ONE man, Emperor Varen Aquilarios, kneels before a stone altar in imperial armour and a red cloak, head bowed in prayer.
Beside the altar, ONE older human man, Abnur Tharn, an Imperial with round human ears (not an elf), in rich chancellor's robes, holds up a golden amulet with a large red gem; it blazes with light.
A few steps behind them, ONE gaunt High Elf necromancer, Mannimarco, raises both hands; glowing glyphs spiral up from the floor around the altar, and the other two are frozen in place.
A great ring of white and violet magic bursts outward from the amulet, cracking the floor and shattering the windows.
No other figures, no blood, no text.
