A great stone cathedral on the shore of a wide lake, in late afternoon. The cathedral is on fire: smoke pours from its tall windows and its central spire is beginning to collapse.
In the foreground, a crowd of about THIRTY Nibenese townsfolk and peasant rebels in plain tunics, hoods and padded jackets, armed with pitchforks, clubs, spears, a few swords and torches, watch the fire and cheer; some carry plain cloth banners with no emblems.
On the steps of the cathedral, a few robed priests of the Alessian Order flee down toward the water.
No bodies, no blood, no text.
