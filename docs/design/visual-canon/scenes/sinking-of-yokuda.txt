At sea, at dusk, seen from the deck level of a fleet sailing away.
In the background, a continent sinking: a long coastline of desert cliffs and pale stone cities tilting and sliding into a churning sea; a great column of steam, dust and smoke rises along the coast; huge waves roll outward.
In the middle ground and foreground, a fleet of about FIFTEEN tall ships with patched sails, crowded with refugees, sailing away to the right; on the nearest ship, about TEN small figures stand at the rail looking back at the sinking land, some holding children.
No sea monsters, no gods, no blood, no text.
