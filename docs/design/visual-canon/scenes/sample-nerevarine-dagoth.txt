The Heart Chamber deep beneath Red Mountain: a vast, dim cavern of dark rock seen from a high side angle, a lava-lit chasm far below, the only light rising from it.
- In the centre, floating in mid-air above the chasm, the enormous Heart of Lorkhan.
- Behind it on the right, filling the upper background, the unfinished colossus Akulakhan stands among construction platforms, linked to the Heart; its head and open chest are clearly visible.
- In the left foreground, a narrow stone walkway with a low rail juts out over the chasm toward the Heart. On it stand exactly TWO figures facing each other, a few paces apart: the Nerevarine on the left, seen at three-quarters from behind, the gauntleted right hand raised holding the short crystal-bladed dagger toward the Heart, the hammer hanging at his belt and his left hand empty (he does not hold two tools at once); and Dagoth Ur on the right, taller, between him and the Heart, gaunt arms spread wide and clawed hands open, his masked face turned toward the Nerevarine.
The tense moment before the final blow. No other people, no creatures, no blood.
