The Nord militia breaks into Markarth, 4E 176.
The gates of the Dwemer city of Markarth, carved into a rocky canyon in pale golden-grey stone with angular reliefs, stand broken open.
At the front, a younger Ulfric Stormcloak (about thirty, dark-blond hair, short beard, steel-grey cuirass and a shaggy fur mantle) leads a charge of Nord militiamen (about TEN) in mismatched steel, iron and fur.
On the walls and in the gateway, Reachmen defenders in hide, fur, bone and feathers, with crude bone axes and bows, fall back.
No banners with symbols, no text.
