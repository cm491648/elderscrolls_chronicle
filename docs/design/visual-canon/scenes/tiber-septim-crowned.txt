The Imperial City in 854 of the Second Era, on the day Tiber Septim is crowned emperor, just after the palace fire. Daylight, a grey sky, a thin trail of smoke still rising from the scorched upper windows of the palace behind.
Seen from the square before the palace, at a slight angle: a short flight of broad steps leads up to the palace at the foot of the White-Gold Tower, which rises out of the top of the picture.
On the top step stands ONE man, Tiber Septim (until now the general Talos), newly crowned, wearing a plain crown; a plain white linen bandage, clearly a bandage and not a collar, is wound around his bare throat where the assassin's blade cut it, his robe open at the neck so the bandage can be seen. He stands upright, one hand resting on the pommel of his sword, facing the square.
SCALE: every figure is drawn at the same ordinary human scale. Tiber Septim is a normal-sized man; only the height of the steps raises him above the crowd. Because he is farther from the viewer than the front rank, he looks smaller than the soldiers nearest to us, never larger; his head is no bigger than theirs.
Below the steps, the square is filled by his army in ordered ranks under banners, with townspeople at the edges; the front rank closest to the viewer is exactly EIGHT soldiers, the rest fading back into the square.
No assassin, no fighting, no fire on the figures, no blood.
