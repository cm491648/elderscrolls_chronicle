A mythic scene at the dawn of time, seen from below. The lower third: a half-made world, a dim grey sea with a few unfinished islands of bare rock, no plants, no buildings, no people.
The upper two thirds: a dark sky. Near the top centre, ONE god, Magnus, small against the sky, rising upward away from the world, seen from behind and below; he is already partly gone into a blazing round hole torn in the sky, and white-gold light pours down through the hole: this hole is becoming the sun.
Behind and around him, about TWENTY much smaller points of light rise after him in loose streams; where each has passed through the sky there is a small bright pinhole: these become the stars.
