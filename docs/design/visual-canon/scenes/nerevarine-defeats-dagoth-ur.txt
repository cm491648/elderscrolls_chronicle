The Heart Chamber deep beneath Red Mountain: a vast, dim cavern of dark rock seen from a high side angle, a lava-lit chasm far below, the only light rising from it.
- In the centre, hanging above the chasm, the enormous Heart of Lorkhan.
- Behind it on the right, filling the upper background, the unfinished colossus Akulakhan stands among construction platforms, linked to the Heart; its head and open chest are clearly visible.
- In the left foreground, a narrow stone walkway with a low rail juts out over the chasm toward the Heart. On it stand exactly TWO figures facing each other, a few paces apart: the Nerevarine on the left, seen at three-quarters from behind, his right hand, in Wraithguard, raised holding Keening toward the Heart, Sunder hanging at his belt and his left hand empty (he does not hold two tools at once); and Dagoth Ur on the right, taller, between him and the Heart, arms spread wide and hands open, his face turned toward the Nerevarine.
The tense moment before the final blow. No other people, no creatures, no blood.
