The founding of Nova Orsinium, around 399 of the Third Era: a steep mountainside between Wayrest and the Wrothgarian Mountains, under a clear, cold afternoon sky; rocky slopes and a few dark pines, the green lowlands and a glimpse of the sea far below on the left.
Up the mountainside, in the upper right half of the picture, rises the new city, as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes it: walls and halls of dark iron with the citadel at the top, a little scaffolding still on one wall.
A broad road climbs toward the city gate, lined on both sides by large dark-iron statues of Orc heroes. The statues must read at once as ORCS: massive, hunched, heavily muscled Orc warriors with tusks jutting from the lower jaw, heavy brows and topknots or braided beards, in thick spiked Orcish plate, in fierce, aggressive poses: one roaring with a huge two-handed war axe raised over its head, one planting a great warhammer and baring its tusks, one gripping a cleaver and a round spiked shield in a battle crouch. They are NOT calm upright knights or saints standing with a sword point-down, and they are not human; no robes, no halos.
In the left foreground, standing alone on the road with clear empty space around him, is ONE Orc chieftain, Gortwog, shown full-length from the front at a three-quarter angle, at a moderate size (about a third of the picture height). His pose is simple and calm: he stands upright with his feet apart, his left arm hanging relaxed at his side, his right arm raised and pointing up the road at his city. He has EXACTLY TWO arms and two hands, both fully visible; his arms are not crossed, not folded and not hidden behind his body, and nobody stands close to him.
Farther up the road, small and well apart from him, about SIX Orc workers haul an iron beam on ropes and carry stones; nobody fights.
Every figure has exactly two arms and two hands. No battle, no blood, no text.
