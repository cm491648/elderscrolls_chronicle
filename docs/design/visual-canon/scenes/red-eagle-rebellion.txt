The Reach in western Skyrim at dusk: Red Eagle's stronghold of piled grey stones and sharpened timber stakes, built into a high rocky crag; behind it jagged grey mountains under a low, deep orange sky.
A close view from slightly below: Red Eagle stands alone in the open gateway, facing the viewer, seen from the knees up and filling the centre of the picture; he has just stepped out to meet the legions, who are not shown.
His bare chest is the focus of the picture: over his heart, an open, raw wound in which a dark, knotted briar heart sits, held in by a few thin wooden pegs laced across the opening; no blood runs from it. This briar heart is the one exception to the rule against wounds: draw it clearly and cleanly as part of his design, not as gore.
His wholly black, glossy eyes look straight ahead; his mouth is closed and his face is hard and calm.
In his right hand he holds his long sword lowered at his side, point down, the blade wrapped in real flickering flames whose light falls warm on his chest and face; his left hand hangs empty.
No one else is in the picture: no soldiers, no bodies, no blood.
