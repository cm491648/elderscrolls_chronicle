The vast audience chamber of the imperial palace at night, lit by braziers: tall stone columns, a long red carpet, and at the far end a raised throne on a stepped dais under hanging banners with no emblems.
The moment of the attack. ONE Dunmer assassin of the Morag Tong (ash-grey skin, red eyes), lightly and quietly equipped for a fast close kill: no plate, no heavy armour. The assassin came in disguised as a palace servant and has just shed the plain servant's tunic, which lies on the steps; underneath is fitted dark brown leather with buckled straps and laced panels, leggings and soft boots, a face wrap. He darts up the dais steps with a short dagger in each hand.
On the throne, ONE emperor, Reman III, a grey-haired man in rich red and gold robes and a simple crown, starts up from his seat in shock, one arm raised, mouth open.
TWO guards at the foot of the dais turn toward the assassin and reach for their weapons, too late. A few courtiers at the sides recoil in alarm; one points and shouts.
No blood, no wound, the blade has not struck, no text.
