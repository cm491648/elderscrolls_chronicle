A high bird's-eye view over the green heartland of Cyrodiil, wide and map-like. In the middle, the Imperial City on its island in the lake, the White-Gold Tower at its centre.
Above the tower hangs the largest Dark Anchor, as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes it: the sky over the city is torn open into a dark swirling rift in a cold, sunless blue-violet sky, and from it huge black chains hang down around the tower to the ground, with jagged black anchor-claws at their ends. It is not a flat disc, ring or glowing orb.
Three marching armies approach the lake from three directions, each a long column of small figures with its banners:
- from the upper left (north-west), the Daggerfall Covenant under its own banners;
- from the upper right (north-east), the Ebonheart Pact under its own banners;
- from the bottom (south), the Aldmeri Dominion under its own banners.
Each column carries only its own alliance's banners, so the three can be told apart.
Between them: forests, rolling hills, roads and a few small forts. EXACTLY THREE columns. No battle yet, no bodies, no giant figures.
