The Red Year, 4E 5: Baar Dau falls on Vivec City and Red Mountain erupts.
Seen from far out over the sea to the south: a colossal dark rock plunges onto the great square stone cantons of Vivec City, which break apart in a burst of dust, steam and spray.
Behind the city, across the island, Red Mountain erupts: a dark volcanic cone throwing a towering column of ash and fire high into the sky, the ash spreading into vast rolling clouds that fill the upper half of the picture and rain grey ash over the sea and the city.
No people, no boats in the foreground; only the sea, the falling rock, the breaking city and the eruption. No text.
