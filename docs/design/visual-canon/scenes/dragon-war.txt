The summit of the Throat of the World, a high snowy mountain top above the clouds, under a dark stormy sky; wind blowing snow.
In the upper half of the picture, ONE black dragon, Alduin, wings spread, being torn away into a swirling rift in the sky: the rift is a ragged tear of pale light and distortion, and the rear half of the dragon is already dissolving into it.
On the snowy summit below, EXACTLY THREE ancient Nord heroes, seen from behind and to the side, small compared with the dragon:
- one, in the centre, holds an Elder Scroll unrolled toward the sky, light streaming from it toward the rift;
- one, a woman, stands with a sword raised;
- one stands with an axe, braced against the wind.
No other dragons, no other people, no blood, no bodies, no text.
