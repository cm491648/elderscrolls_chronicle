A mountain fortress city on high cliffs in deep winter, at dusk, snow falling.
The citadel of Sancre Tor stands on the cliff top; below it, in the snowy lowlands, a small, ragged army is fighting a larger force of Nord and Breton soldiers that has come down from the citadel.
In the foreground, behind and above the citadel, a narrow hidden trail winds down a sheer snowy cliff face; along it, a line of about FIFTEEN soldiers in worn, mismatched armour and fur-lined cloaks descends in silence, led by ONE lean warrior with a short beard, General Talos, in an ornate green-and-gold cuirass and a dark cloak, sword in hand.
Below the trail, at the foot of the heights, a calm, frozen-looking mountain lake reflects the sky; the first soldiers are stepping onto it and vanishing into it as if through a doorway.
