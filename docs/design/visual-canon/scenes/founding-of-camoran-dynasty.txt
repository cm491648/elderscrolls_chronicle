A clearing in an ancient Valenwood rainforest of colossal graht-oaks, daytime, green-gold light falling in shafts through the canopy; in the misty background one gigantic living graht-oak rises like a tower, small glowing lantern windows dotted up its bark and its great roots forming archways.
In the centre of the clearing, on a low mossy rise between the roots of one enormous tree, ONE Bosmer man, Eplear, stands as a newly made king; a simple crown woven of living green branches rests on his head; he wears a finely tooled leather cuirass and a short green cloak.
Around the clearing, about TWENTY-FIVE Bosmer of many clans stand in a wide circle facing him, some raising their hands or their bows in acclaim. They look like the Wood Elves of Skyrim: short and slight, tan to light-brown skin, sharp angular faces with high cheekbones, sunken dark eyes and pointed ears; many with bold red or black face paint across the eyes, some with mohawks, braids or wild curls. They wear Skyrim-style hunters' leather: stitched brown leather cuirasses with straps and buckles, leather bracers and boots, a little hide at the shoulders at most. Nearly all carry longbows and quivers. No helmets, no skulls, no horns, no fur mantles.
At the edge of the clearing, half hidden among the roots, ONE tall spriggan, a tree spirit of living wood and leaves, watches.
No stone or timber buildings, no fighting, no blood, no text.
