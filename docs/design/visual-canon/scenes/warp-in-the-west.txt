The Warp in the West, 417 of the Third Era: Numidium, the brass god, has been woken over the Iliac Bay. Bright, cold daylight under a clear blue sky with scattered clouds; serene but uncanny, as if an enormous god-machine is only half present in this world.
Seen from very low, from a small rowing boat out on the calm water of the bay, looking up; the horizon sits low, about a quarter of the way up the picture, so the sky fills most of the frame.
On the RIGHT third of the picture, a walled High Rock city on a sea cliff, seen from below and from the side: grey stone walls and towers stepping up the cliff above the water, with small banners; the near edge of the city is cut off by the right edge of the picture.
In the LEFT-CENTRE of the picture, ONE colossal figure, Numidium, stands upright and completely still in the bay, the water up to its shins, seen nearly face-on from below; it towers several times higher than the tallest tower of the city. It is drawn pale and partly translucent, the sky and clouds faintly visible through its body, as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes; its reflection lies faint and broken on the water. It does not move, attack or touch anything.
In the near foreground, only the bow of the rowing boat and a little rippled water; no people need to be visible.
No fog bank, no ghostly duplicate copies of the giant, no fighting, no fire, no other giants, no bodies, no blood, no text.
