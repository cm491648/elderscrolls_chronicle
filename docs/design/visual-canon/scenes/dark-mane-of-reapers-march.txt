The Dark Mane at the temple of Moonmont in Reaper's March, 582 of the Second Era, at the moment the Lunar Champions confront him.
Night under two moons, one large and pale, one smaller and reddish; the temple stands on a wooded hill where the trees of Valenwood meet the open plains.
The courtyard of a Khajiit moon temple of richly carved brown stone: a raised altar platform, crescent-moon reliefs, tall braziers whose flames have turned a sickly violet; dark stains of corruption creep over the stones.
On the altar platform stands the Dark Mane, as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes the Dro-m'Athra: the tallest of them, a shadowy, half-translucent violet-black cat-warrior with a tall crescent-moon crown, spiked dark armour veined with glowing blue, a curved dagger in one hand.
Around him stand about SIX smaller Dro-m'Athra in dark-and-gold armour with crescent-moon masks.
Below the platform, EXACTLY TWO Khajiit warrior women, twin sisters, as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes the Khajiit, in matching light armour, stand side by side facing him: one raises a curved sword in one hand, the other holds a round shield on one arm and her sword in the other hand.
ANATOMY AND GRIP: every figure has exactly two arms and two hands; shield and weapon are never in the same hand. No player character, no blood, no text.
