The mythic age after the world was broken, daylight. Seen from the side, so that both lands are visible.
On the right, behind a high pale wall with a great closed gate, lies the hidden land of Old Ehlnofey: a serene, untouched elven city in a green, flowering land.
On the left, a broken, cracked wasteland of shattered rocks, dead trees and grey dust.
Between them, on the wasteland before the closed gate, a band of exactly TWELVE weary Wanderers, worn and ragged after their long search, look up at the wall. On top of the wall stand exactly FOUR tall elven defenders of Old Ehlnofey looking down at them.
Distrust, the moment before a war: no fighting yet, no blood.
