Reachmen rising in Markarth, 4E 174.
The Dwemer city of Markarth, carved into a rocky canyon of pale golden-grey stone with angular reliefs, waterfalls and bronze-domed towers.
In the foreground, Reachmen rebels in hide, fur, bone and feathers, some with antler and skull headdresses, crude bone axes and bows, pour up a broad stone stair; at their head walks an older Reachman leader with long grey hair and beard, in plain fur and ragged cloth, pointing ahead.
At the top of the stair, a few Nord guards in steel and fur fall back, and a Nord jarl in a fur-trimmed robe is led away.
