The Hall of the Elements at the College of Winterhold, 201 of the Fourth Era: Ancano has seized the Eye of Magnus and shut himself in with it.
A tall round hall of grey stone with heavy pillars around its edge, tall lattice windows and a gallery above; cold cyan light fills the middle of the hall.
In the centre of the hall the Eye of Magnus, as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes it, hovers above a round stone dais, its bands opening and turning, rows of glowing green glyphs bright along every band.

Ancano, a Thalmor wizard as the DESIGN REFERENCE describes the Thalmor, stands before the dais FACING THE EYE, seen in three-quarter view from behind, both arms stretched up toward it; bright streams of cyan energy flow out of the Eye into his raised hands as he draws on its power.
Around Ancano and the Eye a shimmering, translucent dome of light closes them in.
Outside the dome, in the lower left foreground and seen from behind, a single College mage in a plain hooded robe shields their face with one raised arm against the glare.
No creatures, no other mages, no bodies; the glyphs on the Eye are the only marks, and there is no other text anywhere.
