Primordial dawn of time, seen from a distance. A rocky island rising from a misty grey sea under a pale, unsettled sky with long streaks of light. No trees, no walls, no other buildings yet: only the bare rock of the island.
In the centre, rising from the island's highest point, ONE immensely tall, slender metal tower (the Adamantine Tower), complete and intact, its tip lost in the cloud.
Around the upper part of the tower, a ring of EXACTLY EIGHT faint, tall, glowing shapes of light stand in the air, each only a soft column of light with no face, limbs or features: the gods in council.
No mortals, no ships, no fire, no weapons, no text, no symbols.
