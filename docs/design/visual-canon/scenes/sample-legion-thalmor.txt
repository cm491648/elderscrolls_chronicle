Fourth Era 175, the battle for the Imperial City: an overcast day, seen from the green lakeshore at a medium-high angle. In the background across the grey water of Lake Rumare, the round walled Imperial City on its island, with White-Gold Tower rising from its centre; the long arched stone bridge runs from the city gate toward the shore.
In the foreground, at the shore end of the bridge, two battle lines meet. Exactly ELEVEN foreground figures:
- On the left, SIX Imperial legionaries advancing side by side in a single rank (no second rank behind them), behind their shields, swords drawn, one of them carrying a red banner with the black Imperial dragon; all six helmeted heads are clearly visible, none hidden behind another man or a shield.
- On the right, FOUR Thalmor soldiers (four, not five) side by side in a single rank holding the bridgehead with swords and shields, and behind them ONE Thalmor Justiciar, standing upright, one hand raised as he gives an order.
Open ground separates these eleven from everyone else: only far away, on the bridge and the far shore, do both armies continue as tiny massed ranks with banners and spears, too small to count. Clash of shields at the centre, no blood, no bodies, no fire, no glowing magic.
