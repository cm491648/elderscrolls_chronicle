A strange land under a warm amber sky: golden autumn hills dotted with giant mushrooms in teal and orange.
Across the land, tall crystalline grey obelisks have burst up out of the ground; from them march rows of grey knights in angular crystal-like armour, the Knights of Order.
At the centre, towering over his knights, ONE immense knight in angular, crystalline grey-silver plate like cut stone, with a long rigid faceplate: Jyggalag, Prince of Order.
In the foreground, ONE mortal hero stands on a rise holding a strange twisted staff crowned with a carved head, facing him.
