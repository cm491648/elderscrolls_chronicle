The throne room of the imperial palace, dark and lit by green magical light.
On the dais before the throne stands ONE sorcerer, Jagar Tharn, in a deep black hooded cloak edged with a sickly green glow, grey hair under the hood; above his raised hand floats a glowing red jewel, and his face is half-way between his own and the face of an emperor, as if a mask of illusion is slipping.
Facing him at the foot of the dais, ONE ragged hero, an escaped prisoner in mismatched armour, braces with both hands on the Staff of Chaos: a long, straight staff taller than the hero, deep indigo-blue shaft bound with gold rings, the eight joins where it was pieced together glowing like cracks of light, and at its head a gold crown-like claw cradling a large pale-blue crystal orb.
The orb erupts with violent, roiling chaotic magic, swirling colours and forked lightning that tear across the hall toward the red jewel; the hero's cloak and hair are blown back by its force. The staff is the brightest and most powerful thing in the scene.
At the sides of the hall lie a few fallen guards whose shapes are melting into flame-like daemons.
