STYLE B — GAME CONCEPT-ART STUDY: one finished scene drawn like a study page from the official art book of a fantasy role-playing game. Confident graphite pencil linework with some visible construction lines and loose parallel hatching for shade; light, transparent watercolour washes laid over parts of the drawing only. Much of the paper stays as bare pencil or untouched white: colour concentrates on the main figures and fades out toward the edges, and the drawing itself dissolves into the paper instead of ending in a cut edge. Washes are muted and cool, but every subject keeps its true design colours from the DESIGN REFERENCE (black scales stay black, red cloth stays red, gold trim stays gold), only lighter and less saturated; wood, leather and metal keep their natural tones.
Keep the picture light overall: no large solid dark masses; night, storms and interiors are suggested with hatching and a few cool washes, not painted dark. Fire and smoke are lightly washed, never solid masses of colour. Light is soft and even, as in a pencil study: no rim light, no bloom, no lens flare, no decorative glow effects; a glow the DESIGN REFERENCE describes stays a small, soft wash.
Still one single scene: no annotations, callouts, arrows, colour swatches, side sketches, turnaround views or handwriting.
