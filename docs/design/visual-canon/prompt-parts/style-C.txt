STYLE C — REALISTIC PAINTING: a realistic, painterly fantasy painting with the finish of the painted loading-screen scenes of a fantasy role-playing game: a depicted moment, not a poster (no title space, no heroic poster pose). Solid, opaque, oil-like brushwork with visible strokes; believable anatomy, materials, weather and atmosphere; deep space with aerial perspective. Dramatic but restrained light: one clear directional light source, soft shadows and atmospheric haze. The palette stays cool and subdued (slate greys, cold blues, snow whites, muted earth tones); the subjects keep their true design colours from the DESIGN REFERENCE, slightly desaturated, with at most one warm accent where the scene calls for it (fire, lava, a banner).
The painting fills the whole image edge to edge (no paper margin, no blank ground) and may have dark areas. It must still not look like a photograph, a 3D render or a glossy airbrushed digital poster: no lens flare, no bloom, no neon glow.
