## 必要特徵
- 巨大的黑鐵錨爪：鋸齒狀、彎鉤形，像巨爪一樣咬進地面
- 從天空翻騰的黑色漩渦裂口垂下的巨型黑鐵鎖鏈（不是平面發光的圓盤或光球）
- 錨落在地面刻滿幾何溝紋的圓形石台上，石台邊緣立著一圈深色立石，中央有小型階梯祭壇
- 風暴般的天色、紫白色閃電，啟動時溝紋發出青藍光（不畫成可讀文字）

## 常見錯誤
- Do not draw a plain nautical ship anchor; the anchor heads are jagged, hooked claws of black iron.
- Do not draw the chains rising from the ground; they descend from a portal in the sky.
- Do not draw an Oblivion Gate; Dark Anchors are chains and anchors, not stone gates.
- Do not draw the Dark Anchor as a flat glowing disc, ring or orb; it is a dark rift with hanging chains and iron anchor-claws.
