## 必要特徵
- 整座城建在橫跨海灣的巨大天然岩石拱上
- 灰石城牆、雉堞、圓塔與一座石造風車塔
- 拱的盡頭是藍宮：深藍色石板金字塔屋頂、小塔與藍色中央圓頂
- 周圍是積雪的松林山丘

## 常見錯誤
- Do not draw Solitude on flat ground or a hilltop; it sits on a giant natural stone arch over water.
- Do not draw the Blue Palace in blue paint; it is grey stone with dark blue slate roofs and dome.
- Do not draw a Nordic wooden town; Solitude is Imperial-influenced stone architecture.
