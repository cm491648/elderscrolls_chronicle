## 必要特徵
- 淡乳白色石材的高瘦塔樓與宮殿，飾有金色線條
- 深色魚鱗瓦的細長圓錐尖頂，層層向上
- 哥德式尖拱窗與拱廊、雕花欄杆、金色旗幟
- 建在臨海的綠色崖地上，周圍有粉紅與洋紅色的花樹

## 常見錯誤
- Do not draw Summerset as a Greek or Roman marble city of flat roofs and temples.
- Do not draw grey, fortified Nordic or Breton castles; Altmer towers are pale, slender and ornate.
- Do not make the buildings out of glowing glass or crystal (only the Crystal Tower is crystal).
