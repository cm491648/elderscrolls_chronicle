In 566 of the {{era-second}}, King {{emeric}} of {{wayrest}}, at war with a rival {{bretons|Breton}} king, promised the {{orcs}} of {{wrothgar}} that {{orsinium}} would be theirs again if they helped him. The clan chief {{kurog}} answered, and his warriors helped crush the enemy army. The {{orcs}} then joined the {{daggerfall-covenant}}, with {{kurog}} as their king.

{{kurog}} wanted the clans to leave their isolated strongholds and become a nation equal to any in {{tamriel}}. He urged them to worship {{trinimac}} instead of {{malacath}}, whose worship, he believed, kept them outcasts. He built his new {{orsinium}} in eastern {{wrothgar}}, near the border of {{skyrim}}. Many chiefs refused to bow, foremost among them his old friend {{bazrag}}, a traditionalist who kept faith with {{malacath}}.

In 582 {{kurog}} called the chiefs to besiege the {{winterborn}}, a {{reachmen|Reachman}} clan, at {{frostbreak-fortress}}, hoping a victory would win them over. His mother {{alga}} invited adventurers from across {{tamriel}} in his name. One of them joined the siege and fought through the fortress beside {{kurog}}, and the last {{winterborn}} warlord was killed. Back in his palace the chiefs still refused him, and when one called him an idiot, {{kurog}} ran him through.

Soon after, attackers claiming to be {{malacath}} fanatics acting on {{bazrag}}'s orders struck the temple of {{trinimac}} in the city. The adventurer drove them off and found they were the {{vosh-rakh}}, zealots of {{trinimac}} in disguise. {{bazrag}}'s stronghold had been attacked too, and he was held in the prison beneath it. {{kurog}} called a great gathering of the chiefs to unite the clans, and asked the adventurer to bring three wavering chiefs to it.

The adventurer freed {{bazrag}}, then slipped into the {{vosh-rakh}}'s hideout and learned that the gathering was a trap to slaughter the chiefs. When questioned, {{kurog}} and {{alga}} tried to kill the adventurer, and failed; {{alga}}, it came out, had led the {{vosh-rakh}} all along. At the gathering {{kurog}} mocked the chiefs and gave the order. The adventurer and {{bazrag}} saved some of them, and {{kurog}} and {{alga}} were slain in the fight. {{bazrag}} gave his old friend a traditional {{orcs|Orc}} requiem. The chiefs asked him to be king, and he agreed with caution, holding that the {{orcs}} should unite only by their own choice. Soon after, the rebuilding of {{orsinium}} was finished.

## Notes

- **Before.** {{orsinium}} had been a province of the {{second-empire}} under {{savirien-chorak}}. After his murder in 2E 430 it lost Imperial protection; the {{bretons}} sacked it the next year and scattered the {{orcs}}.
- **The plan.** {{kurog}} and {{alga}} meant to blame the killings on the high priestess of {{trinimac}}, take credit for stopping the {{vosh-rakh}}, and then disband them.
- **The Covenant.** {{orsinium}} belonged to the {{daggerfall-covenant}} only through {{kurog}}'s pact with {{emeric}}. {{bazrag}} weighed leaving it; what he decided is not recorded.
