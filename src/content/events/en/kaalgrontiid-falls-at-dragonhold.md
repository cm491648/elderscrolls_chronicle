While {{kaalgrontiid}} was gone, his lieutenant {{laatvulon}} gathered followers in his name in {{pelletine}}. Many {{khajiit}} there had suffered plague, the invasion of {{molag-bal}} and now the dragons, and felt their gods had failed them. Rather than fight the dragons, they sought to appease them, and so the {{new-moon-cult}} was formed. It recruited in {{senchal}}, and at its jungle fortress recruits were sacrificed so that their life would flow into the {{aeonstone}}, the crystal that made the dragons stronger.

The {{redguards|Redguard}} swordmaster {{sai-sahan}} set out to rebuild the {{dragonguard}} for its first purpose, hunting dragons. In {{senchal}} the Imperial legion, overwhelmed by refugees, could spare him neither soldiers nor ships, so two {{khajiit}} pirates lent him theirs and became his first recruits. On the island of {{tideholm}} he found the red dragon {{nahfahlaar}}, an old enemy of {{laatvulon}}. Each saw the ruin of {{tamriel}} as a threat to himself, and they made an alliance. The ruined fort on the island became the order's base.

With {{nahfahlaar}}'s help, the {{dragonguard}} hunted down {{laatvulon}} in the keep where the {{dragonguard}} of {{reman}}'s day had once imprisoned him, and killed him there. Then {{abnur-tharn}} brought word that {{laatvulon}} had not been the cult's true master. {{kaalgrontiid}} had found his way back to {{nirn}} and taken command of what was left of the cult.

He went to {{dragonhold}}, a small island off the southern coast. It had been his seat long ago, when his {{khajiit}} followers built a temple there to the {{dark-aeon}}, and sailors had shunned it ever since. Now he poured the power taken from {{jodes-core}}, with the lives his followers gave in sacrifice, into the island's great clusters of {{aeonstone}}. He meant to fulfil an old prophecy of a new moon: to become the {{dark-aeon}}, a third moon in the sky, and the equal of {{akatosh}}. Around the fortress he raised a ward no one could pass. {{abnur-tharn|Tharn}} and an adventurer got through the cult's fortress onto the island and broke it, but the stones grew so charged that the whole island rose into the sky, and people nearby slowly turned into creatures of crystal.

On the floating island {{nahfahlaar}}, {{abnur-tharn|Tharn}}, Queen {{khamira}}, {{sai-sahan}} and the adventurer faced {{kaalgrontiid}} at the height of his power. He was slain, and {{nahfahlaar}} took his soul. The power in the {{aeonstone}} did not fade. {{abnur-tharn|Tharn}} and {{nahfahlaar}} stayed behind to contain the blast, and what was left of the island was destroyed. Only {{nahfahlaar}} came back. {{nahfahlaar}} soon left {{elsweyr}}, and {{sai-sahan}} went on leading the {{dragonguard}}. {{khamira}} travelled to {{senchal}}, seeking to bring {{pelletine}} back under one crown with {{anequina}}.

## Notes

- **Old foes.** Late in the {{era-first}}, the {{dragonguard}} had fought {{laatvulon}} in {{elsweyr}}. {{nahfahlaar}} refused to help them then and later regretted it.
- **The hidden path.** In {{khajiit}} accounts, the spirit of {{khunzar-ri}}, who had sealed the dragons long before, guided the {{dragonguard}} along a path of moonlight to the fortress.
