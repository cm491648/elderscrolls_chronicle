In 572 of the {{era-second}}, the {{argonians}} of {{black-marsh}} helped the {{nords}} and the {{dunmer}} drive off invaders from {{akavir}}, and the three peoples formed the {{ebonheart-pact}}. The Great Houses of {{morrowind}} that joined the {{ebonheart-pact|Pact}} freed their {{argonians|Argonian}} slaves, though {{house-telvanni}} stayed outside it and kept its own. The tribes of {{shadowfen}}, in the northwest of the marsh, joined the alliance. From {{stormhold}} the region was governed by three {{vicecanon|vicecanons}}, one from each people: the {{nords|Nord}} {{hrondar}}, the {{dunmer|Dunmer}} {{servyna}}, and the {{argonians|Argonian}} {{heita-meen}}.

In 582 the {{aldmeri-dominion}} invaded {{shadowfen}}, and trouble spread through the region. At the {{hatching-pools}}, where the eggs of the tribes were tended in the sap of the {{hist}}, a hatching ended in death. {{skin-stealers|Skin-stealers}}, who took the skins of their victims, roamed the swamps, and {{hrondar}} was seen among them. The {{hist}} trees grew troubled. One believed that {{servyna}} had come to the {{hatching-pools|pools}} to destroy the eggs; another, that the {{vicecanon|vicecanons}} were leading the {{skin-stealers}}. The trees began to turn against the outsiders.

An adventurer serving the {{ebonheart-pact|Pact}} tracked the {{skin-stealers}} to their lair beneath {{ten-maur-wolk}}, an old {{daedra|Daedric}} ruin in the northwest of {{shadowfen}}. {{hrondar}} had been taken captive, and a {{skin-stealers|skin-stealer}} had gone about wearing his skin. The trail led on to {{ruuvitar}}, an alchemist of the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}}. He kept one of the {{daedra}} prisoner and used her power to steal skins, and he boiled {{hist}} sap in iron vats to take his victims' memories. From {{hrondar}}'s memories he had learned the ways of the {{hatching-pools}}.

{{ruuvitar}} went to {{loriasel}}, a ruined {{ayleid}} temple, to corrupt the {{mnemic-egg}}; the harm he did to it reached the {{hist}}. {{heita-meen}} sent the adventurer after him, and he died in the fight. When {{heita-meen}} knelt beside the {{mnemic-egg|Egg}}, she fell into a trance. The adventurer touched it and found her inside a dream of the {{hist}}. There, spirits carrying the fears of three troubled trees questioned the adventurer about what had happened at the {{hatching-pools}}, at {{stormhold}} and at {{ten-maur-wolk}}. Then the voice of the {{hist}} sent {{heita-meen}} beyond the dream, spoke with the adventurer, and let the adventurer wake.

The {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}} had meant to cut the {{argonians}} off from the {{hist}}, and so bring the whole race to an end. The plot failed, and the {{argonians|Argonian}} tribes of the {{ebonheart-pact|Pact}} afterwards pledged themselves to it once more.

## Notes

- **The office.** In each region the {{vicecanon|vicecanons}} stood for the three peoples of the {{ebonheart-pact|Pact}} and answered for its safety. They were chosen for their skill in strategy and diplomacy.
- **The trees' fate.** What became of the troubled {{hist}} trees depends on the answers the adventurer gives in the dream, and the game leaves it to the player.
