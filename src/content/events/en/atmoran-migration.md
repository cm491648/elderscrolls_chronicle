North of {{tamriel}}, across a cold sea, lay {{atmora}}. In those days it was still a green land of forests and marshes. The {{atmorans}} were seafarers and hunters, and they often fought among themselves.

For centuries, {{atmorans}} who had no future at home crossed the sea to {{tamriel}}. They came in many waves over hundreds of years. Each new group clashed with those who had arrived before, and the earlier settlers grew into peoples of their own. All of them brought the same faith: animal totems such as the hawk, the wolf, the bear and the whale, with the dragon honored above the rest.

Late in the {{era-merethic}}, civil war broke out in {{atmora}}, and many families chose to leave. One fleet sailed south and came ashore at the northern tip of {{skyrim}}. {{ysgramor}} was among them, with his family, including his sons {{yngol}} and {{ylgar}}.

The land belonged to the {{falmer}}. The newcomers and the elves kept the peace. More ships followed, and the settlement grew. {{ysgramor}} and his people built {{saarthal}}, the first city of men in that land and the largest.

Around the time of {{ysgramor}}'s crossing, the weather of {{atmora}} began to turn. Over the ages that followed, the continent froze. Expeditions in the {{era-third}} found a land of permanent winter with little life and no trace of people.

## Notes

- **Not the first.** {{nords|Nord}} tradition remembers {{ysgramor}} as the leader of the first great migration, but he was following crossings that had gone on for generations. The earliest waves became the forebears of the {{bretons}} and the men of {{cyrodiil}}.
- **Origins.** Myths disagree on where men came from. {{nords|Nord}} belief holds that the first men were made on the {{throat-of-the-world}} and only later crossed north to {{atmora}}.
- **The freezing.** No one knows why {{atmora}} froze. One tale calls it the revenge of the {{falmer}} for their destruction by the {{atmorans}}.
- **Dates.** No years survive. One account puts the founding of {{saarthal}} in the middle of the {{era-merethic}}, while the story of {{ysgramor}}'s voyage places it near the end.
