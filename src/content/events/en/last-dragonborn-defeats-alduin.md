Among the prisoners who escaped when {{alduin}} burned {{helgen}} was a traveller from {{cyrodiil}}. Soon afterwards, near {{whiterun}}, this stranger helped kill a dragon and took its soul into their own body. That was the mark of the {{dragonborn}}, a mortal born with the soul of a dragon. The {{greybeards}} called the stranger to their monastery of {{high-hrothgar}} with a Shout heard across {{skyrim}}, and there taught them the {{thuum|Voice}}.

On an ancient wall in a hidden temple of the {{blades}}, the {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} saw that the old heroes had once used a Shout against {{alduin}}. The {{greybeards}} did not know it, nor did their master, the dragon {{paarthurnax}}. He explained that the heroes had used an {{elder-scroll}} to cast {{alduin}} out of time, and that reading it again on the summit of the {{throat-of-the-world}} might show what they had done. The {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} brought the Scroll up from the depths beneath {{skyrim}}, read it on the summit, saw the ancient battle and learned {{dragonrend}}. When {{alduin}} came, he was beaten off.

The {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} then trapped the dragon {{odahviing}} in the Jarl's hall at {{whiterun}}. He told them that {{alduin}} had fled to {{sovngarde}}, the land of the {{nords|Nord}} dead, to feed on souls, through a gate only a dragon could reach, and he flew them to it in return for his freedom.

{{alduin}} had wrapped {{sovngarde}} in mist to snare the souls of the dead. In its great hall the {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} found the three heroes of old, {{gormlaith-golden-hilt}}, {{felldir-the-old}} and {{hakon-one-eye}}. Together the four drove off the mist and fought {{alduin}}. Brought down by {{dragonrend}}, his flesh burned away, his scales scattered as glowing shards, and at last even his bones faded until nothing remained. His soul was not taken.

## Notes

- **The hero.** The {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}}'s name, race and sex are not recorded.
- **The dragon's end.** Whether {{alduin}} was destroyed for good is uncertain; his soul may return at the end of time.
- **The old dragon.** The {{blades}} demanded {{paarthurnax}}'s death; what became of him is not known.
- **The war.** Whether the {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} took a side in the civil war is not recorded.
