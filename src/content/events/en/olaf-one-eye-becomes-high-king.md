In 369 of the {{era-first}} the High King of {{skyrim}} was killed, and he left no heir of {{ysgramor}}'s line. The chieftains of the {{nords}} went to war over his throne, and for half a century no claimant could make the others yield.

While the war went on, a dragon named {{numinex}} laid waste to the land. {{olaf-one-eye}}, Jarl of {{whiterun}}, went out after it with a band of his warriors and found it on the summit of {{mount-anthor}}. Their weapons could do nothing against each other, so man and dragon fought with the Voice alone. In the end {{numinex}} fell, beaten, and {{olaf-one-eye|Olaf}} brought it back to {{whiterun}}. The people of the city built a great stone cell behind the palace to hold it, and the palace was renamed {{dragonsreach}}.

The tale spread through every hold. Here at last was a man whom all the warring sides could accept, and in 420 the chieftains made {{olaf-one-eye|Olaf}} High King. The war of succession was over. He was chosen for his fame, not for his kindness or his skill at ruling, and his reign was full of quarrels among the {{nords}}. Yet he held the realm together, and he carried war west into {{the-reach}} and conquered it.

In his time a bard named {{svaknir}} wrote a verse that called the king a fraud and a tyrant. {{olaf-one-eye|Olaf}} had the bard thrown into prison, and had every written copy of the verse that could be found destroyed.

{{olaf-one-eye|Olaf}} died in 452 with no clear heir, and the {{moot}} quarrelled again before it gave the crown to another king.

## Notes

- **The dragon.** A historian of {{skyrim}} found old bardic texts that tell it differently: {{numinex}} was old and close to death when {{olaf-one-eye|Olaf}}'s men came upon it, and they took it with almost no fight. Every man who was there later grew rich and powerful.
- **Before the dragon.** The same historian records two versions of who {{olaf-one-eye|Olaf}} was: already Jarl of {{whiterun}}, or made Jarl as a reward for the dragon. He also leaves open whether {{olaf-one-eye|Olaf}} had long known the Voice or only found it on the mountain.
- **The prisoner.** {{numinex}} was held in {{dragonsreach}} until it died, and its skull still hangs above the throne there.
- **The bard.** The verse survived all the same, and the bard's remains were later found in {{olaf-one-eye|Olaf}}'s own tomb. The verse says {{solitude}} alone of the holds refused to kneel to him.
- **Memory.** A plaque in {{windhelm}} honours him for making {{skyrim}} whole again. In {{solitude}} the bards burn a figure of him every year, at a festival called the Burning of King {{olaf-one-eye|Olaf}}.
