In {{era-second|2E}} 852 the kingdom that {{cuhlecain}} was building in {{cyrodiil}} was invaded from the north. An allied army of {{nords}} from {{skyrim}} and {{bretons}} from {{high-rock}} crossed the border, took the passes and towns of the {{jerall-mountains}}, and settled in for the winter at {{sancre-tor}}. From there they dared the king's new general, {{talos}}, to come and fight them.

{{sancre-tor}} was thought impregnable. Its citadel stood on high cliffs above the lower town, with sheer heights behind it, and its entrance was hidden by magic beneath what looked like a mountain lake. When {{talos}} marched on them in the dead of winter with a small, hungry, badly equipped army, the allies left only a few men in the citadel and came down to destroy him.

That was what {{talos}} had wanted. He left a weak force in the lowlands to hold them, and led the rest down the heights behind the citadel by an obscure trail and in through the hidden entrance under the false lake. While his lowland force fought for its life, his men swept aside the few defenders and captured the allied nobles and generals, who were forced to surrender the citadel and their armies.

The captive {{nords}}, already wary of the scheming {{bretons|Breton}} sorcerer-lords, left the alliance, swore loyalty to {{talos}} and joined his army. The {{bretons|Breton}} battlemages who had led the allies were executed, and the captive {{bretons}} were imprisoned or sold as slaves.

{{sancre-tor}} was where the emperors of the old {{reman}} line lay buried. {{talos}} later said he had dared so much because he had seen, in a vision, the {{amulet-of-kings}} lying in {{reman-iii}}'s tomb. Two years later he was crowned emperor as {{tiber-septim}}.

## Notes

- **The traitor.** According to the book *Battle of Sancre Tor*, both the trail and the hidden entrance were revealed by one traitor whose name is not given; tradition makes him a {{bretons|Breton}} sorcerer who changed sides.
- **The veterans.** The {{nords}} who joined him played a large part in the campaigns that followed.
- **The amulet.** Some accounts say {{talos}} took the {{amulet-of-kings}} from {{reman-iii}}'s tomb when he captured the fortress.
- **A change of sides.** Another account says the {{nords}} changed sides when they heard {{talos}} shout in the tongue of dragons and hailed him as a son of {{skyrim}}.
- **After.** The fortress lay in an awkward place, and {{talos}} soon abandoned it to fall into ruin.
