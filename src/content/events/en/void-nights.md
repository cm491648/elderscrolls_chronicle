In 98 of the {{era-fourth}} both moons, {{masser}} and {{secunda}}, vanished from the sky. For two years they did not come back, and no one could say where they had gone. Across the Empire people were confused and afraid.

In {{elsweyr}} it was far worse, for the moons stood at the heart of the {{khajiit}} faith. In their legends the moving moons formed a lattice that shielded the world from what lay beyond it.

In 100 the moons returned. The {{thalmor}} announced that they had brought them back by means of the "Dawn Magicks", an art no one had heard of before. It was the first time the {{aldmeri-dominion}} had spoken to the Empire since it was born again, seventy years before.

The {{khajiit}} hailed the {{thalmor}} as their saviours, and over the next fifteen years the Empire's influence in {{elsweyr}} dwindled. In 115 a coup split the province into two kingdoms, {{anequina}} and {{pelletine}}, both bound to the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}}. The Empire could do nothing.

## Notes

- **The cause.** No one knows why the moons disappeared, nor whether the {{thalmor}} truly brought them back or only knew that they would return.
- **The road to war.** The {{thalmor}}'s new hold over {{elsweyr}} was one of the steps that led to the {{great-war}}.
- **The island.** At about the same time the island of {{artaeum}}, home of the {{psijic-order}}, vanished. No source links the two.
