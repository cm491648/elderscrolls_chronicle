In later centuries the {{orcs}} lost {{orsinium}} once more, and for hundreds of years they had no homeland. In 399 of the {{era-third}}, the {{orcs|Orc}} chieftain {{gortwog}} met the land's {{bretons|Breton}} claimant, a lord, in a duel. He won, and with the victory he took land near the old city.

There he built a new city, named {{nova-orsinium}}, on a mountainside between {{wayrest}} and the {{wrothgar|Wrothgarian Mountains}}. It was built of iron and filled with statues of {{orcs|Orc}} heroes, and {{gortwog}} ruled from a citadel of steel. Most people around the {{iliac-bay}} still looked on the {{orcs}} as villains. Even so, the land prospered under him.

{{gortwog}} did not seek conquest. He wanted his people raised to equal standing with the other races of {{tamriel}}, in a homeland the Empire would recognize. He proved a skilled diplomat. King {{lysandus}} of {{daggerfall}} backed his claim to rule, and {{gortwog}} gathered secrets on his rivals to use against them.

One of the things he bought was a stolen letter. In 401 Emperor {{uriel-septim-vii}} had written to the queen of {{daggerfall}}, asking her to use her influence at {{wayrest}} to recover the {{totem-of-tiber-septim}}. The war between {{daggerfall}} and {{sentinel}} held the letter up, and it never reached her. By the time it arrived a new king ruled {{daggerfall}}, and it went to his queen, {{aubk-i}}. From her court it found its way to {{gortwog}}, who sold copies to the great powers of the bay. The {{orcs}} stood to gain from their quarrels.

After the {{warp-in-the-west}} in 417, {{orsinium}} was one of four kingdoms left on the bay, and by 432 the Empire recognized it as a kingdom of {{high-rock}}, with {{gortwog}} as its king.

## Notes

- **The letter.** In one account, thieves stole it and sold it to the highest bidder.
- **Faith.** {{gortwog}} founded a priesthood of {{trinimac}}, teaching that {{trinimac}} still lived and was not {{malacath}}. Some traditionalists in the city, and most {{orcs}} outside it, called this heresy.
- **The site.** Some sources place the new city on the site of the first {{orsinium}}; others place the first city farther west in {{wrothgar}}. The name {{nova-orsinium}} comes from *Lives of the Emperors*.
