After the {{oblivion-crisis}} the {{elder-council}} could find no fit heir to the throne. The High Chancellor, {{ocato}}, an {{altmer}}, reluctantly agreed to govern as {{potentate}}, and for some years the Council held the Empire together while the provinces went their own ways.

Around 15 of the {{era-fourth}} {{ocato}} was assassinated. The {{elder-council}} broke into factions, and years of in-fighting, plots and betrayal followed. Many tried to claim the throne. Most were pretenders, a few had a lawful claim, and others were brutes who thought strength of arms was all the right they needed. Unnatural storms struck the {{imperial-city}} again and again, their eye always looking down on the {{white-gold-tower}}. While the Empire was in turmoil, the {{thalmor}} seized the {{summerset-isles}}.

After seven years a {{colovia|Colovian}} warlord named {{titus-mede-i|Titus Mede}} seized the crown, around 22. He proved a shrewd and capable ruler, and {{skyrim}} endorsed him as emperor. Under him the Empire began to steady. He was the first emperor of the {{mede-dynasty}}.

## Notes

- **The murder.** The {{altmer}} exile who wrote *Rising Threat* claims that the {{thalmor}} had {{ocato}} killed. It was never proven.
- **The claim.** The writer of *Rising Threat* holds that it hardly matters whether {{titus-mede-i|Titus}} had a rightful claim: without him there would have been no Empire.
- **The dates.** Neither the murder nor the crowning is precisely dated. Both are reckoned from 22, when the {{thalmor}} took {{summerset-isles|Summerset}}, and from the seven years the interregnum is said to have lasted.
- **The dynasty.** The house he founded still held the throne two centuries later.
- **The name.** The years without an emperor are known as the {{stormcrown-interregnum}}.
