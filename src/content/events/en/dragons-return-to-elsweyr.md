In 576 of the {{era-second}}, {{euraxia-tharn}} came to {{rimmen}}, capital of {{anequina}}, as an envoy of the Empire. The king, a loyal ally of the Empire, received her without suspicion. With an army of hired soldiers from {{nibenay}} she murdered the royal family, none of whom was thought to have survived. She proclaimed herself queen and took the north of {{elsweyr}} from {{rimmen}} to {{riverhold}}. The seizure became known as the {{frostfall-coup}}. Her laws burdened the {{khajiit}} with tariffs and fines, and siege engines on her palace were aimed at the city.

The {{khajiit}} told of the hero {{khunzar-ri}}, who in the {{era-merethic}} had shut a host of demons inside the {{halls-of-colossus}}, far to the south. The legends called what lay within a weapon. In 582 {{abnur-tharn}}, {{euraxia-tharn|Euraxia}}'s half-brother and an Imperial battlemage, hoped such a weapon could end the {{three-banners-war}}. He had the two halves of the {{wrathstone}} found, the tablet that led to the {{halls-of-colossus|Halls}} and could open them. {{euraxia-tharn|Euraxia}} wanted the weapon too, and her soldiers lay in wait at the ruins. {{abnur-tharn|Tharn}}, a {{khajiit}} rebel named {{khamira}} and an adventurer fought their way through, and {{abnur-tharn|Tharn}} broke the seal without knowing what it held. Out came dragons, led by {{kaalgrontiid}}, and they spread across {{elsweyr}}.

The northern prides had already formed a force against the queen and had just retaken {{riverhold}}. Now one of {{kaalgrontiid}}'s dragons made an alliance with {{euraxia-tharn|Euraxia}}, and as part of the bargain she ordered the last known member of the {{dragonguard}} in the province killed. {{abnur-tharn|Tharn}} and the adventurer went to {{rimmen}} to warn her that the dragons were using her. Meanwhile her soldiers, her necromancers' dead and a dragon fell on {{riverhold}}. The city held, and afterwards {{khamira}} revealed that she was the murdered king's daughter, the heir to his throne.

A small band slipped into the palace through the sewers. In the courtyard {{khamira}}, {{abnur-tharn|Tharn}} and the adventurer faced {{euraxia-tharn|Euraxia}}, and learned that the dragons had abandoned her. She raised the dead king and queen against them, but she was killed, and the rebels took the palace.

The dragons had gone to the {{moon-gate-of-anequina}} and through it into the {{plane-of-jode}}, where {{kaalgrontiid}} began to drain the moon's power from {{jodes-core}}. {{khamira}}, {{abnur-tharn|Tharn}} and the adventurer followed and banished him before he could take it all. {{khamira}} was then crowned queen of {{anequina}}.

## Notes

- **The old seal.** In the {{khajiit}} tale, {{khunzar-ri}} and four companions could not beat the dragons by force. They persuaded them to pour their strength into {{jodes-core}}, as if to join it to the moon's power, then lured the weakened dragons into the {{halls-of-colossus}} and sealed them there. In time the legends remembered them only as demons.
- **The queen's plan.** A letter of {{euraxia-tharn|Euraxia}}'s shows that she herself brought the {{wrathstone}} to her brother's notice, counting on him to open the {{halls-of-colossus|Halls}} so that her soldiers could seize the weapon.
- **What he took.** Only later did it become clear that {{kaalgrontiid}} had already absorbed a great deal before he was driven out, chiefly the life of the dragons whose strength had been stored in the {{jodes-core|Core}} long ago.
- **The name.** Rendered from the dragons' own speech, {{kaalgrontiid}} comes out as "Champion of the Bound Time".
