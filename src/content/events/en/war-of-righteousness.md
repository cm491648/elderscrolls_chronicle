For a thousand years the {{alessian-order}} had ruled the empire from {{cyrodiil}}. By the twenty-fourth century of the {{era-first}}, its priesthood had grown larger than the empire could support, and its hold on distant lands was failing. In {{era-first|1E}} 2305 {{high-rock}}, tired of the Order's excesses and its hatred of elves, broke away.

The west of {{cyrodiil}} was drifting away as well. The king of {{skingrad}} arranged to marry his child to a child of the queen of {{wayrest}}. The {{arch-prelate}}, the head of the Order, feared an alliance between {{high-rock}} and the west and forbade the match. The king had the marriage made anyway, and the {{arch-prelate}} sent his legions to punish the west. Riots, local risings and shifting alliances followed, and the fighting spread into the lands of the {{bretons}}. A force sent to subdue them was destroyed in {{bangkorai}}. In 1E 2321 the west set up its own government, the {{colovian-estates}}, and the war began in earnest.

It was costly for both sides. The {{colovian-estates}} sold off distant islands to pay for it. The Order, losing in the west, taxed and conscripted the people of the east, and the people of {{nibenay}} rose against it. They killed the {{arch-prelate}} and destroyed the Order's great cathedral at {{lake-canulus}}. With no leaders and no money, the Order's armies in {{high-rock}} gave up. Ten years of war had killed half the people of the {{iliac-bay}}.

In 1E 2331 the war ended, and with it the {{alessian-order}} and its empire. A puppet emperor was set up at {{skingrad}}. The empire's other lands kept the independence they had won, and {{cyrodiil}} split into an eastern and a western half that went their own ways.

## Notes

- **Causes.** The success of the {{all-flags-navy}} against {{thras}} had made the west of {{cyrodiil}} more powerful than the east, which deepened the split.
- **Dates.** One book sends the legions against {{high-rock}} in 1E 2305; another says the Order held back until after the marriage, in 1E 2319.
- **Lost records.** Whatever records the Order's secret sect kept of its work in the {{middle-dawn}} were destroyed in this war.
- **Later.** {{cyrodiil}} and {{tamriel}} stayed divided until {{reman}} founded the {{second-empire}} in 1E 2703.
