{{uriel-v}} came to the throne in {{era-third|3E}} 268. Rather than settle the quarrels he had inherited, he turned the Empire outward and conquered one island realm after another beyond the shores of {{tamriel}}. Then he set his sights on {{akavir}}, the continent from which invaders had come against {{tamriel}} for thousands of years.

Scouting fleets chose the kingdom of the {{tsaesci}} in the south-west as the first target. In 288 the strongest fleet {{tamriel}} had ever assembled carried four legions east. After six weeks at sea they landed at an empty port at a river mouth, then marched upriver to a second empty city, which they named {{ionith}}. Envoys sent to the {{tsaesci}} never came back.

Then the weather turned against them. Storms wrecked much of the fleet on its way home and kept supplies away all winter. {{tsaesci}} raiders harried the colonies, and a delegation that came to talk peace tried to murder the Emperor in his quarters. Twice the {{elder-council}} begged him to come home, and twice he refused. In the summer of 289 he crossed the mountains and scattered a {{tsaesci}} army, but he was driven back to {{ionith}} and besieged.

In 290 a relief fleet reached the coast. {{uriel-v|Uriel}} broke out of {{ionith}} by night to reach it, but was surrounded on the road. He made a stand with the legion that guarded him so that others could get away, and fell under a rain of arrows as he rallied its shield wall. Few survivors reached the ships. His five-year-old son became emperor.

## Notes

- **The verdict.** An inquiry afterward found that he had not acted recklessly. He is still counted the greatest warrior emperor after {{tiber-septim}}.
- **The cost.** The loss of the army left the Empire dangerously weak against its own provinces.
