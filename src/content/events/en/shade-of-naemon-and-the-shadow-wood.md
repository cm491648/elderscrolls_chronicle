In 582 of the {{era-second}}, Prince {{naemon}} was in {{elden-root}}, capital of the {{aldmeri-dominion}} in {{valenwood}}. A ceremony at the city's {{orrery-of-elden-root|Orrery}} was to divine whether {{ayrenn}} was fit to rule as empress. The queen entered its main chamber with the prince and King {{camoran-aeradan}}. When the device was set working, {{naemon}} cast a spell that held his sister and the others motionless, and stepped in first. The {{orrery-of-elden-root|Orrery}} turned him into an {{ogrim}}, and an agent of the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}} killed him. {{ayrenn}} finished the ceremony and saw a vision of {{tamriel}} at peace under her rule. The court gave out that the prince had died a hero, and she asked his devoted advisor, Vicereeve {{pelidil}}, to take the body to {{marbruk}} in {{greenshade}} for the funeral.

{{pelidil}} reached {{marbruk}}, then vanished with the body. He took the {{staff-of-magnus}} from the {{mages-guild}} there and assumed command of what was left of the {{veiled-heritance}}. He promised {{valenwood}} to the {{maormer}} for their help. In {{woodhearth}} one of his agents tried to kill {{ayrenn}}, but the figure in the queen's robes was {{razum-dar}}, her spymaster, disguised by illusion. The society and the {{maormer}} then took the coastal town of {{seaside-sanctuary}}, and there {{pelidil}} raised {{naemon}} from the dead as a lich. The prince spat in his face and walked away. The {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}} retook the town, and the agent killed {{pelidil}} in a battle at sea.

The lich, the {{naemon|Shade of Naemon}}, took over the society's remnants and an army of the dead in northern {{greenshade}}. He began to turn its forests into the {{shadow-wood}}, a double of {{valenwood}} in endless night, full of shades and corrupted spirits. He meant to put it in place of all {{valenwood}} and drive every {{bosmer}} mad. Spirits of {{bosmer}} executed for breaking the {{green-pact}} joined him, expecting to live again in the new realm.

After the shade's forces struck {{marbruk}}, the {{bosmer|Bosmer}} cleric {{indaenir}} felt {{the-green}} crying out for vengeance. With the agent he cleansed corrupted sites and destroyed pockets of the {{shadow-wood|Shadow Wood}}. The shade had used the staff to break the old {{ayleid}} wards of {{hectahame}} and corrupt the {{heart-of-valenwood}}, a tree deep inside the ruin. The {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}}'s fighters could not get in, but {{indaenir}} and the agent entered through the {{shadow-wood|Shadow Wood}} and destroyed the lich. Darkness was still spreading from the {{heart-of-valenwood|Heart}}. {{indaenir}} drew it out of the tree into himself and died. The forest brought him back to life, and those who saw it took it as {{yffre}} choosing him as the new {{silvenar}}.

## Notes

- **The official story.** After the second death {{ayrenn}} cleared {{naemon}}'s name and ordered a royal funeral. She said papers of {{pelidil}} and {{estre}} showed that they had raised the prince only to use him as a figurehead.
- **Succession.** The previous {{silvenar}} had been murdered earlier that year by a {{maormer}} envoy.
- **Dissent.** Some {{bosmer}} denounced {{indaenir}} as a puppet of the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}}, saying it had corrupted their sacred rites.
