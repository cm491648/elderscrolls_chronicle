In {{era-second|2E}} 560 a sickness broke out in the city of {{stormhold}} in {{black-marsh}}. It began with weariness and loss of appetite, then came aching, chills, fever and a red rash that spread over the whole body. At the end the sick bled from the nose, the eyes and the mouth. Most died within three days.

It spread through all of {{black-marsh}} and killed almost everyone there who was not an {{argonians|Argonian}}. The {{kothringi}}, the silver-skinned human people of the marshes, were all but wiped out; within a year some of their villages stood empty. The {{argonians}} hardly fell sick at all, and the other peoples came to believe that the {{argonians}} had made the disease.

The flu crossed into southern {{morrowind}} and over the {{topal-bay}} into {{elsweyr}}, and from there to {{valenwood}} and as far as {{high-rock}}. In {{senchal}}, fear drove people to burn whole quarters of the city. In {{wayrest}} it killed the entire royal family. A ship full of {{kothringi}} refugees, the {{crimson-ship}}, was turned away from every port it tried and was lost at sea.

The last cases were recorded in 2E 603, forty-three years after the first. Of the outsiders who had lived in {{black-marsh}}, only {{dunmer}} slavers stayed on, and the empire had lost any hope of ruling the province.

## Notes

- **The cause.** The tale told among outsiders was that an {{argonians|Argonian}} shaman, in revenge for how his people had been treated, had made the disease through the {{hist}}, their sacred trees. Other accounts say the flu was natural.
- **Immunity.** Some {{argonians}} say they do catch it, only mildly, and deny any part in it.
- **Survivors.** Later writers say the {{kothringi}} died out entirely, but a few of their communities are known to have lived on for some decades by unnatural means.
- **Consequences.** The death of the royal family of {{wayrest}} brought a new king to the throne who went on to found the {{daggerfall-covenant}}. The {{khajiit}}, grateful to the {{altmer}} healers who helped them through the plague, later joined the {{aldmeri-dominion}}. The freedom the {{argonians}} gained let them join the {{ebonheart-pact}} as equals.
- **The ship.** The people of {{hammerfell}} keep a day of shame in memory of the {{crimson-ship}} and stay indoors, for it is said the ship will return on that day.
