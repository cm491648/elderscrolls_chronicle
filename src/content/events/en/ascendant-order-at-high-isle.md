Lord {{bacaro-volorus}}, born to an {{imperials|Imperial}} father and a {{bretons|Breton}} mother, lost his partner and children in a revolt in {{colovia}}. In his grief he gave his fortune to charity, and during the {{three-banners-war}} he founded the {{society-of-the-steadfast}} to feed and shelter the war's victims. He settled on his estates in the {{systres}}.

In the same years the {{ascendant-order}} grew. Its members hid their faces and called themselves knights striking at tyranny from the shadows. Its proclamation called to common folk whose lives the war had ruined, and many answered. The Order made the {{systres}} its base.

In 582 of the {{era-second}} {{bacaro-volorus|Bacaro}} invited the leaders of the three alliances to peace talks on {{high-isle}}; {{jorunn}} sent his son {{irnskar}}. The ships bringing the leaders never reached harbour, and an investigator in {{bacaro-volorus|Bacaro}}'s service set out with an adventurer to find them. {{irnskar}}, Queen {{ayrenn}} of the {{aldmeri-dominion}} and High King {{emeric}} of the {{daggerfall-covenant}} each faced attempts on their lives. The trail led to Count {{damard-dufort}}, one of the island lords, who was unmasked as the Order's chief mage. He led the Order against {{all-flags-islet}}, in the harbour at the heart of {{high-isle}}, where the leaders had gathered. The adventurer held the walls beside {{irnskar}} and killed {{damard-dufort|Dufort}} in the castle courtyard. {{bacaro-volorus|Bacaro}} then held a celebration to revive the talks, and all three leaders came.

On a neighbouring island, meanwhile, the druids had fallen to fighting among themselves. The leader of one circle tried to make herself {{druid-king}} by force and failed. Then {{bacaro-volorus|Bacaro}} himself was unmasked as the Order's lord, and the failed archdruid proved to have been his unwitting pawn. Through his mother he was the last descendant of {{kasorayn}}, the last {{druid-king}} of the {{druids-of-galen}}. He claimed the {{ivy-throne}} as his birthright, meaning to set it in place of the throne of the empire. On {{mount-firesong}} he made himself a false {{druid-king}} and bound the spirit of the volcano, a power that could destroy whole armies. The adventurer defeated and killed him there. The bound spirit fled, another spirit of nature took its place and calmed the mountain, and the islands were saved.

The members of the {{society-of-the-steadfast|Society}} had known nothing of their founder's plans. A trust he had set up years before kept it running. It went on helping the victims of war and disaster and paying for the peace talks, which began again.

## Notes

- **The outcome.** What the renewed talks achieved is not recorded.
- **The prophecy.** {{kasorayn}} foresaw two futures for the druids: a renewal under a freely chosen new king, or a scourge under a tyrant. With {{bacaro-volorus|Bacaro}}'s defeat the second was averted.
- **Another future.** A tome scried by the {{psijic-order}} describes a world where {{bacaro-volorus|Bacaro}} won and ruled {{tamriel}} from the {{ivy-throne}}, firestorms fell on "false kings and queens", and volcanic smoke darkened the sky.
- **The islands.** In 582 the {{systres}} were held by {{bretons|Breton}} houses as vassals of the king of {{wayrest}}.
