After the {{soulburst}}, the barrier that shielded the world from {{oblivion}} lay broken, and {{molag-bal}} set out to drag {{nirn}} into his own realm, {{coldharbour}}. Great chains came down from the sky across {{tamriel}}: the {{dark-anchors}}. Each one hooked into the earth and slowly tore pieces of the land away, and {{daedra}} came down the chains. The largest hung over the {{imperial-city}}, meant to pull the {{white-gold-tower}} itself into {{coldharbour}}. Every death weakened the barrier further, and the {{three-banners-war}} brought many.

Few were left to resist. A servant of {{meridia}}, {{molag-bal}}'s old enemy, took control of the {{fighters-guild}} and turned it to destroying the anchors. The {{mages-guild}} under {{vanus-galerion}} joined the fight. The companions whom {{mannimarco}} had betrayed at the {{soulburst}} were freed from {{coldharbour}} by a hero whose soul {{molag-bal}} had stolen, and together they took back the {{amulet-of-kings}}.

In {{era-second|2E}} 582 the guilds met the leaders of the three warring alliances on the island of {{stirk}}. {{daedra}} attacked the meeting, and the leaders agreed to let the guilds lead an invasion of {{coldharbour}}. There the invaders destroyed the great device that bound the anchors together. At the heart of the meld, {{meridia}} loosed her light and tore it apart, undoing what {{molag-bal}} had won.

{{molag-bal}} withdrew to gather his strength. To end his hold on the world for good, the companions went to his fortress with the {{amulet-of-kings}}. One of them gave their life so that the hero could take on the power of {{akatosh}}. The hero defeated {{molag-bal}} in single combat and cast him into the void, and the souls and lands he had taken were set free.

## Notes

- **The date.** Sources disagree on when the meld began; the dates range from {{era-second|2E}} 578 to 582.
- **The hero.** The sources call the hero the Vestige and give no fixed name.
- **The cost.** The confrontation spent the power of the {{amulet-of-kings}} for more than a generation, and {{molag-bal}} needed a long time to recover.
