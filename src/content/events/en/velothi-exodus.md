{{veloth}} was born to a noble family on the {{summerset-isles}}, but he scorned the life around him. The {{aldmer}}, he said, had built their society on ambition, greed and decadence. He called on those who wanted a plainer and more honest life, and many came to him.

He set down the Velothi Prophecies. They turned away from the {{aedra}}, whom the {{aldmer}} honored as their ancestors, and toward three {{daedra}}: {{boethiah}}, {{azura}} and {{mephala}}. He also taught his followers how to deal warily with the {{daedra}} who meant them harm. Clan after clan took him for their prophet. The {{sapiarchs}} forbade the movement, and the priests of {{trinimac}} called it blasphemy and threatened exile.

{{veloth}} led his people out. He took every boat, every ration and every strong arm he could find and set out for a land in the northeast that he called {{resdayn}}. They crossed the sea and climbed a great range of mountains, following a hawk he had seen in a dream. Many thought the journey folly. {{veloth}} would not turn back.

At a pass in the snow, a wall of ice blocked the way. A young woman appeared and asked {{veloth}} for an oath before she would let them through. He laid down his warhammer and swore to give his life and soul to his people. She melted the ice, and below them lay a land of ash and fungus. A single tear ran down {{veloth}}'s cheek and froze.

His followers gave up the name of {{aldmer}} and called themselves the {{chimer}}. In {{resdayn}} they no longer had to hide their shrines, and they built temples to {{boethiah}}, {{azura}} and {{mephala}}. One of their first towns was named for the prophet: {{narsis}}.

## Notes

- **The teacher.** Some accounts say {{boethiah}} herself led {{veloth}} to reject the {{aedra}}, through dreams and visions. Others say she came to them in the form of {{trinimac}}. The {{altmer}} tell of a battle between {{trinimac}} and {{boethiah}} over the dissidents, after which {{trinimac}} became {{malacath}} and his followers the {{orcs}}.
- **Those who stayed.** Not every follower left. Some clans stayed on the {{summerset-isles}} and kept the faith in secret.
- **Afterward.** The frozen tear was kept as a holy relic that heals. The {{chimer}} later became the {{dunmer}}, who honor {{veloth}} as a saint of outcasts and seekers; the {{ashlanders}} still follow his teachings and reject the {{tribunal}}.
- **Date.** Most sources place the exodus in the later Middle {{era-merethic}}; some traditions set it in the {{era-dawn}}.
