The {{thieves-guild}} of {{cyrodiil}} was led by a master thief known as the {{gray-fox}}. He wore a gray cowl and never showed himself in public, and the watch put up posters for his arrest. Many people doubted he was real. Those who believed in him took him for a single man who had run the guild for three hundred years.

In 433 of the {{era-third}}, {{hieronymus-lex}}, a captain of the watch in the {{imperial-city}}, made it his own business to catch the {{gray-fox|Gray Fox}}. He pressed hard on the guild and its friends. The guild schemed against him in return, and in the end his hunt came to nothing. That same year a newcomer joined the guild and rose quickly through its ranks, until the {{gray-fox|Gray Fox}} began to give out orders in person.

At last he told the newcomer what the cowl was. It was the {{gray-cowl-of-nocturnal}}, stolen long ago from {{nocturnal}}, the {{daedra|Daedric}} Prince of night. She had cursed it. Whoever put it on became the {{gray-fox|Gray Fox}}, and the person the wearer had been was lost. The cowl had passed from one master of the guild to the next. The man who wore it now had spent eleven years looking for a way to break the curse, and the answer lay in an {{elder-scroll}}.

The scroll was kept in the palace library within the {{white-gold-tower}}. The newcomer went in by the old tunnels under the city, found the library, took the scroll and came out again, and brought it to the {{gray-fox|Gray Fox}}. He did not use it there. He gave the thief a ring to carry to the countess of {{anvil}}, and followed.

In her presence at the castle of {{anvil}}, the {{gray-fox|Gray Fox}} used the scroll to reach back to the day the cowl was first stolen and spoke the name of the thief who took it, and the curse was broken. Only then did he say who he was: {{corvus-umbranox}}, the Count of {{anvil}}, missing for years while his wife waited for him. He stayed with her and passed the cowl to the thief who had helped him, and the {{gray-fox|Gray Fox}} went on leading the guild. From then on, whoever wore the cowl could take it off and be known again.

## Notes

- **The first thief.** The cowl was first stolen from {{nocturnal}} by a thief named {{emer-dareloth}}, some time before 133 of the {{era-third}}. Every {{gray-fox|Gray Fox}} since has worn it.
- **The scrolls.** {{elder-scroll|Elder Scrolls}} record both what has happened and what may happen, and their text about the future can shift until it comes to pass. The {{imperial-city}} held the largest known collection, in the keeping of the {{moth-priest|Moth Priests}}.
