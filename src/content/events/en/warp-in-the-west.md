In 403 of the {{era-third}}, the king of {{daggerfall}} died, and at first no one knew he had been murdered. The {{iliac-bay}}, between {{high-rock}} and {{hammerfell}}, was then divided among forty-four rival states. Two years later an agent of the {{blades}} arrived on the orders of Emperor {{uriel-septim-vii}} to look into the matter. The agent found that a lord of {{wayrest}} had killed the king in a bid for power, and took from the plotters the {{totem-of-tiber-septim}}. Whoever held it could command {{numidium}}, the brass giant {{tiber-septim}} had once used to conquer {{tamriel}}.

The {{totem-of-tiber-septim|Totem}} would answer only to a worthy holder, and seven claimants came forward. {{daggerfall}}, {{sentinel}} and {{wayrest}} each wanted to crush the other two. An {{orcs|Orc}} warlord wanted a recognized homeland for his people at {{orsinium}}. The necromancer {{mannimarco}} wanted the power of the {{mantella}}, the great gem that drove the giant, to make himself a god. The {{underking}} wanted back the heart sealed inside it, so that he could finally die. The Emperor wanted the bay brought to order. The agent chose a holder, recovered the {{mantella}}, and the giant was woken.

From the 9th to the 11th of {{frostfall}}, 417, disaster struck the region: earthquakes, sandstorms, fires and great waves. Seven battles were fought at once around the bay. Armies reached fields they could not have marched to in time. For some people the three days ran together into one.

When it ended, four kingdoms remained: {{daggerfall}}, {{sentinel}}, {{wayrest}} and {{orsinium}}, all loyal to the Empire. {{numidium}}, the {{underking}} and the agent were not seen again. Despite the destruction, the people of the bay came to call it the {{miracle-of-peace}}.

## Notes

- **Findings.** {{blades}} reports concluded that each of the seven claimants had held the {{totem-of-tiber-septim|Totem}} at the same moment, and that each had achieved its aim to some degree. For this reason the event is often counted as a {{dragon-break}} like the {{middle-dawn}}, though it seems to have stayed within the region.
- **Identity.** One tradition makes the {{underking}} {{tiber-septim}}'s battlemage {{zurin-arctus}}, who had put his own heart into the {{mantella}}; *The Arcturian Heresy* names the {{nords|Nord}} hero {{wulfharth}} instead.
- **Afterward.** {{mannimarco}}'s followers took a new moon that appeared in later years as the sign of his rise to godhood.
