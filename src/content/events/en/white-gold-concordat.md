In 175 of the {{era-fourth}}, after the {{battle-of-the-red-ring}}, Emperor {{titus-mede-ii}} made peace with the {{aldmeri-dominion}}. His legions had won back the {{imperial-city}} but could not fight on. The treaty, the {{white-gold-concordat}}, ended the {{great-war}}.

Its terms were these. The worship of {{talos}} was forbidden throughout the Empire. The {{blades}} were disbanded. A large part of southern {{hammerfell}} passed to the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}}. The {{thalmor}} were given the right to act within the Empire's borders to make sure the ban was kept.

The {{redguards}} refused to give up their land and kept fighting. To save the treaty, {{titus-mede-ii|Titus Mede II}} formally renounced {{hammerfell}} as a province of the Empire. {{hammerfell}} fought on alone and held the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}} to a standstill. In 180 the {{second-treaty-of-stros-mkai}} sent the elven armies home. {{hammerfell}} came out independent but devastated, and the {{redguards}} saw the Empire's withdrawal as a betrayal.

In {{skyrim}} the ban was at first barely enforced. Then in 176 {{ulfric-stormcloak}}'s militia, promised that they could worship {{talos}} openly, won back {{markarth}} from the native clans who had seized it during the war. When the {{thalmor}} found {{talos}} worshipped there in the open, {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} and his men were arrested, and the arrest became one of the grievances behind the {{stormcloaks}}' rebellion.

## Notes

- **Defense and criticism.** The emperor's defenders say the concessions bought the shattered legions time to rebuild. His critics point out that the terms were almost the same as the ultimatum he had rejected four years earlier.
- **A suspicion.** The legate who wrote *The Great War* suspects that driving {{hammerfell}} and the Empire apart was one of the {{thalmor}}'s aims from the start.
