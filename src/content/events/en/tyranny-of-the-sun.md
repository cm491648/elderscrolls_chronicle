Long ago {{harkon}}, a {{nords|Nord}} king who feared death, made a pact with {{molag-bal}} and became, with his wife and daughter, a vampire of the purest blood. From his castle he ruled the {{volkihar}} vampires. But the sun still weakened them, and he became obsessed with an old prophecy, the {{tyranny-of-the-sun}}: one day the sun would be blotted out and the world would fall into endless night. To fulfil it he needed {{auriels-bow}} and the blood of his daughter, {{serana}}. His wife, {{valerica}}, fled with her, hid {{serana|Serana}} in a sealed tomb with an {{elder-scroll}}, and took a second Scroll into the {{soul-cairn}}.

In 201 of the {{era-fourth}}, as vampires grew bolder in {{skyrim}}, hunters gathered again as the {{dawnguard}}. Drawn into the hunt, the {{last-dragonborn}} found {{serana|Serana}} in her tomb and brought her home. With her Scroll in his hands, {{harkon}} moved to fulfil the prophecy.

A {{moth-priest}} read the Scroll: the prophecy spoke of a dread lord rising among the children of the night, in an age when dragons returned. The {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} and {{serana|Serana}} found the other two Scrolls, one in the {{soul-cairn}} and one deep in a {{dwemer|Dwarven}} ruin, and through them learned where the bow lay: in an ancient {{falmer|Snow Elf}} temple, guarded by its high priest, {{vyrthur}}, who had written the prophecy himself after becoming a vampire. They killed him, and the bow passed into the {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}}'s hands.

Then the {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} and {{serana|Serana}} went to {{harkon}}'s castle, confronted him and killed him.

## Notes

- **Whose side.** In the conflict between the {{dawnguard}} and the {{volkihar}}, which side the {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} joined is not recorded.
- **The prophecy.** With the bow in the {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}}'s hands, it is unknown whether the prophecy was stopped or only changed.
