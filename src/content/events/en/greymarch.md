In {{era-third|3E}} 433, as the era drew to a close, a door to the {{shivering-isles}} opened on an island in the great bay at the heart of {{cyrodiil}}. A mortal came through it into the realm of {{sheogorath}}, the {{daedra|Daedric}} Prince of madness, and found him searching for a champion.

{{sheogorath}} explained what was coming. The Isles had once been the realm of {{jyggalag}}, Prince of order, whom the other Princes had cursed to become the madness he hated. Once in every era the curse let him return: crystal obelisks rose, grey knights of order marched out of them, and the Isles were laid waste. Then he became {{sheogorath}} again and had to build his realm anew. This was the {{greymarch}}, and nothing had ever stopped it. His new plan was that the mortal would take his place.

The obelisks rose, and the forces of order struck first at the gate of the Isles and then at the springs that fed its guardians, and the mortal turned back each assault. Then {{sheogorath}} himself became {{jyggalag}}. With a new staff made from the power of the realm, the mortal took up the Madgod's power and sat on his throne. {{jyggalag}} came to the palace himself and fought, and was beaten. He yielded the Isles, congratulated the mortal on breaking the cycle, and went off to wander {{oblivion}}, free at last. The mortal remained as the new Prince of Madness.

## Notes

- **The mortal.** The mortal is generally taken to be the hero who had fought through the {{oblivion-crisis}} that same year.
- **How often.** {{sheogorath}} said the {{greymarch}} came at the end of every era; other sources speak of every thousand years, or every few thousand.
- **The curse.** The story of the curse is a myth told of the Princes; other explanations of the two Princes exist.
- **Before.** Hints survive that a mortal had taken up the Madgod's mantle once before.
