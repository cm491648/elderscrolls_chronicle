The {{redguards}} of {{hammerfell}} had long been split in two. The {{crowns}}, heirs of the old {{yokuda|Yokudan}} nobility, the {{na-totambu}}, held to the customs of their ancestors and kept outsiders at a distance. The {{forebears}}, who traced their line to the warriors of the {{ra-gada}}, were open to foreign ways and had dealt with the {{imperials}} for generations. By the 850s of the {{era-second}}, the Empire of {{tiber-septim}} was spreading across {{tamriel}}. The old and ailing High King {{thassad-ii}} would not let it in. His heir, {{a-tor}}, stood with the {{crowns}}.

{{thassad-ii}} died peacefully in 862. Then Baron {{volag}}, leader of the {{forebears}}, rose against the court. He took the capital, {{sentinel}}, by force and drove every {{crowns|Crown}} from the city. The bloody war lasted two years. {{a-tor}} regrouped on the island of {{stros-mkai}} in {{hunding-bay}}, the tide turned his way, and the {{crowns}} began to plan the recapture of the capital.

Facing defeat, the {{forebears}} turned to {{tiber-septim}} and made a pact with him: Imperial armies for their cause, in return for lands along {{hammerfell}}'s south-eastern border. The {{crowns}} called them throne-defilers, hosts to the invader, {{frandar-hunding|Hunding}}'s shame. Worn down as they were, they still made the Imperial forces pay dearly. In 864 they made their stand at {{stros-mkai}}.

The {{crowns|Crown}} fleet met the Imperial navy under Admiral {{amiel-richton}}. The battle at sea turned slowly in the prince's favor, until {{amiel-richton|Richton}} called in the dragon {{nahfahlaar|Nafaalilargus}}. As {{a-tor}} ordered his archers to bring the dragon down, an Imperial emissary struck him with a poisoned arrow. Unable to cure the poison, the prince's archmage sealed his soul in a gem and laid the body under a spell that kept it unchanged, before the dragon killed the archmage too. The leaderless fleet fled to the harbor. Most of the soldiers went ashore in the old quarter, and {{amiel-richton|Richton}} had the dragon burn the quarter with everyone inside. Some of the wounded had taken shelter in a temple, where the priests also hid the prince's body. {{amiel-richton|Richton}} let the priests heal them, then had them handed over and executed.

{{hammerfell}} was the last kingdom of men to fall to the {{third-empire}}. {{amiel-richton|Richton}} was made provisional governor of {{stros-mkai}} and hunted down every {{crowns|Crown}} and sympathizer as a traitor. Another Imperial governor moved into the royal palace at {{sentinel}}. The pact with the {{forebears}} was rewritten: Imperial garrisons would stay across the province until its native rulers could safely take back their throne. The day after the battle, {{volag}} vanished into the hills outside {{sentinel}}, and word spread that the {{imperials}} had done away with him.

## Notes

- **Names.** Battle of {{hunding-bay}} is the Imperial name; it is also called the Battle of {{stros-mkai}}.
- **The old quarter.** The {{forebears}} told it differently: the last {{crowns|Crown}} fanatics had set the quarter on fire themselves rather than surrender.
- **The Imperial account.** The *Pocket Guide to the Empire* first appeared to mark this victory. Its opening edition hailed the {{imperials}} as the liberators who had saved the {{redguards}} from a "mad Prince."
