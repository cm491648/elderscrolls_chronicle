By {{era-first|1E}} 2920 the empire had been at war with {{morrowind}} for eighty years. Emperor {{reman-iii}} had come to the throne at fourteen, in the middle of the {{four-score-war}}, and had known no peace in his reign.

In that last year the war turned. His son, Crown Prince {{juilek}}, broke the defences of the {{dunmer}}, and {{vivec}} agreed to a treaty the prince had drawn up. The emperor did not keep it. In the summer he sent his army to seize a fortress on the border while the truce still held. Before the year was out, {{juilek|Juilek}} had been murdered. In the autumn {{reman-iii|Reman}} met {{vivec}} again and agreed to new terms, which they would sign at the palace in the {{imperial-city}} a month later.

In the last month of the year, an assassin of the {{morag-tong}} killed {{reman-iii|Reman}} in the audience chamber of the palace. Two days later his {{potentate}}, the {{akaviri}} counsellor {{versidue-shaie}}, made peace with {{vivec}} on terms drawn from the prince's treaty. The war was over.

{{reman-iii|Reman}} had no heir left. Sixteen days after the murder, the {{potentate}} announced that the line of {{reman}} was ended. He would rule the empire himself but keep the title of {{potentate}}. As his first act he declared that a new age would begin with the new year: the {{era-first}} was over, and the {{era-second}} had begun.

People came to believe that {{versidue-shaie}} had ordered the emperor's death.

## Notes

- **The telling.** Much of the detail of this year comes from *2920, The Last Year of the First Era*, a historical novel.
- **The tomb.** {{reman-iii|Reman}} was buried with the {{amulet-of-kings}} in the tomb of the {{reman}} emperors at {{sancre-tor}}.
- **The prince.** Who arranged {{juilek|Juilek}}'s murder is not recorded; the novel hints that the {{potentate}} and his son were behind it.
