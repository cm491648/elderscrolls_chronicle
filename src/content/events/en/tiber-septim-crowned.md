After four hundred years of the {{interregnum}}, {{cyrodiil}} was a patchwork of feuding states. {{cuhlecain}}, a king in the west, set out to unite it. His general was {{talos}}. Within a year more than half of {{cyrodiil}} was theirs, and {{cuhlecain}} took the {{imperial-city}} and proclaimed himself emperor.

In 852 of the {{era-second}} {{talos}} broke an invading army of {{nords}} and {{bretons}} at the mountain fortress of {{sancre-tor}}, and the {{nords}} he captured swore loyalty to him.

{{cuhlecain}}'s reign was short. In 854 an assassin from the {{western-reach}} entered the palace, killed {{cuhlecain}}, set the palace on fire, and cut {{talos}}'s throat. {{talos}} came out of the burning palace wounded but alive. He took the name {{tiber-septim}} and was crowned emperor, the first of the {{septim-dynasty}}.

Over the next forty years {{tiber-septim|Tiber}} brought all of {{tamriel}} under his rule and founded the {{third-empire}}. After his death he was worshipped as the god {{talos}}.

## Notes

- **Whose account.** The story follows the official Imperial version.
- **Another account.** *The Arcturian Heresy* names the general as {{hjalti}}, a native of {{high-rock}}. It says he had {{cuhlecain}} and his loyalists killed before the king could be crowned, let the {{western-reach}} take the blame, and was crowned by the battlemage {{zurin-arctus}}. In this version the cut throat is a later invention.
- **Birthplace.** Official records say {{tiber-septim|Tiber}} was born in {{atmora}}; the {{bretons}} of {{high-rock}} claim him as one of their own.
