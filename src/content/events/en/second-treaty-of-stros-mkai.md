In 171 of the {{era-fourth}}, days after the {{great-war}} began, an army of the {{aldmeri-dominion}} under {{arannelya}} marched out of {{valenwood}}, crossed western {{cyrodiil}} and entered {{hammerfell}}. Smaller forces landed on the southern coast. The {{redguards}} had long been divided by the feud between the {{crowns}} and the {{forebears}}, and they fought back only here and there. Much of the coast fell quickly. The outnumbered Imperial legions retreated through the {{alikr-desert}} on the {{march-of-thirst}}, and the survivors regrouped in northern {{hammerfell}}.

By the next year the elves held the whole southern coast, and only {{hegathe}} still resisted. Early in 173 a {{forebears|Forebear}} army from {{sentinel}} broke the siege of that {{crowns|Crown}} city, and the two parties began to make up their quarrel. The main elven army crossed the desert and met the legions of General {{decianus}} outside {{skaven}} in a bloody battle that settled nothing. {{decianus}} fell back, and the elves took the city but were too weak to go further.

In 174 {{decianus}} was making ready to drive the elves out of {{skaven}} when he was ordered to march east and defend {{cyrodiil}}. He would not leave {{hammerfell}} with no defenders. Before he left, he discharged a great number of his soldiers as "invalids", and they stayed behind when the legions marched. Late that year these veterans formed the core of an army that turned on {{arannelya}} and drove her forces back across the {{alikr-desert}}. Warriors of the desert struck at the retreating columns again and again, and the elves lost heavily. Meanwhile the legions of {{hammerfell}} reached {{cyrodiil}}, and on the thirtieth of {{rains-hand}}, 175, they came down on the {{imperial-city}} from the west.

That year, after the {{battle-of-the-red-ring}}, Emperor {{titus-mede-ii}} signed the {{white-gold-concordat}}. It gave the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}} a large part of southern {{hammerfell}}, most of it already in elven hands. The {{redguards}} would not admit defeat or give up so much land. To save the peace, the emperor renounced {{hammerfell}} as a province of the Empire, and the {{redguards}} took it as a betrayal.

They fought on alone for five more years. Neither side could break the other, and southern {{hammerfell}} was laid waste. In 180 the two sides made peace in the {{second-treaty-of-stros-mkai}}, and the armies of the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}} withdrew from {{hammerfell}}. The {{redguards}} had kept their land. {{hammerfell}} stood outside the Empire, its south in ruins.

## Notes

- **Was the Concordat needed?** The {{redguards}} say their victory proves the emperor gave in too soon, and that had he held his nerve, {{hammerfell}} and the Empire could have beaten the elves together. The legate who wrote *The Great War* answers that no one can know, and that the Imperial blood spilled at the {{battle-of-the-red-ring|Red Ring}} weakened the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}} enough to make this treaty possible.
- **The "invalids".** The legate puts the word in quotation marks; the men fought well enough to drive an elven army across the desert. He thinks they may also be why the elves did not notice that the legions had left: {{arannelya}} would have believed an Imperial army still faced her.
- **The elves' real aim.** The same legate suspects {{hammerfell}}, not {{cyrodiil}}, was the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}}'s goal from the start, and the march on the {{imperial-city|capital}} was meant to pin the legions down.
- **Keeping watch.** {{fijeh}}, who leads the {{remnants}}, writes in a note that their agents have lived in {{skyrim}} since the elves left {{hammerfell}}, ready in case the {{thalmor}} mean to break the treaty.
- **The name.** The treaty bears the name of the island of {{stros-mkai}}; *The Great War* does not say where it was signed. It is called the second to tell it apart from the {{first-treaty-of-stros-mkai}}, signed by {{tiber-septim}} in 864 of the {{era-second}}.
