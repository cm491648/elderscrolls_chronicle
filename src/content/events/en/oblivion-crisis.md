In 433 of the {{era-third}}, Emperor {{uriel-septim-vii}} and his three sons were murdered within a single night. The killers belonged to the {{mythic-dawn}}, a secret cult that served the {{daedra|Daedric}} Prince {{mehrunes-dagon}}. The emperor was struck down in the tunnels beneath the {{imperial-city}} as he tried to escape. Before he died, he gave the {{amulet-of-kings}} to a prisoner who had been caught up in his flight, and told the prisoner of an illegitimate son.

Without a {{dragonborn}} emperor, no one could light the {{dragonfires}}, and the covenant with {{akatosh}} lapsed. {{oblivion-gate|Oblivion Gates}} began to open. The first great assault fell on {{kvatch}} in western {{cyrodiil}}, and the city was burned. Further gates opened across the province and beyond it.

That son was {{martin-septim}}, a priest of {{akatosh}} at {{kvatch}}. The prisoner found him among the survivors and brought him out of the ruins, and the {{blades}} took him under their protection.

The struggle ended in the {{imperial-city}}, where {{mehrunes-dagon}} came through in person. {{martin-septim|Martin}} shattered the {{amulet-of-kings}} and took the form of an avatar of {{akatosh}}. In that form he drove {{mehrunes-dagon|Dagon}} back into {{oblivion}} and closed the gates. {{martin-septim|Martin}} did not survive. The avatar turned to stone where it stood.

He was the last of the {{septim-dynasty}}. With his death the {{era-third}} ended, and the {{era-fourth}} began.

## Notes

- **Other provinces.** Gates also opened in {{skyrim}} and other provinces. On the {{summerset-isles}} the {{crystal-tower}} was destroyed, and in {{black-marsh}} the {{argonians}} carried the fight back through the gates.
- **The prisoner.** The prisoner was later hailed as the hero of {{kvatch}}, but no name was recorded.
- **Afterward.** With no heir of the {{septim-dynasty|Septim}} blood left, the Empire held together only loosely, and over the following decades several provinces broke away.
