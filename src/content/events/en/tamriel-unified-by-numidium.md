By the last decades of the {{era-second}}, {{tiber-septim}} had brought nearly every province under his rule. {{morrowind}} was held by its living gods, the {{tribunal}}, and he hesitated to fight them. In {{era-second|2E}} 882 the three lost their way to the {{heart-of-lorkhan}}, the source of their power, and chose not to fight either. Under the {{armistice}} they kept their rule at home, and the Empire received {{numidium}}, the brass giant the {{dwemer}} had built long before.

{{numidium}} would not stir without the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}}. The Imperial battlemage {{zurin-arctus}} made a great soul gem, the {{mantella}}, to drive it in the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}}'s place, and the {{totem-of-tiber-septim}}, through which it could be commanded.

The last power still standing against the Empire was the {{aldmeri-dominion}}: the elves of the {{summerset-isles}} and {{valenwood}}, who had never truly bowed to an empire of men. In {{era-second|2E}} 896 the brass giant was woken in {{elsweyr}} and sent against them. The {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}} fell, and with it the last resistance in {{tamriel}}.

For the first time, every province of {{tamriel}} answered to one emperor. At the end of that year {{tiber-septim}} proclaimed the start of the {{era-third}}.

## Notes

- **Whose heart.** Sources disagree on whose life force filled the {{mantella}}: {{zurin-arctus}}'s, the ancient {{nords|Nord}} hero {{wulfharth}}'s, or {{tiber-septim}}'s own.
- **The date.** Some sources say the {{era-second}} ran on until about its thousandth year.
