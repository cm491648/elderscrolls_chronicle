The {{orcs}} built their first {{orsinium}} in the mountains of western {{wrothgar}}. It began as an armed camp and grew, with the work of several clans, into a great stone city. In the tenth century of the {{era-first}}, its king, {{golkarr}}, filled out his army with {{orcs|Orc}} refugees from {{hammerfell}} and set out to take the {{bjoulsae-river}}. He meant to make {{wayrest}}, at the river's mouth, pay for its use. His war parties raided the villages and the wandering {{bretons|Breton}} tribes along its banks, and he praised the raids openly.

The neighboring kingdoms would not accept it, {{daggerfall}} least of all. In 948 of the {{era-first}}, King {{joile}} of {{daggerfall}} wrote to {{gaiden-shinji}}, leader of the {{order-of-diagna}}, a {{yokuda|Yokudan}} warrior order, and proposed a joint attack on {{orsinium}}. The order agreed. The newly named kingdom of {{sentinel}} joined them, and the river tribes lent their aid. In 950 the allied armies laid siege to the city.

The siege dragged on for decades. In its first years {{golkarr}} held out with a horn given him by a shaman of {{wrothgar}}, which called bears and other beasts to the city's defense. Against the shaman's warning he sounded it three times in a single moon, and it cracked. The {{orcs|Orc}} hero {{baloth-bloodtusk}} had at first refused to fight for {{golkarr}}, but he came late with his warband to reinforce the city.

{{joile}} had hired {{reachmen|Reach}} clans for the war, the {{winterborn}} chief among them. Years into the siege, a duel was arranged between {{gaiden-shinji}} and {{baloth-bloodtusk}} to decide the fate of {{orsinium}}. In the middle of it, {{winterborn}} archers under {{joile}}'s general shot both champions down. In 973, with the city still holding, {{joile}} turned on his allies, marched south and overran the {{redguards|Redguard}} garrison at {{bangkorai-pass}}. Six {{redguards|Redguard}} swordswomen met his army there. One by one they fell, until the last of them summoned the {{shehai}}, her spirit sword, cut her way through to {{joile}} and killed him. His army fled back across the pass.

In 980 the allies broke into {{orsinium}}. {{golkarr}} was killed, and the city was overrun and razed to the ground. The {{orcs}} were scattered from the mountains. With the river open, trade along the {{bjoulsae-river}} grew quickly. The river tribes built a city of their own, {{daggerfall}} began a trade in ivory plundered from {{orsinium}}, and {{wayrest}} grew into a rival of {{daggerfall}}.

## Notes

- **Dates.** A few sources place the siege centuries earlier: a loading screen in *Skyrim* dates {{gaiden-shinji}}'s best-known saying to 1E 490, and the epic *The Poison Song*, set around 1E 675, treats the siege as long past. The {{direnni}} also claim that one of their own, in the fifth century, first sacked {{orsinium}}.
- **The king's death.** A legend says {{joile}} seized {{golkarr}} as he prayed to {{malacath|Mauloch}} in his hall, and {{gaiden-shinji}} killed him on the spot. Yet {{gaiden-shinji}} died in the duel years before the city fell.
- **The plan.** It came out later that {{joile}} had arranged the duel himself, meaning to rid himself of both champions; {{gaiden-shinji}} was the warrior most likely to lead the {{redguards}} against the invasion of {{hammerfell}} he already intended. Another account says {{joile}} and his general had also drawn {{baloth-bloodtusk}} into the war by telling him, falsely, that {{bretons|Breton}} soldiers had killed his brother.
- **Afterward.** Some {{bretons|Breton}} writers later defended {{joile}}'s march on {{hammerfell}} as a way to keep his people safe. {{orsinium}} was rebuilt on the same site and razed at least twice more; its ruins, later called {{old-orsinium}}, still stood in 2E 582.
