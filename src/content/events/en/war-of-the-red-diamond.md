When the emperor died in {{era-third|3E}} 120, the {{elder-council}} chose his daughter to succeed him. At the funeral his sister {{potema}}, queen of {{solitude}}, rose before the council and called the girl a bastard of the empress and the captain of the guard. The true heir of the {{septim-dynasty}}, she said, was her own son, {{uriel-iii|Uriel}}. The council ignored her, crowned the girl as {{kintyra-ii}} and banished {{potema}} from court. She went home to {{solitude}} and prepared for war, and all of {{skyrim}} and many nobles of {{high-rock}} and {{morrowind}} stood with her.

The war began in 121 with risings across the north. {{kintyra-ii|Kintyra}} led an army into {{high-rock}} herself. A forged letter in her name drew a second imperial army into an ambush in the mountains; her own army was beaten, and she was imprisoned in a castle in {{high-rock}}, where she was later put to death. With the capital's defenders gone north, {{uriel-iii|Uriel}} marched on the {{imperial-city}}, took it within two weeks and was crowned {{uriel-iii}}.

The dead emperor's brothers would not accept him. {{cephorus-i|Cephorus}}, a king in {{hammerfell}}, raised an army of {{redguards}} in the west, and his brother {{magnus-septim|Magnus Septim}}, a king in {{black-marsh}}, led {{argonians}} north through {{morrowind}} into {{skyrim}}. The death of the young empress turned many to their side. In 127 {{cephorus-i|Cephorus}} defeated {{uriel-iii}} in {{hammerfell}} and took him prisoner. On the way to his trial an angry crowd attacked his escort, and the captive emperor died in the flames. {{cephorus-i|Cephorus}} was proclaimed emperor.

{{potema}} fought on from {{solitude}} with the undead and {{daedra}} for ten more years, until she died of old age while her palace was under siege.

## Notes

- **The name.** The red diamond was the emblem of the {{septim-dynasty}}.
- **A shared name.** {{magnus-septim|Magnus Septim}} was the youngest brother of the dead emperor, {{cephorus-i|Cephorus}} and {{potema}}, and became emperor himself after {{cephorus-i|Cephorus}}. He shares his name with the god {{magnus}}; no source links the two.
- **The empress's death.** Some sources put the execution soon after her capture in 121; others say she was kept alive until 125.
- **The end.** Most histories end the war with {{uriel-iii}}'s death in 127; the fighting around {{solitude}} lasted until 137, and sources differ on when the first attacks began.
