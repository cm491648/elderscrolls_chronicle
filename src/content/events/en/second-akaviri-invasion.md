In the summer of {{era-second|2E}} 572, a fleet carrying the snow demons of {{kamal}}, a land in {{akavir}}, landed just north of {{windhelm}} in {{skyrim}}. Its king, {{ada-soom-dir-kamal}}, led the army himself. The city was taken by surprise and sacked. The queen and her heir died leading its defenders. Her twin sons, {{jorunn}} and {{fildgor}}, arrived with their own companies too late to save the city, but together they drove the invaders out of it.

{{jorunn|Jorunn}} sought the help of the {{greybeards}}, who called up {{wulfharth}}, the Ash-King of old. He then made ready to defend his lands against the next attack. It never came: the invaders passed him by and marched east into {{morrowind}}, where a {{dunmer}} army gave ground slowly before them.

On a beach west of {{ebonheart}}, the {{dunmer}} under {{almalexia}} stopped and held. {{jorunn|Jorunn}} and {{wulfharth}} had followed the invaders out of {{skyrim}}, and they fell on them from behind. The fighting was close, and the {{nords}} were stretched thin, until a phalanx of {{argonians|Argonian}} warriors arrived to reinforce the {{dunmer}}. {{vivec}} raised a great wave from the sea, and the army of {{kamal}} was routed. The beach was afterward called {{vivecs-antlers}}.

Three peoples with centuries of enmity between them had won together. They sealed their alliance with treaties signed at {{ebonheart}}, and the {{ebonheart-pact}} was born. In thanks for their help, the {{dunmer}} freed the {{argonians}} they held as slaves. Soon after the battle, {{jorunn|Jorunn}} won the crown of Eastern {{skyrim}} from his brother in single combat and sent {{fildgor|Fildgor}} into exile.

## Notes

- **The search.** {{ada-soom-dir-kamal|Dir-Kamal}} was said to be looking for someone or something whose name translates roughly as the "Ordained Receptacle".
- **Who called him.** Sources differ on who summoned {{wulfharth}}.
- **The west.** The {{nords}} of Western {{skyrim}}, untouched by the invasion and long at odds with the east, did not join the Pact.
- **Remnants.** Some of the invaders lived through the battle. Long afterward, a band of them took the Ruby Throne of {{cyrodiil}} for a time.
- **The crown.** One account dates the duel for the crown three weeks after the battle; another, three weeks after the summoning of {{wulfharth}}.
