{{solstheim}} was a cold island in the {{sea-of-ghosts}}, north-west of {{vvardenfell}}. At the end of the {{era-third}} the {{nords}} of {{skyrim}} had made it part of their province, though an Imperial garrison still held {{fort-frostmoth}} in the south and the {{east-empire-company}} worked an ebony mine at its colony of {{raven-rock}}.

In 1 of the {{era-fourth}}, after {{aldruhn}} had been destroyed, {{brara-morvayn}}, a councillor of {{house-redoran}}, struck a bargain with the Company and brought {{dunmer}} settlers to {{raven-rock}}. They proved hard-working neighbours. When {{red-mountain}} erupted in 5, ash storms swept over the south of the island and {{fort-frostmoth}} was destroyed. Guards of {{house-redoran}} took over keeping the peace. Within a few years the ash had turned the south of the island into a wasteland, and {{brara-morvayn|Brara}} designed a great wall, paid for by the Company, to keep the town from being buried.

Refugees from {{morrowind}} kept coming. In 16 the High King of {{skyrim}} handed {{solstheim}} over to the {{dunmer}}. {{raven-rock}} became a holding of {{house-redoran}}, its Imperial residents went home to {{cyrodiil}}, and the {{east-empire-company}} soon left the island for good.

## Notes

- **Afterwards.** {{brara-morvayn|Brara}} ruled {{raven-rock}} until her death in 65, and her son succeeded her as councillor.
- **The source.** Most of what is known comes from *History of Raven Rock*, a history written in the town.
