After his release from prison, {{ulfric-stormcloak}} became Jarl of {{windhelm}}. His {{stormcloaks}} clashed with Imperial troops now and then but won little support. Meanwhile the {{thalmor}}, free to act in {{skyrim}} since the arrest at {{markarth}}, hunted down those who still worshipped {{talos}}, and resentment grew.

When High King {{istlod}} died after nearly twenty-five years, the jarls met at the {{moot}} to choose his successor. {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} spoke there of independence in words just short of treason. The {{moot}} chose {{istlod}}'s son {{torygg}}, who held his court at {{solitude}}.

Early in 201 of the {{era-fourth}} {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} came to {{solitude}} and asked for an audience. The court expected him to ask the High King to break with the Empire. Instead he challenged {{torygg}} to single combat by the old ways of the {{nords}}. Had {{torygg}} refused, {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} could have called a new {{moot}}, so he accepted, though he knew he could not win. {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} struck him down with the {{thuum|Voice}} and fled the city through a gate a guard opened for him.

{{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} said he had done it to show that {{torygg}} was a weak puppet of the Empire. The Empire called it murder and named him a traitor. {{torygg}}'s widow, {{elisif}}, became Jarl of {{solitude}} and the Empire's choice for High Queen. The western holds of {{skyrim}} stood with the Empire, the eastern holds with {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}}, and {{whiterun}} kept out of it. The civil war had begun. General {{tullius}} came north from {{cyrodiil}} with a handful of legions to hunt him down.

## Notes

- **The death.** {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} says he shouted {{torygg}} to the ground and ran him through; others at court say the Shout tore him apart.
- **Lawful or not.** To {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}}'s followers it was an honourable duel by the old ways; to the Empire and to {{elisif}} it was murder.
- **The High King's mind.** The court wizard who helped raise {{torygg}} believed he might have declared independence had he been asked.
- **The outcome.** How the war ended is not recorded.
