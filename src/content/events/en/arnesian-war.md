Since the days of the {{chimer}}, the {{dunmer}} of {{morrowind}} had taken {{argonians}} from {{black-marsh}} as slaves. {{house-dres}}, whose lands lay in the south of {{morrowind}}, stood at the heart of the trade. Slavery was forbidden elsewhere in the Empire, but under the {{armistice}} by which {{morrowind}} had joined it, the {{dunmer}} were allowed to keep their old customs. The Empire itself held little of {{black-marsh}} beyond its coasts and borders; the heart of the marsh went its own way. Between the two peoples there was a long and bitter hatred. As the fourth century of the {{era-third}} drew to its close, the Empire lost its grip on the provinces, and they began to make war on one another.

In 396 {{argonians|Argonian}} slaves rose up in the lands of {{house-dres}}. At first it was a slave revolt and nothing more. The rebels captured {{roris|Roris}}, a {{dunmer|Dunmer}} merchant, and demanded that he renounce his faith. He refused. They tortured him and put him to death.

When word of his death reached the rest of {{morrowind}}, the {{dunmer}} declared war on {{black-marsh}}, and a slave revolt became a war between two provinces. The armies of {{morrowind}} defeated the {{argonians}}, and the {{dunmer}} took a wide stretch of their land, pushing the border south into {{arnesia}}, in the north of the marsh.

The {{tribunal-temple}} named {{roris}} a saint, one who had died rather than give up his faith. Once peace was made, a commission was set up to repair the damage the war had done.

## Notes

- **The date.** *The Third Era Timeline* puts the war in 396. The third edition of the *Pocket Guide to the Empire* counts the uprising in {{mournhold}}, in which the queen's consort was killed, as part of it, but that uprising came in 391, five years earlier.
- **Disputed land.** {{dunmer|Dunmer}} rule had reached into the north of the marsh before: in the middle of the {{era-second}}, {{stormhold}} was in {{dunmer|Dunmer}} hands. Yet a {{tribunal-temple|Temple}} report prepared for {{vivec}} says that {{dagoth-ur}} meant to recover "ancient territories stolen by {{skyrim}} and {{black-marsh|Argonia}}", as if the marsh still held land the {{dunmer}} counted as their own.
- **Afterward.** After the war the {{argonians}} slowly began to win back the land they had lost. When {{morrowind}} later abolished slavery, relations between the two peoples improved a great deal, but fighting over the disputed land went on.
