South of {{tamriel}}, behind a wall of mist, lay the island realm of {{pyandonea}}, home of the {{maormer}}, the sea elves. Their king was the sorcerer {{orgnum}}, who had ruled them since ancient times and seemed to grow younger with every century. The {{maormer}} held that the {{summerset-isles}} were rightfully theirs. Through the {{era-first}} and the {{era-second}} there was hardly a year in which they did not raid the coasts of the {{altmer}}. {{orgnum}} led every attack himself, and every one failed.

In 110 of the {{era-third}}, twelve years into the reign of Emperor {{antiochus}}, {{orgnum}} sailed against the isles again. This time the kings of {{summerset-isles|Summerset}} made common cause with the Emperor, and the Imperial fleet fought beside their royal navies. Even together they could not stand against the {{maormer}} ships, and {{orgnum}} came closer than ever to taking the isles.

Then, with his victory in sight, a sudden and unnatural storm rose off the coast of {{artaeum}}, the island of the {{psijic-order}}, and destroyed his fleet. The storm came to be credited to the magic of the {{psijic-order|Psijics}}. In the wreck {{orgnum}} also lost a small magic chest that had made gold for him without end, and it later washed up somewhere in the {{summerset-isles}}.

The war was counted the greatest victory of {{antiochus}}'s reign. One of its heroes, Sir {{amiel-lannus}}, founded the {{knights-of-the-nine}} in 111, an order of knights sworn to recover the lost relics of the crusader {{pelinal-whitestrake}}, with its seat at a priory in {{cyrodiil}}.

## Notes

- **The novel.** *The Wolf Queen*, a historical novel, sets a secret meeting in {{solitude}} three months before the war. There {{potema}}, the Emperor's sister, agrees with {{orgnum}} that if the {{maormer}} conquer {{tamriel}} she will be its empress, and promises to rise against her brother. The book also has her meet the {{psijic-order|Psijic}} Ritemaster {{iachesis}}, though he had died in 582 of the {{era-second}}.
- **The storm.** *Brief History of the Empire* says only that the storm came without warning and was later credited to the {{psijic-order|Psijics}}. *The Wolf Queen* counts the {{psijic-order|Psijics}}' magic among the allied forces that destroyed the fleet.
- **Origins.** {{orgnum}} was remembered as a wealthy {{aldmer}} noble who led a rebellion in {{aldmeris}} and was banished with his followers to {{pyandonea}}. Tapestries in the {{crystal-tower}} suggest that the {{maormer}} split from the other elves in {{aldmeris}} itself, and were not exiled from {{summerset-isles|Summerset}} as once believed.
- **Afterward.** The storm was so violent that {{orgnum}} never again gathered enough strength to dare another battle, and some came to believe that without his chest he could no longer pay for so great a fleet. No further attack by the {{maormer}} was recorded: by the end of the {{era-third}} the war was still their last known appearance in {{tamriel}}, and no word came from {{pyandonea}} to say whether his power had truly been broken.
