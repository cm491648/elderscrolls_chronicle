In 582 of the {{era-second}}, the former steward of Emperor {{leovic}} sent {{eveli-sharp-arrow}} after a book. {{leovic}}, killed five years before, had left a secret: a bargain with {{mehrunes-dagon}} over something called the {{four-ambitions}}. Soon afterwards the steward was murdered, and the killing was laid on the {{dark-brotherhood}}. {{eveli-sharp-arrow|Eveli}} and an adventurer followed a cult, the {{order-of-the-waking-flame}}, into the {{deadlands}}, the realm of {{mehrunes-dagon|Dagon}}. There they heard the Prince tell a cultist sent by the order's High Priest that the {{four-ambitions|Ambitions}} had been lost and the bargain was still unpaid.

Several former councilors of the {{longhouse-emperors}} lived in {{blackwood}}, on the border of {{cyrodiil}} and {{black-marsh}}, and the two went to warn them. The adventurer was sent to {{gideon}} to warn Councilor {{ertus-vandacia}}, once the city's governor. A {{dark-brotherhood|Brotherhood}} assassin caught in the city escaped, and when the adventurer ran him down he swore he had killed no one. In time it came out that {{ertus-vandacia|Vandacia}} was himself the High Priest. His cult had killed the councilors and blamed the {{dark-brotherhood|Brotherhood}}, and he meant to take the bargain's power, the throne and the {{four-ambitions|Ambitions}} for himself.

The bargain went back to {{durcorach}}, a {{reachmen|Reachman}} chief who had seized the throne of {{cyrodiil}} with {{mehrunes-dagon|Dagon}}'s help; {{ertus-vandacia|Vandacia}} had backed him. In return his line would prepare the {{four-ambitions|Ambitions}}, and after fifty years {{mehrunes-dagon|Dagon}} would claim his due. {{leovic}}'s death in 577 had left the work unfinished. {{eveli-sharp-arrow|Eveli}} and the adventurer learned that the {{four-ambitions|Ambitions}} were not weapons but people, filled at birth with {{daedra|Daedric}} power that grew with them. One day they were to be sacrificed, and their power would join {{nirn}} to the {{deadlands}}. From deep in the swamps the two brought the twins {{calia}} and {{destron}} to {{gideon}}, and later a third.

{{ertus-vandacia|Vandacia}} learned where they were and gathered his followers around {{gideon}}. The three were moved northwest to {{fort-redmane}}, an Imperial-built fort held by the city's {{argonians|Argonian}} legion and its allies. Most of the cult's army was beaten outside the walls, but {{ertus-vandacia|Vandacia}} and his strongest followers came in through portals. He sacrificed {{destron}}, took his power, and began to merge {{nirn}} with the {{deadlands}}, and {{mehrunes-dagon}} himself joined the assault. {{calia}} drained the {{daedra|Daedric}} power out of {{ertus-vandacia|Vandacia}}, and he was killed. The merging stopped, and {{mehrunes-dagon|Dagon}} was driven back.

The two surviving {{four-ambitions|Ambitions}} could not yet control their power, and went into the {{deadlands}}, where it was held in check. The adventurer set out to find the fourth, and {{gideon}} celebrated the victory.

## Notes

- **What they carried.** Only later did it become clear that the power inside the four was a part of {{mehrunes-dagon|Dagon}}'s own self, split among them by the bargain.
- **The creed.** The {{order-of-the-waking-flame|Waking Flame}} held that the world was flawed and full of suffering, and looked to {{mehrunes-dagon|Dagon}} to destroy it so that it could be made anew.
- **Afterward.** {{mehrunes-dagon|Dagon}} next tried to seize {{fargrave}}, a realm that served as a crossing between {{nirn}} and the rest of {{oblivion}}, and the cult's plan there failed as well. Later one of the surviving {{four-ambitions|Ambitions}} brought him into {{tamriel}} itself, and the fourth turned the power within them against him and cast him to the far edge of the {{deadlands}}.
