{{betony}} was a small fishing island off {{daggerfall}}, where the {{iliac-bay}} opens to the sea. Few people lived there, but whoever held it held a key to the bay. In 402 of the {{era-third}}, pirates from {{balfiera}} were raiding it again and again, and its lord placed himself under King {{lysandus}} of {{daggerfall}} in return for protection. King {{camaron}} of {{sentinel}}, across the bay in {{hammerfell}}, protested: a contract two hundred years old named {{betony}} a holding of {{sentinel}}. {{daggerfall}} would not give way, and {{camaron}} declared war.

{{daggerfall}} won every major battle. Its knights broke the {{sentinel}} fleet off the coast of {{daggerfall}} and later broke {{sentinel}}'s siege of an island garrison. At court, the king's mother {{nulfaga}}, a sorceress, foresaw that her son would die if the war went on. She begged him to make peace. When he would not listen, she left for her castle in the mountains. Her leaving moved {{lysandus}} at last, and he sought peace.

The two kings met at {{reich-gradkeep}}, a neutral {{bretons|Breton}} fief. The talks went well, and the two sides came to terms. But the treaty set down for them to sign did not say what had been agreed. When this came out, the hall erupted. The fighting spread from the palace into the town, and many of its people were slaughtered. The armies drew apart and made camp on either side of {{cryngaine-field}}.

A week later, in 403, the reinforced armies met on the field. As the battle began, a strange fog came down over both sides. When it lifted, {{lysandus}} was found dead with an arrow in his throat. His son {{gothryd}} was crowned in the field, close behind the lines. Seeing their new king in full regalia, the {{daggerfall}} army pressed on and carried the day. {{camaron}} was killed before the battle ended, and {{sentinel}}'s commander surrendered. {{sentinel}} gave up every claim to {{betony}}. The peace was sealed by the marriage of {{gothryd}} to {{camaron}}'s daughter, Princess {{aubk-i}}. In {{sentinel}}, {{camaron}}'s widow ruled as regent until their youngest son came of age.

{{daggerfall}} came out of the war the strongest kingdom on the bay, and {{gothryd}} was cheered as its victor. A monument to {{lysandus}} was raised on {{cryngaine-field}}. In 404 the dead king returned. The ghost of {{lysandus}} walked the streets of {{daggerfall}} by night at the head of a host of ghosts, crying for vengeance.

## Notes

- **Two histories.** The two main accounts are both called *The War of Betony*. One is the victors' official history; the other was written by a citizen of {{sentinel}}, who claimed that {{lysandus}} had paid the pirates himself so as to seize the island.
- **The false treaty.** The two accounts blame opposite sides. The {{sentinel}} account says a {{daggerfall}} scribe miswrote the treaty on purpose. The official history says {{camaron}} flew into a rage at a clause sharing the island and set his men on the town.
- **The other king.** The official history says {{gothryd}} cut {{camaron}} down in battle. The {{sentinel}} account says {{gothryd}} challenged the exhausted king to a duel, then stabbed him from behind before it could begin.
- **Later findings.** How {{lysandus}} really died came to light only years later, in the {{warp-in-the-west}}. Other records place the war in 403 or 405.
