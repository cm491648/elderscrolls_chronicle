{{alduin}}, the eldest of the dragons, called himself the firstborn of {{akatosh}}. In the {{era-merethic}} he ruled over men through the {{dragon-cult}}, until the {{dragon-war}} ended with three {{nords|Nord}} heroes casting him out of time from the summit of the {{throat-of-the-world}}. His brother {{paarthurnax}} stayed on the mountain.

In the ages that followed, the {{nords}} still feared {{alduin}} as a god. His worship was forbidden, and those who kept it did so in secret. At the old temples of the {{dragon-cult}}, offerings were made on midwinter nights so that he would go on sleeping.

On the 17th of {{last-seed}}, 201 of the {{era-fourth}}, Imperial soldiers in the village of {{helgen}} were beheading captured {{stormcloaks}}, among them their leader, {{ulfric-stormcloak}}. Before the executions were done, a black dragon came down and burned the village. {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} escaped in the confusion.

Soon dragons were seen across {{skyrim}}. The black dragon went from one burial mound to the next, calling dead dragons back to life, and in the old tombs the dead began to stir.

Only later was it known that the black dragon was {{alduin}}. He had come back out of time on the same summit where the heroes had banished him.

## Notes

- **His nature.** The {{nords}} hold that {{alduin}}'s part is to devour the world at the close of an age. Some myths treat him as an aspect of {{akatosh}} rather than his son.
- **The war.** {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}}'s escape kept the civil war in {{skyrim}} going.
- **Other entries.** A prisoner caught crossing the border was also among the condemned at {{helgen}} and escaped in the attack. That prisoner's story is told in a separate entry.
