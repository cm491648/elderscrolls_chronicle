Before {{eplear}}, the {{bosmer}} of {{valenwood}} lived as scattered, untamed clans of the forest.

{{eplear}} brought them together under one king. For the first time, {{valenwood}} was a realm that others had to reckon with.

To the north, in the heart of {{tamriel}}, the {{ayleid}} were at war among themselves: the clans that kept to the {{aedra}} against those that had turned to the {{daedra}}. {{eplear}} was remembered as a generous ruler. He took in refugees from the troubled {{ayleid}} lands, and from elsewhere besides, and let them live among the {{bosmer}}.

He founded a royal house, the {{camoran-dynasty}}. The year of its founding came to be reckoned the year 0 of the {{era-first}}, the earliest date in the recorded history of {{tamriel}}.

His heirs held the throne for centuries and kept {{valenwood}} free of the empire that rose in {{cyrodiil}}, until the {{second-empire}} conquered it in 2714 of the {{era-first}}. In the {{era-third}} a quarrel over the succession produced the {{camoran-usurper}}, and the founder of the {{mythic-dawn}} came of the same line.

## Notes

- **The acorns.** An {{akaviri}} dossier tells that {{eplear}} was an ox-herd when the land was still a sea of grass. To win the hand of a merchant named Camoran, he planted her acorns wherever his oxen grazed until the plains became forest, and the grateful {{spriggans}} crowned him. The {{bosmer}} hold this story to be pure invention.
- **The feat.** How {{eplear}} united the clans is not recorded; later writers counted it among the greatest feats of arms in the history of {{tamriel}}.
