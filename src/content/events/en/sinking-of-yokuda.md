{{yokuda}} was the great continent west of {{tamriel}}, homeland of a people of artisans, mystics and swordsmen. It had seldom known a long peace. Provincial lords, warrior monks and brigands fought one another for land, and civil war followed civil war.

In the last of these wars, a band of {{sword-singers}} called the {{hiradirge}} rose against the rest and was defeated. The {{hiradirge}} were masters of stone magic. In defeat, they took their revenge on the whole land with a forbidden art.

About twelve years after {{frandar-hunding}} and his army had sailed away, {{yokuda}} sank. Most of the continent went down beneath the sea; a few islands were left above the water.

Those who could fled east by ship, from the continent and from the islands, among them the ruling class of {{yokuda}}. The greater part of the refugees came ashore on the island of {{herne}}, off the western coast of {{tamriel}}. The warriors among them prepared to sail on to the mainland.

## Notes

- **The cause.** Sources disagree whether {{yokuda}} sank by earthquake, flood or fire from the earth, by the deeds of its people, or by both. The story above follows the legend of the {{hiradirge}}.
- **The stroke.** One account names the forbidden sword stroke the Pankratosword, a blow that could break the laws of nature.
- **Another telling.** A {{redguards|Redguard}} account speaks of a "Time of Ending", when the people turned from their gods and called up things that should never have been summoned; {{satakal}} rose from the sea and pulled {{yokuda}} under.
- **What remained.** Some islands survived, and later maps still show them. In the {{era-second}}, followers of {{frandar-hunding}} were said to live on in the mines of {{mount-hattu}}.
- **Date.** About {{era-first|1E}} 792.
