The {{thalmor}} began as a movement among the {{altmer}} of the {{summerset-isles}} who held that the elves were the rightful masters of the world. For years few took them seriously. Then, during the {{oblivion-crisis}}, {{daedra}} overran the islands and threw down the {{crystal-tower}}. The {{thalmor}} were given wider powers to meet the danger, and when it had passed, many came to see them as the saviours of the isles.

The Empire could do nothing for its province. Its last emperor was dead, and the {{elder-council}} was fighting over the empty throne. In 22 of the {{era-fourth}} the {{thalmor}} overthrew the ancient monarchy of the islands and took power for themselves. They renamed the province {{alinor}}, after its capital.

Seven years later they reached across the sea. In 29 {{bosmer}} allied with the {{thalmor}} rose in {{valenwood}} and overthrew the government that answered to the Empire. The Imperial garrisons and the {{bosmer}} who stood with them were taken by surprise and beaten by better-prepared {{bosmer}} and {{thalmor}} troops. {{alinor}} and {{valenwood}} then proclaimed their union, and the {{aldmeri-dominion}} was born again.

The new Dominion cut off all contact with the Empire. For seventy years almost no word came out of it.

## Notes

- **The saviours.** The {{thalmor}} presented themselves as the islands' saviours; how much of the victory was really theirs is not clear.
- **The silence.** Imperial scholars believe the seventy years of silence hid unrest inside {{alinor}}, but no one outside could tell.
- **The count.** It is usually called the Third {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}}; some historians of the elves argue that it is really the fourth.
- **The invasion.** One account holds that the {{thalmor}} also stirred up the {{argonians}} to invade {{morrowind}} in 6.
