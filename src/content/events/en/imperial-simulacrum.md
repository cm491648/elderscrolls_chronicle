{{jagar-tharn}}, Imperial Battlemage to {{uriel-septim-vii}}, had laid his plans long before. Years earlier he had arranged the theft of the {{staff-of-chaos}} from its sanctuary beneath {{mournhold}}. In {{era-third|3E}} 389 he asked for leave from court to pursue his studies, and named his apprentice {{ria-silmane}} to act in his place. Later that year he asked the Emperor to meet him about rumours of treason. When {{uriel-septim-vii|Uriel}} came, {{jagar-tharn|Tharn}} used the {{staff-of-chaos|Staff}} to cast him and the general of his guard into a pocket of {{oblivion}} of his own making. He did not kill him, because the {{amulet-of-kings}} would have told the {{elder-council}} of the Emperor's death.

When {{ria-silmane}} learned what he had done, he killed her. {{jagar-tharn|Tharn}} then took on the Emperor's face with illusion magic and sat on the throne. He dismissed the Emperor's inner circle, replaced the Imperial guards with fire daemons disguised as men, and sent the Emperor's wife and children away. He broke the {{staff-of-chaos|Staff}} into eight pieces and hid them across {{tamriel}}.

For ten years the false emperor ruled, and the provinces fell to fighting among themselves. But {{ria-silmane|Silmane}}'s spirit lingered. She came in dreams to a prisoner in the palace dungeons, showed the way out, and set the prisoner searching for the eight pieces. One by one they were found. In 399 the prisoner fought through the palace, defeated {{jagar-tharn|Tharn}}, and used the {{staff-of-chaos|Staff}} to drain the jewel that held his life. He was destroyed, and {{uriel-septim-vii|Uriel}} came back from {{oblivion}} to his throne.

The Emperor named his rescuer the {{eternal-champion}}.

## Notes

- **The theft.** One account says {{jagar-tharn|Tharn}} himself, in the guise of a bard, stole the {{staff-of-chaos|Staff}} after winning the confidence of the queen of {{mournhold}}; another says he hired a master thief.
- **The wars.** During the ten years, {{elsweyr}} fought {{valenwood}}, {{morrowind}} fought {{black-marsh}}, and the {{summerset-isles}} fought {{valenwood}}.
- **After.** The restored Emperor worked to reunite the Empire by subtler, less open means, a change he said he owed to his revulsion at the counsel of his old battlemage.
