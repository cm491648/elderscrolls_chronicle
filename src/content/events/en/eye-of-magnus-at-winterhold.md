In 201 of the {{era-fourth}}, mages of the {{college-of-winterhold}} were excavating {{saarthal}}, the ruined first city of the {{nords}}, not far from {{winterhold}}. The townsfolk disliked seeing the mages dig among the dead. The teacher {{tolfdir}} led a group of new students to the site, among them one who had only just been admitted. Deep in the ruins a hidden way opened, and a monk of the {{psijic-order}} appeared to the student in a vision, warning that what the {{college-of-winterhold|College}} had found would bring it danger. In the lowest chamber lay a great sealed orb, marked with ancient script. The undead warlock sealed in the chamber with it rose and attacked them. When he was destroyed, the orb was carried back to the {{college-of-winterhold|College}}.

The {{college-of-winterhold|College}} already had a guest it did not trust. {{ancano}}, a {{altmer|High Elf}} of the {{thalmor}}, had been sent to the {{arch-mage}} {{savos-aren}} as an advisor. He denied being a spy and said the {{thalmor}} only wanted good relations with the {{college-of-winterhold|College}}, but few believed him. He made no secret of his hatred for the {{psijic-order|Psijics}}, whom he counted as enemies of the {{thalmor}}.

Soon a monk of the {{psijic-order|Order}} came to the {{college-of-winterhold|College}}, spoke of the orb as the {{eye-of-magnus}} and warned of its danger, and sent the student to the {{augur-of-dunlain}}, a mage of the {{college-of-winterhold|College}} who had become a being of pure magic and dwelt in the cellars beneath it. The {{augur-of-dunlain|Augur}} said that to deal with the {{eye-of-magnus|Eye}}, the student would need the {{staff-of-magnus}}. It also emerged that {{ancano}} had been coming to the {{augur-of-dunlain|Augur}} in secret, asking about the same thing.

Then {{ancano}} turned on the {{college-of-winterhold|College}}. He seized the {{eye-of-magnus|Eye}} and shut himself in with it behind a barrier in the great hall. Surges of its power spilled out, and creatures of raw magic roamed the streets of {{winterhold}}. Several senior mages were killed, the {{arch-mage}} {{savos-aren}} among them.

The student brought the {{staff-of-magnus|staff}} back from the ruins of {{labyrinthian}}, turned it against the power of the {{eye-of-magnus|Eye}}, and killed {{ancano}}. Monks of the {{psijic-order|Order}} then arrived and took the {{eye-of-magnus|Eye}} away, saying it was too unstable to remain in the world. With {{savos-aren|Savos}} dead, the student became the new {{arch-mage}}.

## Notes

- **Under {{saarthal}}.** The book *Night of Tears* suggests that the first {{nords}} dug up a great power while building {{saarthal}}, and that the {{falmer}} attacked the city on the {{night-of-tears}} to take it. The book does not name that power; tying it to the {{eye-of-magnus|Eye}} is a later guess.
- **What it was.** The {{eye-of-magnus|Eye}} bears the same script as the {{elder-scroll|Elder Scrolls}}. Its name ties it to {{magnus}}, the god of magic, whose staff could bring it under control.
- **Afterward.** {{ancano}}'s meddling left rifts open across {{skyrim}}, and creatures of magic came through them. The {{college-of-winterhold|College}} set out to close them with the {{staff-of-magnus|staff}}.
