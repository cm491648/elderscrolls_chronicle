Before the {{mages-guild}}, the study of magic belonged to small circles that kept to themselves. The oldest was the {{psijic-order}} on the isle of {{artaeum}}, whose isolation and old ways made people trust it less with every age.

{{vanus-galerion}} had been brought to {{artaeum}} as a boy and trained there under the master {{iachesis}}. He disagreed with the {{psijic-order|Psijics}} on many things, and in time he left the island with a plan of his own: magic should serve anyone who could pay for it. In the city of {{firsthold}} he began gathering mages, sorcerers and mystics who would pool what they had, share their research and do charitable work. Potions, enchanted items and even spells would be sold to the public, no longer kept for nobles and scholars.

Few liked the idea. The people of {{firsthold}} felt that magical experiments belonged far from any town, and they called the venture "{{vanus-galerion|Galerion}}'s Folly". They urged their king, {{rilis-xii}}, to put an end to it.

In {{era-second|2E}} 230, less than a year after leaving the {{psijic-order|Psijics}}, {{vanus-galerion|Galerion}} was called before the king, {{iachesis}}, the nobles and members of the {{psijic-order}} to explain what he meant to do. The charter was approved, and the king himself joined the venture as a co-founder.

{{vanus-galerion|Galerion}} gave the head of the new guild the title {{arch-mage}}, in memory of {{shalidor}}, the first mage known to have claimed it, and he soon raised an order of knights to protect it. The guild was a great success, and {{vanus-galerion|Galerion}} became famous for his wisdom.

## Notes

- **The rival.** {{vanus-galerion|Galerion}}'s fellow student {{mannimarco}} had been banished from {{artaeum}} for necromancy. The new guild condemned necromancy except in rare cases under strict control, and the two remained enemies.
- **The isle.** {{artaeum}} vanished from the world soon after {{vanus-galerion|Galerion}} left.
- **The speech.** What {{vanus-galerion|Galerion}} said before the king was not written down.
- **The law.** The {{guild-act}} of {{era-second|2E}} 321 recognized the guild across the empire.
- **The king.** {{rilis-xii}} was later known for pacts with {{daedra}} and for his cruelty, and the guild worked to dispel the rumour that he had acted for it.
- **The date.** The guild's own histories give the founding as around 2E 230.
