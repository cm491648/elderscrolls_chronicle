In the fifth century of the {{era-first}}, the faith of the {{alessian-order}} had spread into {{skyrim}}, and its priests kept halls among the {{nords}}. In 482 the Order's army was broken on the moors of {{glenumbra}}. {{hoag-merkiller}}, High King of {{skyrim}}, had joined the war against it, and he fell in the battle.

The chieftains then chose {{wulfharth}}, a man from {{atmora}}, to be High King. His {{thuum}} was so strong that he could not speak his oath aloud, so scribes wrote it out for him. His first law brought back the old gods of the {{nords}}. The {{alessian-order|Alessian}} edicts were outlawed, their priests were burned at the stake, and their halls were set on fire. The {{nords}} called him {{shor|Shor}}'s Tongue, and {{ysmir}}, which means the Dragon of the North.

He fought the {{orcs}} of the east and shouted their chief into Hell. When a dragon broke the four hundred and eighteenth step to {{high-hrothgar}}, he made it whole again. He swallowed a thundercloud to keep his army from the cold, and for that he was called the Breath of {{kyne}}.

{{orkey}}, the god of the {{orcs}} and {{wulfharth|Wulfharth}}'s old foe, had stolen the years of the {{nords}} before. Once more he called up {{alduin}} the Time-Eater, who ate away their years until nearly all of them were six years old again, and {{wulfharth|Wulfharth}} himself became a boy. The boy-king called on {{shor}}, chief of the gods, and {{shor}} fought the Time-Eater in the sky and won. Watching that battle, {{wulfharth|Wulfharth}} learned a new Shout, and with it he gave his people back their years. But in his haste to save them all he shook too many years onto himself. He grew older than the {{greybeards}}, and soon he died. The songs say the flames of his pyre rose to the hearth of {{kyne}} herself.

## Notes

- **The songs.** His deeds and his death are told in the *Five Songs of King Wulfharth*, old songs of the {{nords}}.
- **Ghosts.** The song calls both {{alduin}}, whom {{orkey}} called up, and {{shor}}, who fought him, "ghosts" without explaining why, so the story above does not take the word literally. {{alduin}} was not dead: at the end of the {{dragon-war}} the ancient {{nords}} had cast him forward in time with a Shout, and he did not return until the {{era-fourth}}.
- **Dates.** A plaque in {{windhelm}} gives his reign as 480 to 533. The memoir of the last {{ayleid}} king puts the battle that cost {{hoag-merkiller|Hoag}} his life in 482.
- **The elves.** The same plaque says he defeated the {{direnni}}. Another history calls {{wulfharth}} {{hoag-merkiller|Hoag}}'s heir, who carried on the war against the Order, and has the {{nords}} fighting on the elves' side.
- **After death.** Nordic songs say {{shor}} raised him from his ashes as the Ash-King to lead the {{nords}} at {{red-mountain}}. In the {{era-second}} the {{nords}} called him back to fight the {{akaviri}}, and one heretical text holds that he became the {{underking}}.
