Between 240 and 272 of the {{era-first}}, the {{nords}} of {{skyrim}} conquered the land of the {{chimer}} in the northeast, using their armies and the power of the Voice. Only the {{dwemer}}, safe in their underground cities, kept their independence. The {{nords|Nordic}} occupation lasted more than a century.

In 369 the High King, the last of {{ysgramor}}'s line, was killed, and the {{nords}} fell to fighting over the succession. The war crippled their empire.

While the {{nords}} fought among themselves, the {{chimer}} general {{nerevar}} brought the quarrelling {{chimer}} factions together and became their war leader. Then he made an alliance with {{dumac}}, king of the {{dwemer}}. The two peoples had despised each other and fought over land and faith for generations, and the pact rested on the friendship between the two men, who were blood friends.

In 416 their combined armies beat the {{nords}} at {{red-mountain}} and drove them out of the land. The victors named the country {{resdayn}} and agreed to rule it together through the {{first-council}}, with {{nerevar}} and {{dumac}} at its head.

Not all the {{dwemer}} accepted the alliance. The {{rourken}} clan chose exile. Its chieftain threw the clan's great hammer, {{volendrung}}, westward across {{tamriel}} and led his people to wherever it fell. There they settled and named their new land {{volenfell}}. It was later called {{hammerfell}}.

In {{resdayn}}, the two peoples lived in peace for centuries, and both prospered.

## Notes

- **Dates.** Some sources stretch the {{nords|Nordic}} conquest from 240 to 415. One account of the {{nords|Nordic}} civil war says {{skyrim}} had already lost its eastern lands by 401, while the main chronicles place the expulsion in 416. The exile of the {{rourken}} clan is likewise dated to either 416 or 420.
- **The High King's death.** He was killed while planning a war against {{valenwood}} together with the {{alessian-order}}; the elves of {{valenwood}} called up the Wild Hunt against him.
- **The north.** The defeat in the east led a master of the Voice to decide that the {{nords}} had lost the gods' favor and that the Voice must be used only in worship. His followers became the {{greybeards}}.
- **The name.** The land was later called {{morrowind}}.
