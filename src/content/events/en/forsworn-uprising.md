{{the-reach}}, the mountainous western hold of {{skyrim}}, had been the home of the {{reachmen}} long before the {{nords}} ruled it. For centuries they had bowed to {{nords|Nord}} jarls without ever accepting that the land was not theirs.

In 174 of the {{era-fourth}}, with the {{great-war}} going badly, the Empire left its outer lands to fend for themselves, and the Legion was withdrawn from {{the-reach|the Reach}}. {{madanach}} led the {{reachmen}} in revolt. They overthrew Jarl {{hrolfdir}} and took {{markarth}}, the old {{dwemer|Dwarven}} city cut into the mountain, and with it the whole of {{the-reach|the Reach}}. They declared it an independent kingdom.

For two years the kingdom held. It ruled its {{nords|Nord}} and {{reachmen|Reachman}} subjects peacefully, putting to death only a few of the harshest {{nords|Nord}} landowners, and its leaders began to seek the Empire's recognition as a province of their own. The deposed Jarl and his son, meanwhile, looked for a way to take the city back.

## Notes

- **The rule.** The peaceful picture comes from an Imperial scholar's book, *The Bear of Markarth*; {{hrolfdir}}'s son, later Jarl of {{markarth}}, flatly contradicts some of its claims.
- **The taking.** Accounts differ on how the city fell: some describe a largely peaceful changeover, others say the {{reachmen}} broke through the gates.
- **The name.** The revolt is known as the {{forsworn}} Uprising, after the name the {{reachmen}} who fought on later took.
