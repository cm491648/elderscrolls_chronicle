In 573 of the {{era-second}}, Princess {{ayrenn}} of the {{summerset-isles}} disappeared on the day she was to begin her studies at the {{crystal-tower}}, and her younger brother {{naemon}} was named heir. In 580 she returned and was crowned queen as the eldest heir. The same year she bound the {{altmer}} of the isles to the {{bosmer}} and the {{khajiit}} in the {{aldmeri-dominion}}. Many {{altmer}} resented the new allies. A secret society, the {{veiled-heritance}}, drew them together under a leader known only as the {{veiled-queen}}. It meant to depose {{ayrenn}}, break the alliance and drive everyone who was not {{altmer|Altmer}} out of the isles.

In 582 {{ayrenn}} made a royal progress through {{auridon}}, beginning at {{vulkhel-guard}}, the port at the island's southern tip. The town's watch captain and most of the watch had joined the {{veiled-heritance|Heritance}} and planned to kill her when she appeared in public. The {{eyes-of-the-queen}}, her personal guard and spies under {{razum-dar}}, foiled the plot with the help of an adventurer, whom the queen then made one of the {{eyes-of-the-queen|Eyes}}. At {{tanzelwil}}, the mausoleum of the island's old lords, one of the queen's own advisors had corrupted the spirits of the dead for the society. The corruption was purged, and the queen and her new {{eyes-of-the-queen|Eye}} killed the advisor.

{{ayrenn}} wished to make peace with {{estre}} of {{skywatch}}, {{naemon}}'s wife, and planned a ceremony there to raise her rank. Meanwhile the adventurer got into the society's stronghold on {{errinorne-isle}}, killed its ruling council and learned who the {{veiled-queen}} was. The {{eyes-of-the-queen|Eyes}} interrupted the rehearsal of the ceremony and named {{estre}} before the queen. {{estre}} escaped by magic, and the society fought in the open across northern {{auridon}}. {{ayrenn}} declared her a traitor and ordered her death.

At a northern shrine of the {{eight-divines}}, {{estre}} made a bargain with {{mehrunes-dagon}}: her soul in exchange for an army of his {{daedra}}. She loosed them on the shrine and then on the town of {{dawnbreak}}, leaving it largely in ruins. At {{firsthold}} its ruler let her in, not knowing she was the {{veiled-queen}}. She opened four {{oblivion-gate|Oblivion Gates}} in the city, took him hostage and sealed the castle behind wards. {{ayrenn}}'s forces and the {{mages-guild}} broke the wards and closed three of the gates. The adventurer and {{razum-dar}} went through the last, in the castle, into the {{deadlands}}, {{mehrunes-dagon|Dagon}}'s realm, and killed {{estre}} there. The ruler was rescued, but most of {{firsthold}} lay in ruins.

## Notes

- **Outside help.** {{estre}} had also promised a sealord of the {{maormer}} to help his fleet take {{auridon}}, in return for governing the island under him. The society's own letters show she meant the attack only as a distraction. A {{maormer}} landing near {{vulkhel-guard}} was beaten back.
- **The prince.** {{naemon}} travelled with the queen during the progress. How much he knew of his wife's plans is not recorded.
- **Her soul.** Before her death the necromancer {{mannimarco}} had pressed {{estre}} into submitting to {{molag-bal}}. Her soul went to {{coldharbour}}, and so did those of many of her followers.
- **What remained.** The survivors of the society carried the fight on into {{valenwood}}.
