{{dagoth-ur}} had not died at {{red-mountain}}. Still bound to the {{heart-of-lorkhan}}, he woke in 882 of the {{era-second}} along with his kin. When the {{tribunal}} came to the mountain to renew their power, he ambushed them and drove them away from the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}}. From then on he sent ash storms and a wasting disease out of the mountain, drew followers to him through their dreams, and began building a giant mechanical god with which to drive the Empire out of {{morrowind}}. The weakened {{tribunal}} could only hold him inside a magical barrier around {{red-mountain}}.

In 427 of the {{era-third}}, on the orders of Emperor {{uriel-septim-vii}}, an unknown prisoner was released and shipped to {{vvardenfell}}. The prisoner survived {{dagoth-ur}}'s divine disease and passed the trials named in the prophecies of the {{ashlanders}}. Three of the Great Houses named the stranger their war leader, and four {{ashlanders|Ashlander}} tribes accepted the stranger as the {{nerevarine}}, the reborn {{nerevar}} whom {{azura}} had promised.

{{vivec}} knew that if the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}} were set free, the {{tribunal}} would lose its divinity. He gave the hero one of {{kagrenacs-tools}}, a gauntlet that made it safe to handle the other two, and explained how to attack. The hero took the remaining tools from {{dagoth-ur}}'s strongholds and went down into the mountain.

At its heart stood the half-built giant, with the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}} set inside it. The hero struck down {{dagoth-ur}}, but he rose again in the giant's chamber and could not be harmed. The hero turned the tools on the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}} and broke the enchantments that bound it. {{dagoth-ur}} became mortal again and died, and the giant was destroyed. {{azura}} appeared and declared the disease at an end.

With the enchantments broken, the {{tribunal}} could no longer draw on the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}}.

## Notes

- **Another path.** In another telling the hero kills {{vivec}} and takes the gauntlet, which then has to be remade by the last living {{dwemer|Dwemer}}.
- **The Emperor's part.** {{uriel-septim-vii}} acted on a prophecy; the {{blades}} on {{vvardenfell}} guided the prisoner in the early stages.
- **Names.** The {{ashlanders}} called {{dagoth-ur}} the {{sharmat}}.
- **Afterward.** Within a few years the {{tribunal}} began to fall apart. {{almalexia}} and {{sotha-sil}} died, and {{vivec}} later disappeared.
