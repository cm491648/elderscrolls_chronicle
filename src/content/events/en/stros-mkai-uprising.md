Three months after the battle in {{hunding-bay}}, a {{redguards|Redguard}} mercenary named {{cyrus}} landed on {{stros-mkai}}. A letter from his old captain had told him that his sister {{iszara}} had gone missing there. She had been the consort of the dead prince {{a-tor}} and his go-between with the {{restless-league}}, a band of {{redguards|Redguard}} pirates who fought the Empire. When {{cyrus}} pressed the governor, {{amiel-richton}}, for answers, he was thrown into the catacombs beneath the palace. He escaped, found the {{restless-league|League}} in hiding, and agreed to help them in return for news of his sister.

The {{restless-league|League}} had held the gem with the prince's soul and meant to bring him back, but had gone to ground under the Imperial crackdown. {{iszara}} would not wait. She stole the gem and took it to {{n-gasta}}, a {{sload}} necromancer whom the governor let work in the open, and offered him her own soul if he would restore the prince. {{n-gasta}} took her soul and kept the gem. He had it shaped like a dragon and sent to {{amiel-richton|Richton}}, who gave it to {{nahfahlaar|Nafaalilargus}} to guard. {{cyrus}} killed {{n-gasta}} and brought his sister's soul back from the {{daedra|Daedric}} realm where it had been taken. He also found the ring the prince's archmage had used to keep the body unchanged.

In the palace vaults he killed {{nahfahlaar|Nafaalilargus}} and took the gem. With the gem, the ring and the help of an old {{yokuda|Yokudan}} witch, the {{restless-league|League}} slipped into the temple where the body lay and tried to raise the prince. The spell went wrong. {{a-tor}}'s soul passed into his sword instead. The rebels lost heart, and {{cyrus}} took up the sword and spoke to them: "The Prince is dead! Long live the Prince!"

Then the {{restless-league|League}}, led by its captain and {{iszara}}, killed the Imperial guards at the harbor and burned their ships. Most of the palace guard had been sent to raid the {{restless-league|League}}'s hideout, and {{cyrus}} stormed the empty palace. He caught {{amiel-richton|Richton}} on a {{dwemer}} airship and beat him in a sword fight. The emissary who had shot the prince at {{hunding-bay}} sprang from hiding, but the sword struck first and killed them both.

When news of the victory spread, {{volag}} came out of hiding and drove the Imperial governor out of {{sentinel}}, and the whole province rose. {{tiber-septim}} was forced to come to terms. He signed the treaty for the Empire, {{iszara}} for the {{crowns}} and {{volag}} for the {{forebears}}. Under its terms {{hammerfell}} stood level with every other province of the Empire. Before long the two parties were vying for power again, their old grudges intact.

## Notes

- **The treaty.** It is known as the {{first-treaty-of-stros-mkai}}, to tell it apart from the {{second-treaty-of-stros-mkai}} of the {{era-fourth}}.
- **Standing.** Nearly two hundred years later, *The Wolf Queen* records that the {{redguards}} saw themselves as "part of the Empire, but not a subject."
- **Afterward.** The rest of {{cyrus}}'s life survives only in legend. Later tales send him sailing west to uncharted lands, and a sea shanty of the {{era-third}} still sang his name.
- **The dragon.** {{nahfahlaar|Nafaalilargus}} was the name the red dragon {{nahfahlaar}} went by in the service of {{tiber-septim}}. Nearly three centuries earlier he had fought beside the {{dragonguard}} in {{elsweyr}}.
