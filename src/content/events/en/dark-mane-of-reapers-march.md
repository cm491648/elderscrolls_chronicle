{{reapers-march}}, shared by {{bosmer}} and {{khajiit}}, joins the woods of {{valenwood}} to the plains of {{elsweyr}}. In 582 of the {{era-second}}, when it belonged to the {{aldmeri-dominion}}, a {{colovia|Colovian}} legion under General {{lavinia}} occupied {{arenthia}}, as the first step toward annexing the {{reapers-march|March}}.

Meanwhile {{akkhuz-ri}}, the {{mane}} of the {{khajiit}}, was travelling to {{valenwood}} for the handfasting of the new {{silvenar}}. On the road he turned on his own followers and fled into the {{reapers-march|March}}. {{dro-mathra}}, the old enemies of the {{khajiit}}, broke out of a forgotten temple beneath {{fort-grimwatch}} and joined a {{colovia|Colovian}} siege of the fort. The {{mane}}'s two {{lunar-champion|Lunar Champions}}, the twin sisters {{khali}} and {{shazah}}, drove them back with an adventurer at their side. The spirit of {{rid-thar-ri-datta}}, the first {{mane}} of {{elsweyr}}, appeared to them, declared that {{akkhuz-ri}} could no longer serve, and named the adventurer the {{moon-hallowed}}.

In {{arenthia}} the {{moon-hallowed}} helped the city's resistance defeat {{lavinia}} and end the occupation. Behind it stood {{javad-tharn}}, an {{imperials|Imperial}} mage and the son of {{euraxia-tharn}} of {{rimmen}}. His {{stonefire-cult}}, servants of {{molag-bal}}, had secretly built a {{dark-anchors|Dark Anchor}} in the temple district; it was destroyed. He had also woken the {{dark-mane}}, an ancient {{mane}} fallen to darkness and now leader of the {{dro-mathra}}, and set him upon {{akkhuz-ri}}. The {{mane}} stood for the soul of the {{khajiit}}; once it was stained, he hoped, the whole people would fall into darkness. The spirit was driven out of {{akkhuz-ri}}, who gave up his office. Both sisters passed the first of the trials to succeed him.

The {{dark-mane}} seized the temple of {{moonmont}} and corrupted it. There the {{moon-hallowed}} defeated him, but one sister had to contain him within herself, sealed beneath the temple; the {{moon-hallowed}} chose which. {{javad-tharn|Javad}} and the cult then loosed {{daedra}} on {{dune}}. The remaining champion and the {{moon-hallowed}} closed the portals, destroyed a second anchor and won back the city for the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}}.

Before the leaders of the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}}, the champion and the {{moon-hallowed}} walked the final path from the temple at the top of {{dune}}. In the {{plane-of-jode}} they were shown a future in which the {{aldmeri-dominion|Dominion}} fell for want of a {{mane}}. At the end, in the {{den-of-lorkhaj}}, they faced {{javad-tharn|Javad}} and the {{dark-mane}}, now possessing the captive sister. {{javad-tharn|Javad}} was killed and the {{dark-mane}} beaten, and the sister was freed to go on to {{aetherius}}. In {{dune}}, {{rid-thar-ri-datta}} gave his blessing to the new {{mane}}.

## Notes

- **Background.** The {{dark-anchors|anchors}} in the {{reapers-march|March}} were part of {{molag-bal}}'s attempt that year to pull {{nirn}} into his realm.
- **Two champions.** Never before had two {{lunar-champion|Lunar Champions}} sought the office of {{mane}} at the same time.
- **The relic.** {{javad-tharn|Javad}} and the cult also tried to seize a claw-shaped relic of an earlier {{mane}} from a monastery in the {{reapers-march|March}}, and were driven off.
- **Succession.** Which sister became {{mane}} depended on the {{moon-hallowed}}'s choice and is not recorded.
