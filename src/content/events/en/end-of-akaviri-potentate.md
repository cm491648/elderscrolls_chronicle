{{versidue-shaie}} ruled the empire as {{potentate}} for more than three hundred years. In {{era-second|2E}} 324 he was murdered in his palace at {{senchal}}, and the name of the {{morag-tong}} was written on the wall in his own blood. Every ruler in the land made the destruction of the {{morag-tong|Tong}} their first concern. It was hunted down in every province and held on only in its homeland, {{morrowind}}. Out of what was left of it grew a new brotherhood of killers, the {{dark-brotherhood}}.

His son {{savirien-chorak}} took his place. He lifted his father's ban on standing armies, but he was a poor administrator and handled crises badly. In 2E 400 he held months of celebration to mark four hundred years of rule by the {{potentate|Potentates}}. Late in his reign he recognized {{orsinium}} as a province.

In 2E 430 assassins killed {{savirien-chorak|Savirien-Chorak}}, and his heirs were hunted down as well. Within two weeks the {{akaviri-potentate}} had collapsed. Civil wars and uprisings broke the empire apart. Its provinces went their own ways, and what remained of the {{second-empire}} shrank to {{cyrodiil}}, ruled by one warlord after another. The years that followed were called the {{interregnum}}.

## Notes

- **The killers.** Most accounts name the {{dark-brotherhood}}; one names the {{morag-tong}}.
- **The year.** Some sources give 2E 431.
- **One heir.** One of the heirs escaped south into {{elsweyr}}, but a {{morag-tong}} assassin found and killed him there.
- **After.** Roads and cities fell into ruin, and the {{amulet-of-kings}} was lost.
- **The blood on the wall.** Some {{dunmer}} scholars doubt parts of the story of 2E 324.
