Early in the {{era-first}}, the heart of {{cyrodiil}} was ruled by the {{ayleid}}, elves who governed from the {{white-gold-tower}} and kept the native humans as slaves. As their rule grew crueler, slaves were tortured for sport and sacrificed to the {{daedra}}.

A slave woman named {{alessia}} prayed to {{akatosh}} and the other {{aedra}} for deliverance. At {{sancre-tor}}, {{akatosh}} answered her with three visions. Once free, she rose in open revolt and, with a demigod companion beside her, raised an army. In 242 a wild warrior named {{pelinal-whitestrake}} came into her camp, still bloody from killing elves, and became her champion.

Allies gathered to her. Some {{ayleid}} lords had turned against their own kind. {{nords|Nord}} and {{bretons|Breton}} warbands fought for promises of land and trade, and the {{nords|Nord}} kingdom of {{skyrim}}, which had long tried to break the elves' power, joined the war. The rebels took {{cyrodiil}} east of the great river and closed on the capital.

In 243 the allied army surrounded the {{white-gold-tower}} but hesitated to attack. {{pelinal-whitestrake|Pelinal}} went in alone and struck down the sorcerer-king who held it, and the sorcerer-king was cast out into {{oblivion}}. {{pelinal-whitestrake|Pelinal}} fell in the same fight, and the elves cut his body into eight pieces. The army followed, took the tower, and the {{ayleid}} empire collapsed.

{{alessia}} declared herself the first empress of {{cyrodiil}} and made {{sancre-tor}} her holy city. To give her people and her {{nords|Nord}} allies one faith, she founded the worship of the {{eight-divines}}, joining elven and {{nords|Nord}} gods. {{akatosh}} made a covenant with her and gave her the {{amulet-of-kings}}: as long as rulers of {{dragonborn}} blood sat on the throne and kept the {{dragonfires}} lit, the gods would hold back the powers of {{oblivion}}.

## Notes

- **The vision.** Another legend places {{alessia}}'s vision in an {{ayleid}} shrine beneath the {{white-gold-tower}}, which her followers later tore down.
- **The covenant.** The chronicle of the {{era-first}} dates the covenant to {{alessia}}'s death in 266, when {{akatosh}} made her the first saint and her soul became part of the {{amulet-of-kings|Amulet}}.
- **The assault.** Some accounts give the demigod companion command of the army that followed {{pelinal-whitestrake|Pelinal}} into the tower.
- **Afterward.** Several {{ayleid}} cities held out for years. A century later the {{alessian-order}} began driving out and killing the elves who remained in the Empire.
