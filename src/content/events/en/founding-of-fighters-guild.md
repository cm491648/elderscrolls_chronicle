In {{era-second|2E}} 283 the vassal kingdoms of the empire were in open revolt. They refused to pay taxes and attacked the imperial garrisons. The {{potentate}} {{versidue-shaie}} declared martial law across the land: every kingdom would disband its army or be conquered. The {{war-of-subjugation}} that followed lasted thirty-seven years. By 2E 320 the last of the kingdoms' armies had been crushed, and only one army was left in {{tamriel}}, his own.

The victory left the empire and its kingdoms poor. Both had spent the last of their gold on the war. Farms and trades were ruined, and there were no taxes to collect. Local guards and militias were forbidden, crime spread through the conquered lands, and the {{potentate}}'s army could not keep order everywhere.

His kinsman {{dinieras-ves}}, called the Iron, proposed an answer: warriors for hire, whom nobles could engage for a time, with part of every fee going to the {{potentate}}'s government. Order would be kept without the cost of a standing army. At first the company was called the {{syffim}}, a word meaning soldiers, and took only {{akaviri}}. The land was too wide for so few, and soon it took in men and women of every race. Young people joined in great numbers, and crime began to fall.

In 2E 321 the {{potentate}} signed the {{guild-act}}. It chartered the company under the name people already called it by, the {{fighters-guild}}, and recognized the {{mages-guild}} and the guilds of many trades besides. The guilds paid levies to the empire, and the empty treasury began to fill again.

## Notes

- **Armies again.** In 2E 324 the next {{potentate}} lifted the ban on standing armies. By then the {{fighters-guild}} was too useful to lose, and it remained.
- **The trades.** Among the guilds the act recognized were the tinkers, cobblers, scribes, architects, brewers, healers, tailors and minstrels.
- **The date.** *History of the Fighters Guild* gives 2E 320, the year before the act, as the year the guild was founded.
