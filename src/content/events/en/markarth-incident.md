Since 174 of the {{era-fourth}} the {{reachmen}}, led by {{madanach}}, had held {{markarth}} and the rest of {{the-reach}} as a kingdom of their own. The deposed Jarl, {{hrolfdir}}, and his son wanted the city back, but the {{great-war}} had drawn the Legion away. They turned instead to {{ulfric-stormcloak}}, son of the Jarl of {{windhelm}}, who had come home from the war and gathered a militia of {{nords}}.

{{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}}'s price was {{talos}}. Once the city was taken, his men must be free to worship him in {{markarth}}, though the {{white-gold-concordat}} forbade it. The Jarl agreed, hoping the {{thalmor}} would never hear of it. In 176, after a short and bloody siege, the militia broke the gates and drove the {{reachmen}} out with heavy losses. {{madanach}} was captured and condemned to death, but was locked away instead in the silver mine beneath the city, which served as its prison. Those who escaped fled into the hills and fought on as the {{forsworn}}.

When the Legion came back to restore order, {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} would not let it into the city unless it too allowed the worship of {{talos}}, and it agreed. But the {{thalmor}} learned of it. Under the treaty they demanded that {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} and his men be arrested, or there would be war again. {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} and much of his militia were thrown into prison, and the Emperor had to let the {{thalmor}} hunt down worshippers of {{talos}} throughout {{skyrim}}.

{{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} took the arrest as a betrayal by the Empire he had fought for. He stayed in prison for years, and his militia became the {{stormcloaks}}.

## Notes

- **The Voice.** Some accounts have {{ulfric-stormcloak|Ulfric}} shouting the defenders off the walls with the {{thuum|Voice}}.
- **The reprisals.** An Imperial scholar's book, *The Bear of Markarth*, accuses the militia of executing everyone who had served the rebels and many who would not fight.
- **The arrest.** Accounts differ on who made the arrest: the Jarl, his son who succeeded him, or the Legion.
- **The name.** His followers named the affair the {{markarth}} Incident.
