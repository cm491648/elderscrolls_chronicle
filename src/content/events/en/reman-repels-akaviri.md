In 2703 of the {{era-first}}, a great army sailed from {{akavir}} and landed on the northern coast of {{tamriel}}. The {{akaviri}} marched through {{morrowind}} and {{skyrim}} and defeated every force sent against them.

Their landing in {{morrowind}} had angered {{vivec}}, one of its living gods. He struck at them from behind and cut them off from their supplies and from the sea. Running short of food, the {{akaviri}} halted in the mountains and made their headquarters at {{pale-pass}}, a pass between {{skyrim}} and {{cyrodiil}} that was easy to defend.

There they faced the army of {{reman}}. {{cyrodiil}} had long been divided between west and east; {{reman}} had united it against the invaders. His troops were just starting up into the hills when the whole {{akaviri}} host laid down its arms. They had heard {{reman}}'s voice and knew him for {{dragonborn}}. They knelt before him, said he was the one they had crossed the ocean to find, and swore to serve him.

{{reman}} took many of the veterans into his armies and his personal guard. They became the {{dragonguard}}, and their skill carried him through the campaigns that followed. From those campaigns he built the {{second-empire}}.

The {{dragonguard}} outlasted his dynasty. In later eras their order was known as the {{blades}}.

## Notes

- **The date.** Most sources date the invasion and the battle to {{era-first|1E}} 2703; some put the first {{akaviri}} landings as early as 2700, when raiders were also striking {{high-rock}}.
- **His birth.** The *Remanada* tells of a newborn found at {{sancre-tor}} with the {{amulet-of-kings}} set in his forehead and carried straight to the throne. Skeptics suspect the tale was made up to support his claim.
- **Allies.** {{vivec}} did not fight alone; he made an alliance with the kings of the sea creatures off the coast of {{morrowind}}.
