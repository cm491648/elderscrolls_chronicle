In {{era-second|2E}} 576 Emperor {{leovic}} made the worship of {{daedra}} lawful. A lord of western {{cyrodiil}}, {{varen-aquilarios}}, rose against him, and in 577 he killed {{leovic|Leovic}} in the palace and took the throne himself. To secure his rule he married {{leovic|Leovic}}'s widow, {{clivia-tharn}}. Yet he believed that only a {{dragonborn}} could rightfully rule, and he was not one.

The sorcerer {{mannimarco}} told him there was a way. With a slightly changed coronation rite, the {{amulet-of-kings}} could relight the {{dragonfires}} and move {{akatosh}} to grant {{varen-aquilarios|Varen}} the blood of a dragon. The amulet had been lost for over a century. {{varen-aquilarios|Varen}} set out to find it with four companions, among them {{mannimarco}} and the chancellor {{abnur-tharn}}, and they found it.

The five gathered in a temple in the {{imperial-city}} to perform the rite. While {{abnur-tharn|Tharn}} held the amulet, {{varen-aquilarios|Varen}} asked {{akatosh}} to make him {{dragonborn}}. Then {{mannimarco}} held them all where they stood and told them he served {{molag-bal}}. For weeks he had been writing glyphs into the rite, and now it worked against itself. Instead of renewing the ancient covenant that shielded the world from {{oblivion}}, it broke it.

A wave of magic burst outward from the city. {{varen-aquilarios|Varen}} vanished in it. Mages near the city died or went mad, the ground split open at {{chorrol}}, {{red-mountain}} shook and storms battered the southern coasts. {{daedra}} came into the world in greater numbers than ever before, and ghosts were seen even by ordinary people.

The {{elder-council}} blamed the {{mages-guild}} and drove it out of the {{imperial-city}}, giving its university to a fellowship that was in truth {{mannimarco}}'s {{worm-cult}}. {{clivia-tharn}} ruled as empress regent.

## Notes

- **The year.** Sources give both 2E 578 and 2E 579; one account has the search for the amulet take two years.
- **What followed.** {{molag-bal}} used the broken barrier to begin drawing the world into his own realm.
- **The name.** The {{elder-scroll|Elder Scrolls}} themselves gave the event its name, the {{soulburst}}.
- **The companions.** The other two companions were a half-giant warrior and the captain of the emperor's guard, who fled with the amulet and hid it.
- **The emperor.** {{varen-aquilarios|Varen}} survived, though he was thought dead; he woke with no memory in a distant abbey.
