{{solstheim}} was a cold island in the {{sea-of-ghosts}}. Late in the {{era-third}} an {{imperials|Imperial}} garrison held {{fort-frostmoth}} on its southern shore, and in 427 the {{east-empire-company}} found ebony there and set up the mining camp of {{raven-rock}}. The island's own people were the {{skaal}}, {{nords}} who kept to their village and their old ways.

The {{skaal}} kept a prophecy. Once in every era, they said, the {{daedra|Daedric}} Prince {{hircine}} came to {{nirn}} with his hounds to choose mortals as quarry for the {{great-hunt}}. Four signs would come first. {{werewolves}}, the Hunter's hounds, would appear on the island. Fire would rise from the frozen {{lake-fjalding}}. Slaughtered {{horkers}} would wash up all along the northern coast. Last would come the {{bloodmoon}}, when {{secunda}} turned red with the blood of the prey. When the hunt was done, {{hircine}} would go back to his own realm until the next era.

In 427 the signs came true one after another, while an outsider newly arrived on the island was there to see them. When {{secunda}} rose red, the hunt began, and among its quarry were the island's three champions, whom {{hircine}} had already taken one by one: the {{frost-giants|frost giant}} {{karstaag}}; {{falx-carius}}, captain of {{fort-frostmoth}}; and {{tharsten-heart-fang}}, chief of the {{skaal}}. The hunt was held inside the {{mortrag-glacier}} on the north-west coast, and the outsider was drawn into it as well. To reach the Hunter, the prey had to find a way through a maze full of {{werewolves}}.

The outsider came through the maze. To win free of the hunt, the outsider then had to fight one of {{hircine}}'s aspects, of strength, of speed or of guile. The outsider won. The glacier fell in on itself, the hunt was over, and {{hircine}} withdrew to his realm for another era.

## Notes

- **Hound or prey.** It is not known whether the outsider joined the hunt as one of {{hircine}}'s hounds or as his prey.
- **The hunt.** The {{skaal}} called it the {{great-hunt|Hunter's Game}}. Its form changed from era to era: it might be the hunt of a whole tribe, or of a single man. Some of the chosen were taken to {{hircine}}'s own hunting grounds rather than hunted on {{nirn}}.
- **Not only here.** The {{great-hunt}} and the {{bloodmoon}} were not bound to {{solstheim}}. The red moon could appear wherever {{hircine}} had called a hunt.
- **Afterward.** By 201 of the {{era-fourth}}, few {{werewolves}} were left on the island, only the remnant of a single pack.
