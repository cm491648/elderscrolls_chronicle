In 582 of the {{era-second}}, Queen {{ayrenn}} opened {{summerset-isles|Summerset}}, the main island, to outsiders of every race. Many {{altmer}} resented the decree, and some joined the {{court-of-bedlam}}, a cult serving three {{daedra|Daedric}} Princes, {{nocturnal}}, {{mephala}} and {{clavicus-vile}}, allied as the {{daedric-triad}}. The {{psijic-order}} sent the monk {{valsirenn}} to investigate a warning of a plot against the island. With an adventurer she uncovered {{daedra}} worship at a monastery, and the adventurer was invited to {{artaeum}}. The {{psijic-order|Psijics}}' island had left the world some three and a half centuries before, and could be reached only through their portals.

The {{court-of-bedlam|Court}} worked with {{k-tora}}, a {{sload}} sorcerer whose magic was opening rifts meant to sink the main island into the sea. Then {{k-tora}} broke into {{artaeum}}, seized the {{psijic-order|Order}}'s Ritemaster, {{iachesis}}, and tried to tear his oldest memories from his mind. {{valsirenn}} and the adventurer took a staff from the {{psijic-order|Order}}'s vaults, broke through the {{sload|Sload}}'s mental barrier and killed him.

Next the {{court-of-bedlam|Court}} struck at the college of the {{sapiarchs}}, who kept the two diamonds that served as the key to the {{crystal-tower}}. Its leader was {{leythen}}, once a {{psijic-order|Psijic}} and now a servant of {{mephala}}, and {{valsirenn}}'s long-lost husband. The tower stood in every realm at once, anchored by its foundation stone, the {{transparent-law}}. As {{iachesis}} regained his memories, the reason came out. Long ago {{k-tora}}'s magic had driven him to steal the heart of the {{transparent-law|Stone}} for the {{daedric-triad|Triad}}. He had hidden it with a spell learned from {{nocturnal}}, and then forgotten where.

{{valsirenn}}, the adventurer and {{darien-gautier}}, a knight sworn to {{meridia}}, followed the trail with {{iachesis}} into {{nocturnal}}'s realm, the {{evergloam}}. The heart, they found, lay inside {{iachesis}} himself. {{mephala}} and {{clavicus-vile}} appeared to claim it, but {{nocturnal}} turned on them, cast them out of her realm and tore the heart from {{iachesis}}, killing him. With it she took the {{crystal-tower}}, and the {{sapiarchs}} inside became shadows. She meant to reach through the tower into the realms of the other Princes, absorb their power and then unravel reality itself.

The {{psijic-order|Psijics}} reluctantly bargained with the two betrayed Princes, and {{clavicus-vile}} opened a way in through the tower's counterpart in his realm. The adventurer, {{darien-gautier|Darien}} and {{leythen}} went in. When {{nocturnal}} tried to swallow them in shadow, {{darien-gautier|Darien}} proved to be a living light of {{meridia}}. He drove back the dark and gave his life to restore his sword, {{dawnbreaker}}. With it the adventurer mended the {{transparent-law}}; {{nocturnal}} was banished and the heart returned to the tower. Only the adventurer came back alive. {{valsirenn}} took {{iachesis}}'s place as acting Ritemaster, as he had asked with his last words.

## Notes

- **The old bargain.** {{clavicus-vile}} had promised {{k-tora}} the main island of {{summerset-isles|Summerset}} itself in return for the heart. The {{court-of-bedlam|Court}}'s rifts were meant to pay that price.
- **The hiding spell.** {{iachesis}} bargained with {{nocturnal}} for the spell to keep the heart from {{k-tora}}. In giving it, {{nocturnal}} secretly betrayed her two allies, keeping the heart hidden from them until she could use it herself.
- **Before this.** The {{daedric-triad|Triad}} had earlier tried to seize the hidden city of {{sotha-sil}}. From it {{nocturnal}} stole the method of draining the life of {{nirn}} that she meant to use in the tower.
- **Afterward.** {{mephala}} and {{clavicus-vile}} then accepted the pact {{sotha-sil}} had made with other Princes, and the {{daedric-triad|Triad}} was dissolved. The {{sapiarchs}} agreed to seek allies to guard the tower.
- **Artaeum.** {{artaeum}} returned to the world around 730 of the {{era-second}}, without {{iachesis}}, and the {{psijic-order|Psijics}} never explained where it had been.
