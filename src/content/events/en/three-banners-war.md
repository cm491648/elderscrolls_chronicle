In 430 of the {{era-second}}, the last {{akaviri}} ruler of {{cyrodiil}} was murdered with all his heirs, and the {{second-empire}} came to an end. In the long {{interregnum}} that followed, one weak or doubtful ruler after another held the throne while the provinces went their own way.

Out of that disorder grew three alliances. The {{daggerfall-covenant}} joined the {{bretons}}, {{redguards}} and {{orcs}} in 566. In 572 invaders from {{akavir}} landed in the northeast; the {{nords}}, the {{dunmer}} and some tribes of {{argonians}} drove them off together and then formed the {{ebonheart-pact}}. In 580 the {{altmer}} of the {{summerset-isles}} joined with {{valenwood}} and {{elsweyr}} to form the {{aldmeri-dominion}}.

By then the throne in the {{imperial-city}} was held by a regent who answered to the necromancer {{mannimarco}}. Each alliance meant to take the city, overthrow that government and crown its own emperor. In 580 their armies entered {{cyrodiil}} from the northwest, the northeast and the south. The war became a long round of sieges. Keeps and forts changed hands again and again, and each side also raided the others' homelands. Each alliance took the {{imperial-city}} at some point and crowned an emperor from its own ranks, and each lost it again.

During the fighting, great chains came down from the sky. {{molag-bal}} was trying to drag {{nirn}} into his realm in {{oblivion}}, and his forces seized the {{imperial-city}}. The three leaders met in 582 but could not agree on a joint attack, and the Mages Guild and the Fighters Guild led the assault on his realm instead. When the danger had passed, the alliances went back to war.

By the ninth century of the {{era-second}}, none of the three alliances remained, and neither did the empire they had fought over.

## Notes

- **Another view.** *From Argonian to Saxhleel*, written by an {{argonians|Argonian}} commander who had escaped slavery among the {{dunmer}}, tells how she led her people to help drive off the {{akaviri}}. In the new alliance, she writes, the {{argonians}} found allies rather than masters; only the tribes of three regions of the marsh joined.
- **The date.** Some historians date the end of the {{second-empire}} to 431.
- **The outcome.** Peace talks were attempted more than once, but how the war ended is not recorded.
- **Names.** The conflict is also called the {{three-banners-war|Alliance War}}; Imperials called it the {{three-banners-war|Usurper War}}.
