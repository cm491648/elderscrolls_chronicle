In 431 of the {{era-third}}, {{hannibal-traven}} won election as {{arch-mage}} of the {{mages-guild}} by a narrow margin. Necromancy had been forbidden in the guild since its founding, but several senior mages had let the rule slide, and some were said to practice the art in secret. {{hannibal-traven|Traven}} enforced the ban. Half the {{council-of-mages}} resigned, and mages who would not give up necromancy were driven out.

In 433 necromancers began to attack the guild in the open, and its hall in {{bruma}} was burned to the ground. Behind them stood {{mannimarco}}, the guild's oldest enemy, who had returned to mortal affairs. Many of the mages pushed out by the ban had gone over to him and joined his {{worm-cult}}.

The {{council-of-mages|council}} could not agree on an answer. Two of its own members each carried off one of {{mannimarco}}'s old relics from the {{arcane-university}}, an amulet and a helm, meaning to study them for a weapon against him. One was killed by the {{worm-cult|cult}} and its {{daedra}} allies. The other proved to be secretly working for {{mannimarco}}. A recent recruit to the guild recovered both relics before the {{worm-cult|cult}} could take them back, and later helped capture a giant {{black-soul-gem}} that the necromancers had made.

By then the guild understood what {{mannimarco}} wanted: to destroy it and take the soul of its {{arch-mage}}, as he had sought the souls of {{arch-mage|Arch-Mages}} before to learn the secret of their power. His magic could enthrall anyone who stood against him. {{hannibal-traven|Traven}} gave his own life to close that door. He bound his soul into the great gem, so that whoever carried it would be shielded, and before he died he named the recruit as his successor.

The new {{arch-mage}} took the gem to {{echo-cave}}, where {{mannimarco}} waited. His enthralling magic found no hold, and there he was slain.

## Notes

- **The council.** By the end of the {{era-third}}, only three mages besides {{hannibal-traven|Traven}} still sat on the {{council-of-mages|council}}. The two who took the relics were among them.
- **The god.** {{mannimarco}} had risen to godhood during the {{warp-in-the-west}}, and his followers took a new moon in the sky for his divine body. The death in {{echo-cave}} was the last known death of his mortal body. The moon still rose afterward, and necromancers went on offering souls to it to darken soul gems well into the {{era-fourth}}.
