In {{era-first|1E}} 2200 a sickness came ashore on the western coasts of {{tamriel}}. It began on the shores nearest the open sea and spread inland as far as {{elsweyr}}. The sick broke out in boils, their bones grew brittle and their eyes and ears ran, and a thirst came over them that nothing could quench. They drank until their bones cracked under their own weight. More than half the people of {{tamriel}} died.

Those who could flee did. The nobles of the {{iliac-bay}} took refuge on the island of {{balfiera}}. In the {{summerset-isles}}, the ruler of one city flooded it to keep the plague from spreading, and most of its people drowned.

Healers searched for a cure and died searching. What they learned was that the disease was no natural thing. It had come from the {{sload}}, the slug-folk of the coral kingdom of {{thras}}, who had sent diseased sea beasts onto the coasts and then raided the stricken towns for slaves.

In 1E 2241 the emperor in {{cyrodiil}} agreed to pay for a fleet proposed by {{bendu-olo}}, king of {{anvil}}. Ships came from almost every people of {{tamriel}}, old enemies sailing side by side. This was the {{all-flags-navy}}. Open war with the {{sload}} began in 1E 2258, and in 1E 2260 the fleet reached {{thras}}. Its sailors killed every {{sload}} they could find and sank the coral kingdom into the sea. The whirlpool of the sinking island dragged down about half the fleet.

The surviving captains met in the {{systres}} and signed a pact binding themselves and their peoples to peace and cooperation, then sailed home.

## Notes

- **Dates.** Some sources put the outbreak as late as 1E 2260.
- **The lost ships.** The ships swallowed by the whirlpool were later said to have been drawn into {{coldharbour}}, the realm of {{molag-bal}}.
- **The wizard.** The {{altmer}} wizard {{syrabane}} is credited with saving many from the plague and with helping the fleet against the sea beasts.
- **After.** {{thras}} rose from the sea again in later ages, but the {{sload}} never made such an attack again. The plague also broke the {{khajiit}} lands into two rival kingdoms.
