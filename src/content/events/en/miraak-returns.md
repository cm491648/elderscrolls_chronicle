{{miraak}}, a dragon priest of the {{era-merethic}} and the first mortal born with a dragon's soul, learned from {{hermaeus-mora}}, the {{daedra|Daedric}} Prince of knowledge, how to bend dragons to his will, and turned on his masters. When the dragons struck back, {{hermaeus-mora|Mora}} took him away into {{apocrypha}}, his realm of endless books, where he served for thousands of years and waited for a chance to free himself.

In 201 of the {{era-fourth}} cultists attacked the {{last-dragonborn}}, calling them a false {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} and proclaiming the return of the true one. The {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} crossed to {{solstheim}}. At the island's heart, in the ruins of {{miraak}}'s temple, people in a trance were building a shrine around one of the {{all-maker-stones}}. A {{skaal}} woman, {{frea}}, said a curse had fallen on her people.

Inside the temple the {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} read one of {{hermaeus-mora}}'s {{black-books}} and was drawn into {{apocrypha}}, where {{miraak}} appeared and promised his return. In the {{skaal}} village, {{frea}}'s father {{storn}}, the shaman, sent the {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} to an ancient wall to learn the Shout that bends the will. With it the {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} freed the villagers and began to cleanse the stones.

The last word of that Shout could only come from {{hermaeus-mora|Mora}}, and his price was the secrets of the {{skaal}}. {{storn|Storn}} agreed to give them up; the Prince took his secrets and his life, and gave the {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} the final word.

The {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} returned to {{apocrypha}}, bent the dragon {{sahrotaar}} to their will and rode him to the summit where {{miraak}} waited. {{miraak}} killed his own dragons to take their souls, but he lost. As he tried to flee, {{hermaeus-mora}} lifted him into the air and impaled him, and the {{dragonborn|Dragonborn}} took his soul.

## Notes

- **The legend.** The {{skaal}} of {{solstheim}} remember {{miraak}} as the Traitor. Their tales say that a duel between him and another dragon priest tore the island away from the mainland.
- **The stones.** The {{skaal}} call the island's standing stones the {{all-maker-stones}}.
