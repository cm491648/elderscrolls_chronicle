For thousands of years the {{dunmer}} of {{morrowind}} had taken slaves from {{black-marsh}}. Before the {{oblivion-crisis}}, the {{hist}} called many {{argonians}} home to the marsh.

{{morrowind}} meanwhile suffered one blow after another, and in 5 of the {{era-fourth}} the eruption of {{red-mountain}} laid it waste. In the months that followed, the {{argonians}} invaded.

{{mournhold}}, the capital, was sacked. By the middle of 6 the invaders had pushed deep into the lands of {{house-telvanni}} in the north-east, and the House came close to destruction. {{house-redoran}}, which had raised a standing army again, kept them from taking the whole province.

The {{argonians}} held on to the south, and some of their tribes settled there. Many {{dunmer}} fled west into {{skyrim}} by land and by sea.

When the fighting was over, the {{dunmer}} no longer thought of {{morrowind}} as an Imperial province but as a nation of their own. {{house-redoran}} was now the strongest of the Great Houses. It moved the capital to {{blacklight}}, in the north, and drove {{house-hlaalu}} out of the ruling council.

## Notes

- **The cause.** Why the {{argonians}} invaded is not known. A councillor of {{house-redoran}} saw it as a chance seized against a weakened enemy; an {{altmer}} exile who wrote against the {{thalmor}} claimed that they had stirred the {{argonians}} up.
- **The raids.** Raids along the border went on long after the invasion. In 150 a small band of {{argonians}} landed on {{solstheim}} and was driven off.
- **The name.** The war is also called the {{argonians|Argonian}} Invasion.
