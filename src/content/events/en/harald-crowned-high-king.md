After {{ysgramor}}, his descendants held the high kingship of {{skyrim}}. The {{nords}} still kept lands in {{atmora}}, and warriors from the old homeland served in {{skyrim}} as mercenaries.

{{harald}}, the thirteenth of {{ysgramor}}'s line, was born in 113 of the {{era-first}}. Before he took the throne, he led the war against the last {{falmer}} in {{skyrim}}. In 139 his forces drove the elves back across a great lake in the south. Late that year his army found a hidden stronghold of the {{dragon-cult}} in the mountains and laid siege to it. The siege dragged on for days. Early in 140 the cultists killed themselves rather than surrender, and the fortress fell.

In 143 {{harald}} was crowned High King. He gave up the last {{nords|Nordic}} holdings in {{atmora}}, and the {{atmorans|Atmoran}} mercenaries left {{skyrim}} for home. He joined the scattered holds into a single kingdom of {{skyrim}} and made {{windhelm}} its capital. He ordered new weapons and armor for the first uniform army of the {{nords}}; its horned helmets became the fashion. Around 184 he called the chieftains of the realm to his hall in {{windhelm}} for the first council to advise the throne.

{{harald}} died in 221 at the age of 108, having outlived most of his children. His eldest surviving son reigned for only a year. The next son carried the kingdom's armies into {{high-rock}} and {{morrowind}} and made it the first {{nords|Nordic}} empire.

## Notes

- **The crown.** Some later writers credit {{harald}} with forging the {{jagged-crown}} from the bones and teeth of dragons; others believe the crown is older than he is.
- **The elves.** Many of the {{falmer}} who escaped {{harald}}'s war took refuge with the {{dwemer}}, who enslaved and blinded them.
- **Written history.** The *Anuad* closes the {{era-merethic}} with {{harald}}: the thirteenth of {{ysgramor}}'s line, and the first king to appear in written history.
