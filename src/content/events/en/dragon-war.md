In the {{era-merethic}}, the dragons ruled over men, first in {{atmora}} and then in {{skyrim}}. {{alduin}} had brought the wild dragons to order and stood at their head. The {{nords}} worshipped the dragons as gods and raised temples to them. The dragons cared little for the affairs of men. They chose priests from among them, gave them the Voice, and let them govern in their name. These priests were the {{dragon-cult}}.

In time the priests grew harsher and made slaves of the people. Men rose against them, and thousands died.

{{alduin}}'s brother {{paarthurnax}}, his chief lieutenant, came to pity men. At the call of the goddess {{kyne}}, he taught them the Voice, which until then only the dragons and their priests had used. Other dragons took the side of men, and the war turned. The priests were overthrown and many dragons were killed.

{{alduin}} remained. Three heroes, {{hakon-one-eye}}, {{gormlaith-golden-hilt}} and {{felldir-the-old}}, made a new Shout, {{dragonrend}}, which forced a dragon to know mortality and brought it down from the sky. Still they had no way to kill him. They met him on the summit of the {{throat-of-the-world}}. There {{felldir-the-old|Felldir}} used an {{elder-scroll}}, and {{alduin}} was cast out of their time and into the future.

{{paarthurnax}} stayed on the mountain, for he knew that {{alduin}} would return to the place he had left. The surviving dragons scattered to remote places. The cult lived on in hiding, raising mounds over the bones of dead dragons and waiting for {{alduin}} to come back.

## Notes

- **The refusal.** Before the last battle, {{hakon-one-eye|Hakon}} asked the dragon priest {{miraak}} for help, since only a {{dragonborn}} like him could kill a dragon for good. {{miraak}} refused; he had already turned from the dragons to serve {{hermaeus-mora}}.
- **Who called him.** The {{greybeards}} say it was {{kyne}} who called on {{paarthurnax}}. Other sources say it was {{akatosh}} himself.
- **Date.** No year is recorded. The war belongs to the later {{era-merethic}}, after the {{atmorans}} had settled in {{skyrim}}.
