{{winterhold}} stood on a headland at the north-eastern tip of {{skyrim}}, above the {{sea-of-ghosts}}. It had once been the capital of the province, and the {{college-of-winterhold}}, a school of magic, stood beside the town on the cliffs. After the eruption of {{red-mountain}} its Jarl took in {{dunmer}} refugees, especially those who could help the College, and many {{nords}} resented it.

In 122 of the {{era-fourth}} violent storms battered the coast for a year. At last the sea rose in monstrous waves that tore away whole districts, and most of {{winterhold}} slid into the sea: the harbour, the quarters along the shore and much of the headland itself. The Jarl's palace went with it, and so did his family. Of the town only an old hall, an inn and a few houses were left.

The {{college-of-winterhold|College}} was untouched. It now stood alone on a column of rock rising out of the water, reached from what was left of the town by a narrow stone bridge.

The Jarl's council blamed the mages, and rumours spread. The head of the {{college-of-winterhold|College}} wrote to the Jarl with his condolences, saying that the {{college-of-winterhold|College}} had been saved by protective magic laid long before and had nothing to do with the disaster. People drifted away all the same, and the {{college-of-winterhold|College}} lost its standing.

## Notes

- **The cause.** Nobody is certain what caused the collapse. Some at the {{college-of-winterhold|College}} hold that it was a distant consequence of the eruption of {{red-mountain}}; many in {{winterhold}} still blame the mages.
- **The storms.** Another account speaks only of monstrous waves that rose just before the collapse, with no year of storms.
- **Afterwards.** Eighty years later {{winterhold}} was almost a ghost town.
- **The source.** Most of what is known comes from the book *On the Great Collapse*.
