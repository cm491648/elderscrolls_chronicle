## Purpose and sources

This site gathers the major events of the Elder Scrolls world, from the Dawn Era to the Fourth Era: browse them along a timeline, see where they happened on the map, and read each story with its variant accounts and sources. The stories draw only on in-game material (books, dialogue and quest text) and the *Pocket Guide to the Empire*, never on website articles, strategy guides, comics or developer interviews. Where accounts disagree, the story tells one of them in full and the others go to its notes. Research leaned heavily on [UESP](https://en.uesp.net/), and every event's sources link to the UESP pages it used. UESP is used to locate and cross-check the original texts; lore claims rest only on the in-game sources and the *Pocket Guide to the Empire* listed above.

## How it is made

The text is written by Claude (Anthropic) and proofread by Codex (OpenAI), in Traditional Chinese and English. Chinese names primarily follow the fan translations by 大學漢化 (Daxue Hanhua), then established community usage, then the official Traditional Chinese localization, with a few choices made by the site owner. The Glossary records the names used throughout the site. The illustrations are AI-generated: Codex draws them from the site's visual canon, and a person reviews every one.

## Maps

Tamriel and its provinces are shown on the map bundled with *The Elder Scrolls Anthology* and on in-game maps, obtained through UESP. Those maps are © Bethesda Softworks / ZeniMax Media, and each map's caption credits its source. The maps of Nirn and the cosmos are the site's own drawings.
