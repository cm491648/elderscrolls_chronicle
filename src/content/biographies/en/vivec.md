{{vivec}} was a {{chimer}} of {{resdayn}}, both a warrior and a poet. With {{almalexia}} and {{sotha-sil}} he became one of the three counselors of {{nerevar}}.

He was there when the long peace with the {{dwemer}} ended at {{red-mountain}}. {{nerevar}} was carried out of the mountain gravely wounded, having seen {{kagrenac}} turn his tools on the {{heart-of-lorkhan}} and vanish. The three counselors advised keeping {{kagrenacs-tools}}. {{nerevar}} agreed once they had sworn by {{azura}} never to use them as the {{dwemer}} had meant to. When they went back for the tools, {{dagoth-ur}} would not give them up; they fought him, and he vanished. {{nerevar}} died soon after.

Some years later the three broke their oath, used the tools on the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}} and became living gods. {{azura}} cursed them, and the {{chimer}} became the {{dunmer}}. {{vivec}} took a body half gold like the {{chimer}} and half grey like the {{dunmer}}. Within the {{tribunal}} he was the warrior-poet, the most public of the three. He watched over {{vvardenfell}} and {{red-mountain}}, and he held the great rock {{baar-dau}} in the air above the city that bore his name, {{vivec-city}}.

When an army from {{akavir}} landed on the northern coast in {{era-first|1E}} 2703, he struck it from behind and cut it off from its supplies and the sea, and the hungry {{akaviri}} halted at {{pale-pass}}, where {{reman}} met them. Through the {{four-score-war}} against {{reman}}'s heirs he led the {{dunmer}}. In {{era-first|1E}} 2920 he agreed a treaty with Crown Prince {{juilek}}, saw the emperor {{reman-iii}} break it, and finally made peace with the {{potentate}} {{versidue-shaie}} two days after a {{morag-tong}} assassin killed the emperor. In {{era-second|2E}} 572, when invaders from {{kamal}} were pinned on a beach west of {{ebonheart}}, he raised a great wave from the sea and routed them. The beach was afterward called {{vivecs-antlers}}.

In {{era-second|2E}} 882 {{dagoth-ur}} returned. He ambushed the three at {{red-mountain}} and drove them from the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}}. Cut off from their power, they chose not to fight {{tiber-septim}}. Under the {{armistice}} they kept their rule in {{morrowind}}, and the Empire received {{numidium}}. The three could only hold {{dagoth-ur}} inside a magical barrier around the mountain. In {{era-third|3E}} 417, when {{almalexia}} and {{sotha-sil}} were overrun there, {{vivec}} brought them out alive.

In 427 a stranger arrived on {{vvardenfell}} and was hailed as the {{nerevarine}}, {{nerevar}} reborn. {{vivec}} knew that once the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}} was freed, the {{tribunal}} would lose its divinity. He gave the hero one of {{kagrenacs-tools}}, a gauntlet that made the other two safe to handle, and explained how to strike. The hero destroyed {{dagoth-ur}} and broke the enchantments on the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}}. Before the year was out {{almalexia}} and {{sotha-sil}} were dead, and {{vivec}} was the last of the three. At the end of the {{era-third}}, he vanished.

{{baar-dau}} still hung over his city. In {{era-fourth|4E}} 5 it fell, {{vivec-city}} was destroyed, and {{red-mountain}} erupted. The {{tribunal-temple|Temple}} that had worshipped him turned back to {{azura}}, {{boethiah}} and {{mephala}}, and kept {{vivec}} and his companions only as saints.

## Notes

- **The birth.** His sermons tell that he was born to a netchiman's wife before his people's war with the northern men. She carried him as an egg, first beneath the sea and then on the shore, while spirits and {{daedra}} taught it.
- **Whose account.** The story of {{red-mountain}} follows {{vivec}}'s own record, which says nothing about how {{nerevar}} died.
- **The murder.** {{ashlanders|Ashlander}} tradition holds that {{vivec}}, {{almalexia}} and {{sotha-sil}} poisoned {{nerevar}}. The {{tribunal-temple|Temple}} rejected the charge.
- **Before the mountain.** His sermons speak of the three as divine long before {{red-mountain}}, and say the change of color was {{boethiah}}'s wish, brought about by his grief.
- **His forerunner.** The {{dunmer}} saw {{vivec}} as the higher form of {{mephala}}, the {{daedra|Daedra}} who anticipated him.
- **Allies.** Against the {{akaviri}} in 2703 he allied with the kings of the sea creatures off the coast of {{morrowind}}.
- **The novel.** Much of the detail of 2920 comes from *2920, The Last Year of the First Era*, a historical novel.
- **Another path.** In another telling the {{nerevarine}} kills {{vivec}} and takes the gauntlet, which then has to be remade.
- **His fate.** What became of {{vivec}} is disputed: some say he died, others that he simply disappeared.
