{{dumac-dwarfking}} was king of the {{dwemer}} in the northeast of {{tamriel}} during the {{era-first}}. His people lived in great underground cities, built machines of metal, and put their faith in reason and logic. They scorned the {{daedra}} and laughed at the rituals of their {{chimer}} kin, who had no king and chose a Hortator only to lead them in war, and the two peoples had fought for generations. Beside {{dumac}} stood {{kagrenac}}, chief of his people's smiths and tonal architects.

When the {{nords}} overran the region, the {{chimer}} fell under their rule, but the {{dwemer}} held their underground cities and stayed free. After 369 the {{nords}} fell into civil war, and the {{chimer}}, newly united under their Hortator {{nerevar}}, sought the {{dwemer}} as allies. {{dumac}} agreed. In 416 the two armies beat the {{nords}} at {{red-mountain}} and drove them out, and the land, renamed {{resdayn}}, was governed through the {{first-council}}, with the king of the {{dwemer}} and the Hortator of the {{chimer}} at its head.

The pact split his own people. The {{rourken}} clan refused to follow King {{dumac}} into the {{first-council}}. Its chieftain threw the clan's great hammer, {{volendrung}}, westward, and the clan left to settle where it fell, in the land it named {{volenfell}}. The {{dwemer}} who stayed with their king now shared a country with the people they had fought for generations.

{{dumac}} kept that peace with gifts and diplomacy. As a sign of the alliance he gave {{sotha-sil}}, one of {{nerevar}}'s counselors, a wing of a {{dwemer|Dwemer}} city on {{vvardenfell}}. When {{nerevar}} married {{almalexia}}, he gave the couple twin swords, masterworks of {{dwemer|Dwemer}} craft whose edges burned with fire; {{nerevar}}'s was {{trueflame}}. In 461 he went with {{nerevar}} to {{cyrodiil}} as an envoy of {{resdayn}} for the coronation of Emperor Gorieus. Gorieus ruled the Alessian Empire, the first empire of Men, which then lived by the doctrines of the {{alessian-order}} and hated all elves; the Dwarf King and the Hortator attended all the same, and the day passed in peace. Out of this long partnership {{dumac}} and {{nerevar}} came to love and respect one another, and they became blood friends. By negotiation and compromise they kept their peoples at peace for centuries.

The break came through {{kagrenac}}. The {{chimer}} lord {{dagoth-ur}} told {{nerevar}} that {{kagrenac}} had found the {{heart-of-lorkhan}} beneath {{red-mountain}} and was building a new god with it. When {{nerevar}} came to ask {{dumac}} whether this was true, it was {{kagrenac}} who answered, angrily denying that {{nerevar}} had any right to judge the {{dwemer}}. {{nerevar}} came once more to talk. This time the two friends quarreled bitterly, and war followed.

The {{dwemer}} held a strong fortress at {{red-mountain}}, but most of {{dumac}}'s armies were drawn out into the field and pinned there while {{nerevar}} slipped into the chamber of the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}}. {{dumac}} was in the chamber. He and {{nerevar}} fought until both collapsed, gravely wounded, their magic spent. With his king down, and threatened by {{dagoth-ur}} and the others, {{kagrenac}} turned his tools on the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}} and vanished along with the {{dwemer}} around him.

What became of {{dumac}} himself is told differently by each people that remembered him. {{vivec}}'s record of the battle leaves him fallen in the chamber at the moment {{kagrenac}} and those around him vanished, and does not mention him again. The {{ashlanders}}' account, kept in the {{tribunal-temple|Temple}}'s hidden writings as *Nerevar at Red Mountain*, also has both kings fall gravely wounded; in it {{dagoth-ur}} kills {{kagrenac}}, and the {{dwemer}} on the battlefield crumble to dust. Asked about him, the {{dunmer}} of {{vvardenfell}} say that {{dumac}} died with {{nerevar}} at {{red-mountain}}. {{vivec}}'s sermons say {{nerevar}} killed him there. The {{nords|Nordic}} *Five Songs of King Wulfharth* say the Ash-King {{wulfharth}} killed him and doomed his people, while the secret song kept with them has him fighting at {{nerevar}}'s side and slain by {{dagoth-ur}}, after he had struck the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}} with his hammer. None of these tellings has him vanish alive with his people. Each that speaks of his fate says he died at {{red-mountain}}; they differ on whose hand killed him.

His kingdom emptied with his fall. At the moment {{kagrenac}} used his tools, the {{dwemer}} had vanished from every one of their cities, as others learned only later. {{dumac}}'s name survived only in the histories and songs of other peoples. His gift outlasted him: {{trueflame}} was shattered in the battle and its pieces lost, and in 427 of the {{era-third}} it was forged anew at {{almalexia}}'s bidding.

## Notes

- **Whose account.** The story above follows {{vivec}}'s record, *The Battle of Red Mountain*, the main version of the {{battle-of-red-mountain}} event. It does not record what {{dumac}} said in the final quarrel.
- **What the king knew.** The {{ashlanders|Ashlander}} account says {{kagrenac}} and the high priests had kept their new god secret from {{dumac}}, so he honestly denied any wrongdoing. When {{nerevar}} came back in arms, he refused to give up his people's old reverence for the {{heart-of-lorkhan|Heart}}, just as the {{chimer}} would not give up their {{daedra}}, and threatened war.
- **High priest.** The {{dunmer}} writings of later ages call {{kagrenac}} a high priest of the {{dwemer}}. The {{dwemer}} bowed to no {{daedra}}, yet among them he was something close to a teacher of faith: in their cities his precepts were taught, and the most learned of them studied his words, weighed their place in the life to come, and valued nothing above understanding the self.
- **Names.** The sermons and the {{ashlanders|Ashlander}} account call him {{dumac|Dumac Dwarf-Orc}}, and the {{ashlanders|Ashlander}} account also calls him King of {{red-mountain}}. {{vivec}}'s record calls him {{dumac|General Dumac}} and the Dwarf King, and the {{nords|Nordic}} songs name him {{dumac|Dumalacath}}.
- **Allies.** An Imperial history and a {{house-telvanni}} history both say the {{dwemer}} and {{dagoth-ur}}'s house took {{nords|Nord}} and {{orcs|Orc}} clans as allies against the other Houses. {{vivec}}'s sermons also have the northern men come to help {{kagrenac}}.
- **An Imperial history.** *The War of the First Council* says that after a short siege, treason let {{nerevar}} into the {{dwemer|Dwemer}} stronghold, where the leaders of the {{dwemer}} side were slain. It does not name {{dumac}}.
- **The exile.** The {{rourken}} clan's departure is dated to either 416 or 420.
- **Dates.** The alliance won its victory in {{era-first|1E}} 416. Most accounts place the final battle around 700, perhaps earlier.
